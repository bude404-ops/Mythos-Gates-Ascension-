# THE TWELVE — King and Queen Colossus Cards
Canon: v9 PRIMORDIAL 12-SET (kept Sept 5, 2026). Each colossus has its own art file (art/approved/*-EMERGENCE-CANON.png) and its own card below. The god that rises at the Gate Rite matches the pilgrim's aspect — a male pilgrim wakes the King, a female pilgrim wakes the Queen. Both aspects are canon per faction.

---

## VHARKAR — The Furnace King
**Faction:** Ashfall · **Realm:** The Cinder Weald · **Gate:** Cinder Gate
The first of the Twelve to be dreamed of again. Vharkar is the mountain that remembered it was a king — eon-layered basalt and shrine-crowned in obsidian monoliths, risen over the Cinder Gate like a father standing over a hearth no one else could keep lit. Pilgrim offerings fused into his strata tell of centuries of unanswered prayers.
*Rite line: When the lantern lights where he dreams, the Cinder Gate opens, and the Weald burns warm again.*

## VESSKRA — The Furnace Queen
**Faction:** Ashfall · **Realm:** The Cinder Weald · **Gate:** Cinder Gate
Vharkar's other aspect. Where the King is the hearth, the Queen is the kiln — the shaping fire, patient and total. Her magma pour and ember-flower gardens mark where the Weald's forges once stood, and her shrine-crowned brow still carries the braziers of the first offering-lights.
*Rite line: She wakes for the pilgrim who mends what fire has broken.*

## HAELDIR — The Storm King
**Faction:** Skyrend · **Realm:** The Storm-Crag · **Gate:** Squall Gate
A cliff that learned to thunder. Haeldir's strata buttresses carry wind-shrines and leaning watch-towers, and his storm-bell shrine-crown still rings when the Hollow climb the Crag. He is the wall the storm broke against for a thousand unanswered winters.
*Rite line: When he rises, the bells answer for themselves.*

## SYLWEN — The Storm Queen
**Faction:** Skyrend · **Realm:** The Storm-Crag · **Gate:** Squall Gate
The storm's mercy, in the aspect that wakes for female pilgrims. Sylwen's streaming squall-crown and slate strata read as the eye of the weather — the one still place in the storm. Sky-burial platforms grown on her shoulders held the Crag's dead with honor.
*Rite line: The storm is not the enemy. It is a queen that forgot who she guards.*

## THEVRIL — The Bloom King
**Faction:** Everbloom · **Realm:** The Bloom-Reach · **Gate:** Bloom Gate
A forest that decided to stand. Thevril's bark-strata body, heartwood ridge, and blossom-bough crown carry hanging gardens and overgrown shrines — a whole sacred grove grown into a king. His sap-gold light is the Reach's spring, long overdue.
*Rite line: What grows back after him is worth every winter.*

## MIRIATH — The Bloom Queen
**Faction:** Everbloom · **Realm:** The Bloom-Reach · **Gate:** Bloom Gate
The Reach's other answer. Miriath's flowering-vine cascade and petal-shed are the Reach's own blossom returned as a guardian — the aspect that wakes for those who plant rather than take. Her bark-cliff chest holds grove-shrines older than any road.
*Rite line: She rises where something was planted and kept.*

## MORVAIN — The Dusk King
**Faction:** Duskmourn · **Realm:** The Shadow-Fen · **Gate:** Dusk Gate
A barrow that refused to stay buried. Morvain's bone-pale chalk strata, shadow-ash plume, and barrow lantern-crown keep the Fen's dead lit against the Hollow — the aspect of dusk that protects rather than mourns. Candle-shrines on his shoulders never went out, even when the prayers stopped.
*Rite line: The lanterns on his crown are every grave-light he saved.*

## SENVRA — The Dusk Queen
**Faction:** Duskmourn · **Realm:** The Shadow-Fen · **Gate:** Dusk Gate
Dusk's keeper-aspect. Senvra's shadow-mist veil and ivory ridge read as the Fen's night itself standing guard — the quiet before the Hollow come, holding the Dusk Gate like a wick holds its flame. Her wisp-light is cold and it is kind.
*Rite line: She is the dark that does not swallow.*

## NEROVAX — The Tide King
**Faction:** Marenth · **Realm:** The Tide-Mire · **Gate:** Deep Gate
The sea's standing answer. Nerovax's sea-stack arms, lighthouse shoulder, and sunken sea-shrine crown are every harbor's lullaby made colossal — risen in the shallows over the half-sunken Deep Gate, kelp-draped, still keeping the tide's promise.
*Rite line: The tide always came back. So does he.*

## NERISSA — The Tide Queen
**Faction:** Marenth · **Realm:** The Tide-Mire · **Gate:** Deep Gate
The sea-foam aspect that wakes for female pilgrims. Nerissa's foam-and-kelp crest, coral standing-stone crown, and pearl-green tide-light are the Mire's memory of being beautiful before the Hollow came. Offering-floats still fused in her strata carry the harbor's old wishes.
*Rite line: She rises like the sea forgives — all at once.*

## GROTHMAR — The Stone King
**Faction:** Stoneheart · **Realm:** The Geode-Spire · **Gate:** Stone Gate
The mountain's oldest name. Grothmar's granite strata, crystal-spired crown-growth, and true trilithon shrine-crown — a ring of standing stones with lintels, the old henge grown across a god's brow — carry cairn-fields and mine-shrine ruins from the Spire's first century.
*Rite line: The henge was never a door. It was a crown waiting for its king.*

## BERGRUN — The Stone Queen
**Faction:** Stoneheart · **Realm:** The Geode-Spire · **Gate:** Stone Gate
The bedrock aspect. Bergrun's dust-mote plume, geode-lit seams, and henge-crown are the Spire's patience given a shape — the aspect that wakes for those who carry weight without complaint. Her watch-tower shoulder still holds the mountain's watch.
*Rite line: Everything built on her holds.*
