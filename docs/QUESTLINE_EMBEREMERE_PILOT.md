# QUESTLINE PILOT — EMBERMERE: "THE FORGE MUST BURN" (Ashfall Chapter One)

**Doctrine:** QUEST_DESIGN_DOCTRINE.md v1.0 (five-beat grammar; lore through quests, never text panels)
**Status:** PILOT — on BudE404's success verdict, the remaining five realms roll on this template.

**Chapter spine:** the pilgrim arrives in a wounded Embermere, walks the pilgrim road through the canon benchmark quest and five new quests, and ends at the Cinder Gate Rite — the Gate wakes Vaelthorn or Vessamaine (gender-matched), the Echo of the Forgotten dies, and the chapter finale is a roll call of every choice the pilgrim made.

**Theology under test (Rogue Trader pillar):** Ashfall = HEAT IS DUTY. The forge must burn something — the question is always *what*. Every quest in this chapter is a different answer to that question, and no answer is free.

**Three-state tracking (BG3 pillar):** Emberhollow, Forgefall, Kilnreach each hold three states — LEDGER-CLEAN / WOUNDED / HOLLOW-TOUCHED — set by quest choices, all visible at the finale.

## Q1 — "THE COLD LANTERN" (Emberhollow) — CANON BENCHMARK
The doctrine-audited quest (see QUEST_EXAMPLE_EMBERHOLLOW.md). Teaches: the Rite, the FAITH economy, the Echo silhouette, mercy-as-mechanic.
- **Truth sliver revealed:** the Hollow eats worship before it reaches the Gate. (One sliver, nothing more.)
- **Choice:** evacuate the families / hold the lanterns. **Echo:** Emberhollow state set; Brackle lives or doesn't; the ledger records "The Night Emberhollow Rekindled."

## Q2 — "THE KILN-TENDER'S DEBT" (Forgefall) — doctrine worked example
- **HOOK:** a kiln-master won't light her kiln; she asks the pilgrim to fire it. Want: the kiln must burn. Lie: the Kiln-Queen's chevron is on her anvil and she's terrified of what she owes. Cost: the guild learns she underpaid the harvest-debt.
- **TRAIL:** the anvil's chevron leads to a cellar of undelivered first-pours — generations of the guild's owed tribute. The player learns Vessamaine collects oaths like a mother collects chores, without one word of lore.
- **RITE:** pour the owed first-pour; speak the half-forgotten kiln-oath aloud.
- **CHOICE:** pay the cellar's debt and bankrupt the guild's year / fire the kiln clean and let the debt ride. **ECHO:** the kiln burns gold or gray a chapter later; the oathsmiths speak differently; Codex unlocks "Vessamaine — The Mother's Kiln" after the player already knows it.
- **Truth sliver:** the gods collect promises like debts. Nothing else.

## Q3 — "WHAT THE FORGE BURNS" (Kilnreach) — the theology test
- **HOOK:** a cold front has the kiln-city's furnaces failing; the glass-blowers' guild asks the pilgrim to find fuel. (Want: the city survives the cold. Lie: the guild's ash-glass archive — the city's entire recorded history — is the only fuel left that burns hot enough. Cost: they know it, and won't say it first.)
- **TRAIL:** the fuel hunt crosses the ash-glass wastes; every alternative fuel fails on-screen (wet peat, green timber, hollow-ash — each attempt visible, each one gutters). The player arrives at the archive door themselves.
- **RITE:** the pilgrim stokes the furnace — whatever they feed it, they feed with their own hands.
- **CHOICE:** burn the archive and the city lives warm / the archive stands and the kilns gutter out a season (merchants leave, the WOUNDED state seeds). **ECHO:** Kilnreach's glass is clear or smoke-gray forever after; a glass-blower child either recites the city's lineages from memory ("we burned the papers, we keep the names") or stands in a cold shop.
- **Truth sliver:** Ashfall's theology — heat is duty, and duty always costs something. The player has now role-played it.

## Q4 — "THE VIGIL" (the Cinder Gate approach) — mystery by omission
- **HOOK:** no one asks for anything. A vigil-keeper at the Gate's scorched circle silently offers the pilgrim a place at the night fire. (The only quest in the chapter with no want — because the theology being shown is Vaelthorn's: a furnace does not ask.)
- **TRAIL:** one night of vigil, played: the circle where nothing grows, the ash holding the shape of footprints not the pilgrim's, offerings left by travelers centuries dead, the vigil-keepers' half-practice of a rite the player half-recognizes from the Cold Lantern quest.
- **RITE:** the pilgrim stands the watch. Nothing is explained; the silence is the content.
- **CHOICE:** at dawn, the pilgrim either takes the vigil-keepers' unclaimed tribute (a chest of old offerings — real money, real gear) / leaves it and marks the circle with their own flame. **ECHO:** at the chapter's Gate Rite, the tribute-taker's Mark flickers once; the marker's burns steadier. No one says why.
- **Truth sliver:** Vaelthorn stood here, for a season, for nothing owed. The player assembles it themselves — that's the point.

## Q5 — "GOOD STEEL" (the pilgrim road between towns) — Order politics
- **HOOK:** a Smith-Order quartermaster refuses to re-arm a wounded Warden road-captain — the Warden's rite-blade was "wasted on ceremony" (a Gate-vigil, not a fight). Want: the captain's escort must leave by dawn. Lie: the Smith sold the steel meant for the Warden's repair to a Cantor caravan — at a profit. Cost: if it surfaces, the Smith's forge-credit collapses.
- **TRAIL:** the pilgrim traces the missing steel through a Cantor caravan's song-receipts (Cantors record debts as sung lines — the player HEARS the ledger), uncovering the sale without being told.
- **RITE:** the pilgrim re-forges the blade's edge with the Smith — hours of hammer rhythm, the Smith talking with his hands, not his mouth.
- **CHOICE:** expose the sale (the Smith is stripped from the road's support; the pilgrim gains the Wardens' deep trust) / bury it (the Smith owes the pilgrim a lifetime debt — and pays it at the Depths' door, quest 6). **ECHO:** the road's Order stations open or close to the pilgrim; the roll call remembers.
- **Truth sliver:** the Orders are not one family — and the pilgrim now holds standing among them.

## Q6 — "THE FIRST OATHBLADE" (the Depths beneath the Cinderlands) — VOW QUEST
- **HOOK:** the Gate Rite cannot complete without the Gate's own relic — and the shrine-keeper of the First Vigil cannot say its name aloud (the sound goes missing — the Hollow's silence-siege, from the benchmark quest, escalated).
- **TRAIL:** the Depths: braided fracture-dungeons drawn from the Furnace-King's fever of the forge — halls of half-formed steel, walls that pour like slag, T1 swarms of nightmare vermin, a T2 Unmade Champion walking in a First Pilgrim's recovered armor (bestiary canon: the vault's own grammar worn by the Hollow).
- **RITE:** at the vault's heart, the pilgrim must re-enact the First Vigil's oath-half — the half the shrine-keeper couldn't say — over the lost Oathblade. (The player has been practicing the rite-fragments since Q1; now they land whole.)
- **CHOICE:** claim the Oathblade and wake the Unmade Champion (hard fight, the relic comes home) / take the champion's armor and leave the blade (the armor is worth more at market — and the blade sleeps on). **ECHO:** the pilgrim exits with the blade or in stolen plate; the Gate Rite's final beat differs accordingly.
- **Truth sliver:** the Hollow steals even vows. One sliver, held for the finale.

## FINALE — "THE GATE RITE" (the Cinder Gate)
The chapter's roll call: **who stands with the pilgrim at the Gate = who the choices left standing.** Brackle or her lantern. The kiln-master, or her guild's ashes. Kilnreach's glass-blowers, warm or gray. The Warden captain armed or barehanded. The Smith, stripped or indebted. All visible on the Gate field — no text, just faces.
- The Rite wakes the god THROUGH the pilgrim (canon): Vaelthorn for a male pilgrim, Vessamaine for a female pilgrim. The Gate-Cycle ultimate plays out in the wave defense (gameplay canon).
- **Boss:** the ECHO OF THE FORGOTTEN (T3, false waking) — and the duel's third phase drops to pilgrim scale inside the wound (Master Architecture law: god-fight and climb are the same encounter).
- **ECHO (chapter):** the realm's Hollow burns off per Avalon Doctrine; the three towns' states lock into the world; the Kiln-Queen's silence quest hook plants (canon); the Codex unlocks the Embermere saga pages — as confirmations.
