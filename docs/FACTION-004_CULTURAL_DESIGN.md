## Cultural Design Doc (pre-art, per Amendment 1)
Pantheon of boundaries. Gods of every threshold: matter/spirit, reflection/original, storm/shrine, life/death.
Art culture: **Japanese — Shinto shrine-architecture world.** A mountain path of a thousand vermilion torii gates receding into mist.

Against warm pale shrine-cypress godflesh seams. The realm GLOWS vermilion — every gate an ember in the fog.

Alive, not carved, not dull wood — a LIVING DIVINE BEING that can manifest into the mortal realm (per the Titan Definition: warm organic godflesh, heat-shimmer aura, never stone/chisel/robot).

- **Arashido:** shrine-guardian war robes over lacquered chest plates (tatenashi-style, NO pauldrons — godflesh shoulders bare), layered crimson silk slashed by wind, shimenawa rope belt with hanging gofu paper streamers (BLANK — abstract folds only, never letterforms), waraji sandals of gold rope, forearms wrapped in shrine rope.
