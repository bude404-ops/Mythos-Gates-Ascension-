# CLASS TREE — TOWER (Gate-Shield / Vigil) v1 CANON

> Faction-neutral weapon discipline. Class = hands; god = flame. STAMINA/cooldowns, never FAITH.

## Doctrine
The Gate-Shield is a wall that walks. The Vigil does not kill quickly — the Vigil denies: space, timing, hope. Every enemy that swings at the shield pays a toll, and the wall moves on.

## Tier 1 — The Post
1. **PLANTED** — Passive. Blocking reduces damage 70%; stamina regenerates while holding block (unique).
2. **SHOVE** — 15 stamina. Shield bash — flinch + push 2m (space control).
3. **TOLL** — Passive. Each blocked hit refunds 3 stamina to you.

## Tier 2 — The Rampart
4. **BRACE** — 8s cd. Dig in for 2s: immune to knockback, block covers 360° for the duration.
5. **BULWARK MARCH** — 25 stamina. Advance behind the shield: walk forward, fully blocking, pushing enemies with you (the gate advances).
6. **RIM STRIKE** — Passive. Shove against a blocking/staggered enemy interrupts + deals +40%.

## Tier 3 — The Gate
7. **THE THRESHOLD** — 12s cd. Slam the shield down: a 3m radius taunt — enemies must attack you for 3s.
8. **COUNTERVAIL** — 30 stamina. Parry timing: perfect block within 0.2s window staggers the attacker outright.
9. **OLD STONES** — Passive. While below 40% HP, block strength +20% (the wall remembers).

## Tier 4 — Capstone (mastery, not god)
10. **THE GATE, HELD** — 25s cooldown. The Vigil becomes the Gate: planted and immovable for 8s — damage blocked is CONVERTED, storing 50% of it; when the stance ends, release it all as a shockwave knockback. Hold the line, then break it.

## Stat pins (engine source of truth)
- Shield block: 70% reduction (75% with Old Stones); stamina pool 100.
- Parry window 0.2s; Taunt overrides enemy AI targeting for 3s.
- Synergy: Cinder/Gloom/Everbloom ward rites stack on top of block; WAKING MOUNTAIN + THE GATE, HELD = the full wall fantasy.
