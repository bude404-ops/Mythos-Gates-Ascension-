## 1. ENEMY TIERS

| Tier | Who | Model | Crowd cap (mobile) |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 ANTS | mortals, mortal soldiers, small creatures | one-tap kill + ragdoll launch, NO HP bar, NO damage numbers | up to 40 on screen, pooled |

**T1 design rule:** a titan hit is ALWAYS lethal to ants. The shockwave only LAUNCHES
(ants fly per the game-feel spec); your follow-up swing kills. Impact > damage.
**T2 rule:** stagger is the language — hit reactions sell the weight before the kill.
**T3 rule:** duels use the SAME timing curves as the player titan — slow telegraphs both ways.
