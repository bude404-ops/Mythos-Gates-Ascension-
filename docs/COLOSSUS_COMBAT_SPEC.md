## 1. ENEMY TIERS

| Tier | Who | Model | Crowd cap (mobile) |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 ANTS | mortals, mortal soldiers, small creatures | one-tap kill + ragdoll launch, NO HP bar, NO damage numbers | up to 40 on screen, pooled |
| T2 BRUTES | siege beasts, giant champions, elite guards | 3–6 titan hits, stagger + launchable, small HP bar | up to 8 |
| T3 COLOSSI/BOSSES | enemy titans, dungeon bosses, faction heads | full stat duels — phase thresholds, weak points, real numbers | 1 (+ adds) |

**T1 design rule:** a titan hit is ALWAYS lethal to ants. The shockwave only LAUNCHES
(ants fly per the game-feel spec); your follow-up swing kills. Impact > damage.
**T2 rule:** stagger is the language — hit reactions sell the weight before the kill.
**T3 rule:** duels use the SAME timing curves as the player titan — slow telegraphs both ways.

## 4. DAMAGE MODEL BY TIER

- **T1 ants:** any hit = kill. No damage numbers rendered (numbers only for T2+). Ragdoll
  launch force per game-feel spec (force ∝ 1 − dist/18 m; airborne inside 6 m).
- **T2 brutes:** HP = 3–6 hits. Heavy attack = 2 hits. Shockwave staggers (no damage) and
  launches at close range. Killing blow = ragdoll.
- **T3 colossi/bosses:** full stat sheet — HP pools in the hundreds, phase thresholds at
  66% / 33% (visual activation cues per the worship-god lore), weak-point crit windows.
