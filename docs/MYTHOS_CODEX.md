## WORLD LAW
- 6 factions = 6 main elements: FIRE, AIR, WATER, EARTH, LIFE, DEATH
- Each faction = a REALM DOMAIN the deities were cast to in the exile (faction names ARE realm names)
- Each realm's Gate stands in Mythos — the only bridge between exile and the world they made
- 4 deities per faction = 24 colossi
- Every name invented — no real-culture echoes
- Every deity: Master Titan Formula (COLOSSAL x ELEMENTAL x REALM IDENTITY), one invented armor construction philosophy, one function-made-solid weapon
- Class spread per realm: Warden (warrior), Caster (ALL CASTERS WIELD STAFFS — hard rule), Striker (assassin), Ranged
- The Hollow: a VOID OF ELEMENTAL POWERS — the unformed residue of creation. Not a faction. The wound.

## THE ORIGIN (canon)
1. The six elemental powers helped FORM the mortal realm — Mythos.
2. Creation left a residue: THE HOLLOW, a void of raw unformed elemental power.
3. The Hollow threatened to unmake the young world — so the six TOGETHER imprisoned it. That imprisonment SAVED Mythos.
4. But the act left too much concentrated divine power inside the world — an imbalance Mythos itself could not hold. To keep balance, MYTHOS EXPELLED THE SIX to their realm domains. (This is why the Gates exist, and why the deities cannot simply walk back in.)
5. Ages passed in exile. The six grew into their realms — and FORGOT the Hollow. The imprisonment holding it was built of their combined vigilance, and vigilance faded with memory.
6. The colossi are the six's ONLY way back through the Gates — vessels grown in exile, powered remotely from the realms. Mortal faith is the structural integrity of the whole bridge.

## WING DOCTRINE v2 (Sept 4 2026, BudE404) — wing type matches the faction's ELEMENT, not one global style

**THE MATERIAL CLAUSE (Sept 4 2026, BudE404):** Wings are PART OF THE ARMOR — not organic appendages, not elemental flesh, not separate growths. Every wing pair is forged of the same Aetherium relic-material as the deity's chest plate, ANCHORED INTO THE BACK-PLATE of the chest armor as a fused extension of the five-piece set, sharing its gouged finish, its seams, and its faction glow. The element-native grammar (storm/petal/bone shape language) lives in the wing's ARCHITECTURE only — the SUBSTANCE is always armor of the faction's material. A wing is a relic, not a limb.

**THE GLOBAL RULE (revised):** Wings are ELEMENT-NATIVE — each faction's wing type embodies its own element. Only the FIRE faction is draconic (dragons ARE the fire beast-language — this is why Vharkar and Sorvath wear dragon wings). The other winged factions carry wings built of their own element.

**ELEMENT EXCEPTIONS — two factions never fly (unchanged):**
- F5 WATER (Marenth): ZERO wings. Fin architecture — crest-fins, fin-mantles, tail-blades; the swimming silhouette.
- F6 EARTH (Stoneheart): ZERO wings. Mountain architecture — cliff-ridge pauldrons, basalt spines, root-anchors. Stone does not leave the ground.

**THE TENDRIL SYSTEM (Sept 4 2026, BudE404: 'OK make tendrils wings' — dual-system CONFIRMED):** Wing anchors are always armored wing-root collars fused to the chest back-plate; out of the roots pours the deity's OWN ELEMENT as sweeping tendril-wings (Diablo-archangel silhouette, element-native). TENDRIL wings = casters, heralds, agile strikers (Sorvath: fire tendrils — FIRST tendril-wing deity, v4). PLATE wings = wardens and heaviest builds (Vharkar canon untouched, Morvain, Thevril). Every wing is armor-rooted; the blade is either poured element or forged plate. TENDRIL COUNT + SCALE LAW (BudE404): exactly 3-4 thick DISTINCT tendrils per side, one armored root per shoulder blade (both sides always), separated by clear gaps so the wing reads as fingers, NEVER a unified flowing mass — no cape, no mantle, no sheet of element. SCALE: each tendril root as thick as the deity's own torso, length ~2x body height, slow taper over the full sweep, outermost longest/highest — wings dwarf the figure, true Diablo archangel proportions.
