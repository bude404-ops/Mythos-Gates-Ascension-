## WORLD LAW
- 6 factions = 6 main elements: FIRE, AIR, WATER, EARTH, LIFE, DEATH
- Each faction = a REALM DOMAIN the deities were cast to in the exile (faction names ARE realm names)
- Each realm's Gate stands in Mythos — the only bridge between exile and the world they made
- 4 deities per faction = 24 colossi
- Every name invented — no real-culture echoes
- Every deity: Master Titan Formula (COLOSSAL x ELEMENTAL x REALM IDENTITY), one invented armor construction philosophy, one function-made-solid weapon
- Class spread per realm: Warden (warrior), Caster (ALL CASTERS WIELD STAFFS — hard rule), Striker (assassin), Ranged
- The Hollow: a VOID OF ELEMENTAL POWERS — the unformed residue of creation. Not a faction. The wound.

## THE ORIGIN (canon)
1. The six elemental powers helped FORM the mortal realm — Mythos.
2. Creation left a residue: THE HOLLOW, a void of raw unformed elemental power.
3. The Hollow threatened to unmake the young world — so the six TOGETHER imprisoned it. That imprisonment SAVED Mythos.
4. But the act left too much concentrated divine power inside the world — an imbalance Mythos itself could not hold. To keep balance, MYTHOS EXPELLED THE SIX to their realm domains. (This is why the Gates exist, and why the deities cannot simply walk back in.)
5. Ages passed in exile. The six grew into their realms — and FORGOT the Hollow. The imprisonment holding it was built of their combined vigilance, and vigilance faded with memory.
6. The colossi are the six's ONLY way back through the Gates — vessels grown in exile, powered remotely from the realms. Mortal faith is the structural integrity of the whole bridge.
