## WORLD LAW
- 6 factions = 6 main elements of Mythos: FIRE, AIR, WATER, EARTH, LIFE, DEATH
- Each faction guards one Mythos Gate in its own terrain
- 4 deities per faction = 24 colossi total
- Every name below is INVENTED from the element's own sound — no real-culture echoes
- Every deity: Master Titan Formula (COLOSSAL x ELEMENTAL x FACTION IDENTITY), one invented armor construction philosophy, one weapon that is their function made solid
- Class spread per faction: one Warden (warrior/duelist), one Caster, one Striker (assassin), one Ranged
