THE ECHO DUEL

At the Gate Rite's height, the Hollow answers the waking with its own god-shape: THE ECHO OF THE FORGOTTEN — a silhouette vaster than any giant, with no face, because no one ever recorded one. It fights as your DARK MIRROR: the same telegraphs you know, shed in reverse — where you shed element, it sheds absence.

I — GOD VS GOD (100–66%). Gods do not chase — where you rose is your ground. WARD (bottom-left, timed) drinks any attack it catches and converts it to FAITH. Answer with the kit: three-swing combo (finisher staggers it), SURGE orbs, VOLLEY lances, QUAKE shockwaves.
II — THE DRAINED FIELD (66–33%). Its shell cracks and the arena DE-COLORS: standing on drained ground saps the vessel. Fight from the ember islands.
III — THE WOUND (33–0%). The god-fight and the climb are one encounter: the Echo splits, and YOU — the pilgrim, lantern in hand — cross inside the wound to strike the heart. Void-motes swarm; roll through them with the lantern raised.

And at the heart: the endgame's one weapon. To name a thing is to form it — and absence was all it had.
