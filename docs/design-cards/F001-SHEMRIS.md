# F001-SHEMRIS — Design Card (New Era)

**Deity:** SHEMRIS, the Verdict String
**Faction:** F001 Egypt (Sun-Verdict Desert)
**Class:** Archer / Divine Precision
**Pipeline:** Stage 1 — Character Lock

## Essence
Shemris is the goddess of PRECISION — the shot loosed before the target knows it exists, the arrow that is already fate by the time it's fired. Where Khaveth weighs the verdict and Djekhur carries it out, Shemris DELIVERS it — across any distance, without warning, without escape. Divine marksmanship as worship.

## Body
- Weathered golden desert sandstone, anatomy-fused (zero separate plates, ornament carved as relief into the stone skin, gold inlay linework WITHIN the stone)
- Female colossus — sexy AND titanic: statuesque athletic goddess silhouette, long powerful legs, sculpted waist, proud posture; seductive in presence and styling but unmistakably divine and monumental in anatomy (never mortal softness, never humanized)
- Proportions: tall and lean where Djekhur is broad — 10 heads tall, elongated elegance, arrow-straight spine
- Weathered ancient patina, fine chiseled facets, cracks, gold-inlay linework
- Grimdark activation: faint molten ember seams, exactly 3 sigils (heart-sigil largest, brow glyph, weapon glyph), tight lamp eyes (solid light, no pupils)

## Invented Divine Iconography (no historical replicas)
- FALCON-SWOOP PAULDRONS: shoulder masses carved as invented diving-falcon wing geometry — layered swoop-planes and feather-chevrons in her own invented geometry, NOT museum bird replicas
- VEIL OF FEATHERS: her face-veil and back-cloak carved as cascading feather-forms in the same sandstone — hard-carved, never fabric
- WARDEN-GAZE DIAMOND: single carved gemstone set in her brow above the eyes — her targeting organ
- OATH-STRING BELT: carved cord-loop belt at the waist

## Weapon — SEMBLANCE
An absurdly oversized divine greatbow — a one-of-one invented form:
- Limbs: twin carved sandstone arcs of invented falcon-bone geometry, reinforced with gold-inlay struts
- String: a single thread of pure glowing light, taut and thin
- Grip: a carved sandstone grip wrapped in stone cords; no arrows visible — her shots are drawn light
- Held low at her side in one hand, or planted point-down

## Stage 1 Composition Rules
- Neutral dais, character-only (no world, no Gate, no environment)
- Full figure, heroic readable pose
- Effects as accents — restrained ember seams, 3 sigils, lamp eyes, the light string
- LOTR-cinematic rendering quality, WoW scale reading
