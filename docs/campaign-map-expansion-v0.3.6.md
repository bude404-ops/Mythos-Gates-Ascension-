# Mythos Gates Campaign and Map Expansion — v0.3.6

> **Avatar System**: Players channel Avatars — divine projections of their chosen Deity. The Deity remains protected in its domain. Progression via Belief and Influence. Avatar death = respawn. Solo combat. God-scale in all realms.


Date: 2026-08-10

This expansion adds three design-ready realm campaign arcs with linked campaign maps, tactical maps, chapters, and map art prompts.

## Added Campaigns

- **Helio-Kemet Sun Realm** — Aten Ra campaign-map and tactical battle flow based on the locked mobile references.
- **Kami Mirror Tempest** — shrine-road campaign with mirror, foxfire, wind, and seal mechanics.
- **Empyrean Choir Vault** — floating celestial ascent with resonance beacons, elevation, and timed bridge pressure.

## Design Direction

Campaign maps define the mobile chapter-select layer. Tactical maps define the 2.5D battle-board layer. Every new map has an art prompt and an individual JSON record so the Dev Platform can validate, search, and eventually turn these into playable/selectable screens.
