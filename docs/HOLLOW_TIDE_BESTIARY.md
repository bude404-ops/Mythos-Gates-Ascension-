# THE HOLLOW TIDE — ENEMY BESTIARY
**Canon v1.0 — Sept 4 2026. The Hollow's units: erased-things, mockeries of what they consumed. Tiers map to the combat spec (T1 ants / T2 brutes / T3 bosses). The Hollow cannot create — it can only hollow: every unit is a THIEF's copy of something real, which is the art direction.**

**GLOBAL HOLLOW LOOK:** forms that read as "silhouette where substance should be" — edges that don't quite hold, faces that are absence rather than features, movement like a film run backwards. Where they stand, Aetherium grays out: color drains in a radius (environment tell). NEVER goo/rot/gore horror — ERASURE horror. The scariest thing about them is what's missing.

## T1 — THE UNDONE (ants — one-tap, crowd)
- **MUTEFOLK:** hollowed mortals walking the shapes of their old routines — fishermen casting dry nets, mothers rocking nothing. No faces, just smooth absence. Kill = release (they crumble to gray light that flows toward the nearest Gate — FAITH from mercy).
- **SHADE-BEASTS:** hollowed fauna running in silent herds — the thistleddeer herd with no eyes, the moorhound pack made of outline. Ragdoll-fodder.
- **GRIMLIGHTS:** fist-sized void-motes that swarm like gnats; alone harmless, in cloud they dim lantern-light (the lantern towns' bane).

## T2 — THE WRONGFUL (brutes — 3-6 hits, staggerable)
- **UNMADE CHAMPIONS:** hollowed gate-guardians still wearing their QUARRY-PLATE/LANTERN-PLATE armor — the armor is REAL and stays behind when they fall (loot + lore: every kill recovers a mortal thing).
- **ERASED DRAKES:** lesser drakes with their element inverted — a fire-drake that breathes cold-absence, wings that are pure outline. First sign a region is deep-hollowed.
- **THE STILL CHOIR:** six MUTEFOLK fused in a ring, chanting in reverse; break the ring or it re-hollows the ground (T2 + zone-denial).

## T3 — THE AVATARS OF HUNGER (bosses)
- **GATE-WORMS:** colossal serpentine masses of eroded Gate-masonry that push through fractures — living siege engines. Tower Wave sieges are Gate-Worm attacks.
- **THE ECHO OF THE FORGOTTEN:** mid-campaign: a titanic winged silhouette made of the ABSENCE of the Forgotten's old shape — it has no face because no one ever recorded one. Fights like a dark mirror of a dragon god (the player learns dragon-phase timings by fighting their own mirror).
- **THE NAMING (endgame event, not a unit):** at the Empty Pedestal, the Hollow's advance stalls because mortals begin to SAY THE NAME — and naming is the one act the Hollow cannot hollow. The endgame's true weapon is a word.

**DESIGN RULE:** no Hollow unit is ever "evil-colored" — they are DE-COLORED. Faction units fight in their region's palette; Hollow units drain it.
