# COLOSSUS DESIGN DOCTRINE — LOCKED CANON (BudE404, Sept 4 2026)

Supersedes all prior armor/styling guidance. Applies to ALL factions (F001–F006) and every future Colossal roll.

## The Doctrine (verbatim directive)

Design Colossals as godlike ancient beings whose armor is **carved and fused directly into their massive material bodies, not worn like conventional armor.** Create **legendary, Tolkien-inspired fantasy silhouettes** with **monumental crowns, shoulders, chest plates, bracers, and relic structures integrated into their bodies.** Use **ancient regional architecture, symbols, materials, and mythology to make each faction distinct.** The result should feel **ancient, divine, legendary, handcrafted, and impossibly powerful — like living gods sculpted from sacred stone and metal.**

## Operational Rules

1. FUSED, NOT WORN — Armor is one continuous carved mass with the body; the armor-body boundary is ERASED. No straps, no detachable plates, no "wearing" gear. If it looks like it could be taken off, it's wrong.
2. LEGENDARY SILHOUETTE — Tolkien-inspired monumental silhouettes: gable-end pauldrons, fortress war-belts, temple-column greaves, crowned diadems. Over-the-top and proud. If a mortal saw it, they'd fall to their knees.
3. REGIONAL SOUL — Each faction's armor/relics are built from the region's own ancient architecture, symbols, materials, and mythology (Egypt: sandstone/gold; Norse: granite/bog-iron; Greece: Pentelic marble; Japan: cypress/vermilion).
4. RELIC STRUCTURES — Weapons and relics are monumental integrated structures (planted pillars, planted staffs, planted bows), hewn from the same regional material.
5. MATERIAL LANGUAGE — Weathered millennia patina: erosion, ancient cracks, chipped edges, dust in crevices, mineral stains. Excavated from history, not polished.
6. WORSHIP-BY-EVIDENCE — NO visible mortals in frame (they break scale perception). Worship shown via braziers, incense, offerings, prayer-light.
7. LANDSCAPE DOMINANCE — Sweeping panoramic vistas (F001 standard): the colossus owns the horizon. Scale reads through landscape + architecture built to the colossus's proportion.
8. CINEMATIC TREATMENT — LOTR-film grounded realism, Argonath framing: extreme low angle, head breaking the skyline, warm light breaking through dramatic sky.
9. GLOW DISCIPLINE — Runes/glow subtle and serene, candlelight-through-stone. Divine, never flashy.

## ARMOR ESCALATION ADDENDUM (BudE404, Sept 4 2026 — same day, doctrine v1.1)

Armor standard raised to: **extraordinary, legendary, the greatest armor ever forged for a god.**
- Massive, ornate, highly detailed, unmistakably divine
- Monumental shoulder pieces, intricate chest armor, TOWERING crowns
- Engraved plates, flowing relics, inlaid GEMSTONES, glowing runes, mythical symbols
- Epic Tolkien-level craftsmanship — ancient, majestic, supernatural, awe-inspiring
- Armor appears partially CARVED INTO and GROWN FROM the Colossal's body (fusion rule held: never worn, never detachable)
- Weathering rule still applies: the ornate armor carries millennia patina — legendary but excavated from history, never polished-new
