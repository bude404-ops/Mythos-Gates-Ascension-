THE FIRST WAKING

AVALON — VERTICAL SLICE · THE DEPTHS BELOW EMBERMERE

The pilgrim descends the fever-hall with lantern raised. The Hollow is already here — it was always here. At the hall's end stands the Gate: plain standing stones, and the ember-flame that answers a mark held to it with faith.

Clear the waves. Reach the Gate. Hold the rite. Wake the god.
