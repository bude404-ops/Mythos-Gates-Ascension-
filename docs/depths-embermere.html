EMBERMERE — TIER I · THE FORGE FEVER

The god's dream runs hot below the kiln-country. The deeper halls are colored and sick — fever-orange, magma-veined — and past the heart they drain to grey where paint itself gives out.

Your lantern is the only light that keeps. A drake keeps the altar. Take the relic-gift and climb back before the grey takes the stair.
