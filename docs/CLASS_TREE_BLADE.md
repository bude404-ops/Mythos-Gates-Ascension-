# CLASS TREE — BLADE (Oathblade / Warden) v1 CANON

> Faction-neutral weapon discipline. The Orders teach hands; the gods lend flame — class trees cost STAMINA/cooldowns, never FAITH.

## Doctrine
The Oathblade is a two-handed greatsword — commitment as style. Every swing is slow, heavy, and honest: you trade mobility for unbreakable offense. The Warden does not dodge the blow; the Warden ends it.

## Tier 1 — The Grip
1. **OATH STANCE** — Passive. +15% greatsword damage; swings can't be interrupted by light hits.
2. **CLEAVE** — 20 stamina. Wide horizontal arc; hits all enemies in front (the crowd answer).
3. **IRON FOOTING** — Passive. +poise during any swing animation.

## Tier 2 — The Weight
4. **SUDDEN OATH** — 8s cooldown. Quick diagonal cut that interrupts an enemy's attack (the one fast move the Order allows).
5. **PILGRIM'S ARC** — 25 stamina. Overhead smash; enemies struck are staggered 1s.
6. **LONG ROAD** — Passive. +10% move speed while the greatsword is shouldered (at rest).

## Tier 3 — The Verge
7. **RUPTURE** — 12s cd. Cleave against a staggered enemy deals +50% and applies a wound (bleed 8s).
8. **GATEBREAKER** — 30 stamina. Charging shoulder-bash into upward cut — knocks enemies aside, opens a path.
9. **OATHBOUND EDGE** — Passive. Full stamina swings deal +25%; below half stamina, swings cost less.

## Tier 4 — Capstone (mastery, not god)
10. **THE LAST WORD** — 25s cooldown. A single perfect step-through cut: brief invulnerable step, massive damage, ends with the blade at rest. The duel-ender — one enemy, one answer.

## Stat pins (engine source of truth)
- Greatsword base: 22 dmg/swing, 1.4s swing. Cleave hits up to 5.
- Stamina pool 100; bleed = 2 HP/2s.
- Synergy: pairs with any faction — Cinder/Frost/etc. EDGE rites ride the weapon.
