# THE AVALON NAME BIBLE
**Canon v1.0 — Sept 5, 2026, BudE404 directive: "change the names of the regions and gods to match a more Avalon vibe."**
Rule: GRAMMAR, NOT LIFTS — no real Arthurian names (no Arthur, Merlin, Avalon in-world); the vibe comes from the MUSIC: soft Brythonic consonants, -mere/-holt/-weald/-march/-barrow place suffixes, -wyn/-eth/-aine name endings. The world stays fully original.

## NAMING GRAMMAR

- **Realms**: [romantic element word] + [Avalon place-suffix] — mere (lake), march (borderland), weald (wildwood), barrow (burial-mound country), vale, fell (high crag country).
- **Gods**: personal names + regnal form — every god is a SLEEPING MONARCH: "the Sleeping King/Queen of [realm]" + functional epithet. (This also fixes the WARDEN title collision: gods no longer share the player-role word "Warden" — they are Kings and Queens.)
- **Gates**: single romantic element word + "Gate."
- **Roads**: "Warden of the Ember roads" — address grammar per realm.
- **Mortal regions**: the [element]-Marches.
- First letters kept where possible so canon art still maps at a glance.

## CONSEQUENCES

- **Mortal regions** become the Marches: Embermarch, Galeways, Bloommarch, Barrowmoor, Tidemarch, Stonefell-march (gate-towns keep their own names per NPC roster — re-keyed in the sweep).
- **Roads grammar**: Ember roads / Gale roads / Bloom roads / Barrow roads / Deep roads / Rune roads.
- **Player address**: "Pilgrim of the Ember roads" etc.
- **First Pilgrims**: their titles re-key to the new realms (the Kiln-Warden → the First Warden of the Ember roads — titles of the ROLES survive; only realm names change).
- **The Forgotten**: unchanged (already Avalon-toned).
- Full World Bible / quest / NPC sweep executes after BudE404's verdict on the table (art filenames do NOT change — files keep their working names; canon names live in the docs).
