# THE AVALON NAME BIBLE
**Canon v1.0 — Sept 5, 2026, BudE404 ruling: "change the names of the regions and gods to match a more Avalon vibe — make sure any language is in an Avalon-type theme."**
Companion to docs/AVALON_DOCTRINE.md. All invented names in Mythos follow this grammar. Where any doc uses the old names, this bible's mapping governs.

## I. THE SOUND GRAMMAR (what "Avalon vibe" means here)

- **Roots:** Brythonic/Celtic sound-families — Welsh, Cornish, Breton flavored (the same waters Arthurian myth swims in), but always INVENTED: no real mythological or real-world proper names.
- **Starts:** B-, Br-, C-, Ca-, Gw-, Rh-, Ll-, T-, M-, N- (soft and old — NO Vh-, Kh-, Sk-, X- clusters; that era is retired with Vharkar and Grothmar).
- **Endings:** -wyn / -wen / -wenna (blessed/white), -mor (great/sea), -mere (lake), -var / -mar (keeper of), -rys / -aid, -dir (ward).
- **REALM HEARTS = THE MERES.** Every realm's sleeping god lies closest beneath a sacred lake — each realm is named for its heart-lake, and the realm's heart-Gate stands at the water. (Avalon itself = the isle of the apple; our world = the world of the meres.)
- **Place grammar:** CAER- = gate-towns (Caerduff, Caerlow); LLYN- = shrine-lakes (Llynved); BRYN- = hill shrines; -HOLT / -WOLD / -DOWNS / -MOOR = sub-regions and wilds.
- **Address grammar:** mortals are addressed by role + road: "a Warden of the Ember roads." Region roads: Ember / Gwyn / Afall / Gwyll / Marra / Carreg roads.

### GATES
The Gate-rings rename to their meres: **the Gates of Embermere, Gwynmere, Afallmere, Gwyllmere, Marramere, Carregmere.** (Old Cinder/Squall/Bloom/Dusk/Deep/Stone Gate names retire; a Gate's "element" is now its mere's nature.)

### TITLES THAT SURVIVE UNCHANGED
The Avalon Doctrine terms: the Sleeping Gods, the Pilgrim, the Marked, the First Pilgrims, the Gate Rite, the Hollow, the Wasteland, the Forgotten, the Echo, relic-gifts, Dream-Motes, the roles (Warden/Keeper/Walker/Singer/Smith/Vigil). Mortal-region names (Emberwaste etc.) re-key to realm grammar (the Emberwaste = the Ember Holt of Embermere).

## IV. LANGUAGE LAW (all text in Avalon idiom)

- Mortal speech: plain, road-worn, salt-of-the-earth; no modern words. Oaths and blessings carry the faith: "may the road keep you," "light in the dark places," "the mere remembers."
- The gods do not speak in words; they speak in WEATHER, WARMTH, AND DREAM. Any god-voice in text is render-style: poetry, not dialogue.
- Quest titles read as road-songs: "The Lantern of Llynved," "What the Gwyll Remembers," "The Mere-road East."
- Every proper noun invented from this grammar: no sci-fi, no modern, no other-mythology borrowings.
