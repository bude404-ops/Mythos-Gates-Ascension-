# DESTRUCTIBLES v2, THE ENDLESS VERDICT, AND MONETIZATION
## BudE404 rulings Sept 5: (1) full Consequence Ledger too expensive — propose alternatives; (2) achievement-grinding allowed but decoupled from leveling/fun; (3) monetization = skins, effects, pets.

---

## 1. DESTRUCTIBLES v2 — the Tiered Ledger (replaces the full Consequence Ledger)

The expensive part of the original Ledger was authoring a unique Boon+Wound per destructible. Fix: **three tiers of destructible, only one of which carries authored consequences** — same doctrine as handcrafted-vs-procedural ("author what you remember, pattern what you survive"):

### TIER A — HERO DESTRUCTIBLES (~10–15 per region, named landmarks)
The bridge, the watchtower, the bulwark, the gate pillars' channel-stones. Full authored Boon + Wound. These are the memorable, story-relevant, clip-worthy destructions. Authored individually, tied to missions.

### TIER B — PATTERN DESTRUCTIBLES (all standard battlefield walls, fences, ruin chunks, rock spires)
Destruction **patterns authored once, applied everywhere** via context tags:
- **RUBBLE-COVER** — broken pieces become sprite-scale cover (and giant-scale nothing)
- **LANE-BLOCK** — a collapsed mass blocks an enemy wave path until it decays
- **SEEP-POINT** — broken stone oozes a minor Hollow seep for 30–60s (light wound; corruption creeps through YOUR rubble)
- **PASSAGE** — a breach becomes a new route (dual-edge: yours AND theirs)
Each pattern costs one authoring pass, applies to dozens of objects. The Consequence Law survives (destruction still opens AND costs) at ~20% of the cost.

### TIER C — ENVIRONMENTAL TOYS (trees, carts, loose rocks, hollow props)
Pure spectacle: they break beautifully, spray faith motes (+juice, +1–2 faith), NO ledger entry. The cheap joy that makes the giant feel elemental.

**Net effect:** the giant still feels like a living disaster everywhere, the authored consequences live where players will remember them, and the content cost drops ~80%.

## 2. THE ENDLESS VERDICT — achievement grinding, decoupled from power

The ruling: endless wave play EXISTS, it is REWARDED, and it touches nothing about leveling, faith economy, or fun. It becomes a **prestige sport**, not a job.

### Structure (per region, at the region's Gate)
- **THE ENDLESS VERDICT**: infinite escalating waves — sprite precision waves → elite → manifestation window (tier-2 clock, per Core Gameplay Structure) → colossal wave → boss → loop, forever. Difficulty scales until you fall.
- **Uses the SAME combat systems** as campaign — mastery you build here transfers to missions. No separate balance.

### Scoring (mastery metrics, not time-served)
Wave count · no-hit streaks · environmental kills (hazard/lane kills, never raw DPS) · clock-efficiency in manifestation windows · faith unspent at death. **Score, not hours.** A brilliant 20-wave run outranks a sloppy 40.

### Rewards — GLORY ONLY, zero power
- **Titles & Honors**: "The Unbroken Wave," "Warden of the Nineteenth Verdict," etc.
- **SHRINE HONORS (the lore-perfect reward):** your greatest feats are carved as glowing sigils onto shrines and gate-stones — visible to every warden who visits. Belief is structural integrity — so your achievements literally becoming part of the world's sacred surface is canon-deep, while granting NO mechanical faith/integrity (pure cosmetic world-state).
- **Leaderboards**: per region, per season.
- **Cosmetics**: verdict armor tints, mantle flourishes, pet variants (see §3).
- **HARD RULE: zero faith, zero relics, zero world-state, zero verbs.** Nothing sold, nothing grinded, can touch the map-progress bar.

## 3. MONETIZATION — skins, effects, and the PET SYSTEM

The line that governs all of it: **sell identity, never power.** The master question applies to monetization too — what can we sell that REINFORCES the scale fantasy?

### SKINS
- **Sprite aspect armor sets** — elven-LOTR craft language per canon, faction-themed lines, seasonal sets
- **Giant mantles** — cosmetic variations of the colossal form's element grammar (Vharkar alt-flames: blue-flame variant, obsidian-core variant — palette/material expressions, never new anatomy that contradicts canon)
- **Weapon skins** — warden blades, greatswords, staves with faction sigilwork

### EFFECTS
- Slash/sweep colorways · slam shockwave styles · manifestation burst skins · ascension beam tints · gate-sympathy pulse colorways for your region · glimpse hand variants (the colossal hand is a cosmetic canvas!)
- Death/dispersal effects, victory poses at verdicts

### PETS — EMBERLINGS (the mote companions)
**The lore-perfect pet: a mote of YOUR OWN deity's element** — the god's own flame, small enough to sit on the warden's shoulder. Every faction's pets speak its element: Ashfall emberlings, Skyrend static-wisps, Everbloom seedlings, Duskmourn lantern-motes, Marenth brinelings, Stoneheart pebble-sprites.

- **Scale-dual by design**: at sprite scale it follows as a living lantern beside you; at manifestation/ascension it perches on the god's shoulder or circles the colossal head like a stray mote — your pet present at BOTH scales (monetization that reinforces the identity, not off-brand fluffy commerce).
- **Utility = QoL only**: faith-mote vacuum radius, idle emotes, a soft chime near undiscovered secrets. NEVER combat: no damage, no buffs, no faith generation, no gate integrity effects. A pet cannot touch the economy, period.
- **Earnable AND purchasable**: rare pet lines come from achievements (Endless Verdict ranks, discovery milestones, hunts) and seasonal events; standard lines are sold. Collection/growth is cosmetic (pets evolve visually through achievements you perform WITH them).

### NEVER SOLD (the trust contract)
Faith · relics · progression · world-state · gate integrity · anything affecting the map-progress bar · loot rates · power of any kind at any price.

**Why this works commercially:** the audience that loves this fantasy will pay to look like their god at both scales — and the strongest cosmetics in our game (the giant form itself, the glimpse hand, the ascension moment) are exclusive to us because they're built on our mythology. We're not competing in the generic skin market; we sell the one thing only Mythos Gates can sell.
