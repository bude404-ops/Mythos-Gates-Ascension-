# Mythos Gates: Ascension — Deity Ability Kits

**Combat System v3.0.0 — Locked Aug 20, 2026**

> **Avatar System**: Players channel Avatars — divine projections of their chosen Deity. The Deity remains protected in its domain. Progression via Belief and Influence. Avatar death = respawn. Solo combat. God-scale in all realms.

> **Controls**: Tap-to-move, auto basic attacks (free, triggers when enemy in range), 3-4 ability buttons (tap when charged/cooldown), 1 dodge button (spatial dash, NOT timing-based). Zero joysticks. One-hand playable.

> **Dodge/Parry**: Auto-resolved stat checks (Dodge Stat vs Enemy Accuracy, then Parry Stat vs Enemy Power). The dodge BUTTON is a separate spatial dash for repositioning only.

## Design Principle
Every deity is a FULL SOLO combatant. The role changes HOW you fight through an encounter, not whether you can clear it. Same battlefield = completely different playthrough per deity.

## Resources
- **Divine Energy** — Built from basic attacks and taking damage. Spent on abilities 1, 2, and Signature.
- **Belief Bar** — Built throughout the battle from all combat actions. At 100%, Ultimate is unlocked.
- **Faction Resource** — Unique per faction (Solar Charge, Oath Fury, Divine Glory, etc.). Powers the deity's passive.

## Roles (4 Locked Combat Roles)
- **Warrior** — Methodical, high survivability, holds ground. Faith trigger: Win while staying above 50% HP.
- **Caster** — Ability-focused, combo chains, resource management. Faith trigger: Win after 3+ ability combo chains.
- **Archer** — Positioning-based, ranged pressure, battlefield control. Faith trigger: Win while controlling 60%+ of battlefield nodes.
- **Assassin** — Stealth, burst, execute, high dodge. Faith trigger: Win after breaking 3+ enemy armor/defense stacks.

## Ability Kit Structure (Per Deity)
Each deity has:
1. **Basic Attack** — Auto, no cooldown. 3-4 hit combo chain. Builds Divine Energy. Unique per deity.
2. **Ability 1** — Short cooldown (5-8s). Low energy cost (20-30). Tactical — mobility, CC, shield, or buff.
3. **Ability 2** — Medium cooldown (8-12s). Medium energy cost (35-45). Stronger — AoE, heavy damage, or sustain.
4. **Signature** — Long cooldown (15-20s). High energy cost (65-75). Signature divine skill.
5. **Ultimate** — Requires 100% Belief Bar. Screen-wide effect. Unique per deity. Cinematic.
6. **Passive** — Always active. Builds faction resource. Empowers at threshold.
