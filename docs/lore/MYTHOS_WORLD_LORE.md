# MYTHOS WORLD LORE — Master Cosmology
Canonized September 4, 2026 by BudE404. This is the master world document. All factions, art, and game systems answer to this.

---

## THE MORTAL REALM: MYTHOS

The mortal realm is called **Mythos**. It is the **center of everything** — all the fighting, the battles, and the campaigns of the game happen here. The deities' own realms are separate dimensions beyond the Mythos Gates; Mythos is the prize.

## THE EXILE — WHY THE DEITIES LEFT MYTHOS

**Secret lore (hidden from the world's inhabitants, known to us as creators):**

The deities once existed **in Mythos itself**, walking the mortal realm and shaping it with their own hands. That is why the world's scenery and buildings are impossibly exaggerated — the god-shaped architecture (plaza-sized steps, ravine-spanning gates, impossible terraces) is the standing evidence of their ancient presence.

But their power was too great for one realm to hold. **Mythos itself** — the realm, as a self-balancing force — **forced the deities through the Mythos Gates**, each into their own separate realm, to balance their powers. This was not a war the deities chose; the realm exiled them to restore equilibrium.

- The age when deities walked Mythos is **long passed and hidden** — mortals have forgotten it, or never knew it.
- The impossible architecture they left behind still stands, unexplained.
- Since the exile, the deities cannot walk Mythos directly. Their only presence is power channeled through their Gates.

## THE FORGOTTEN DEITY — ORIGIN OF THE HOLLOW

When the Mythos Gates were created, one deity — **the Forgotten** — was not moved to a realm of its own. It was **trapped in the void** between/beyond the realms.

- Alone in the void, the Forgotten **grew resentful** of the other deities.
- Out of that resentment it **created the Hollow** — the corrupting force that now threatens Mythos.
- The Forgotten **escaped the void through a fracture in its own Mythos Gate**. The fracture opened because the other deities had grown **complacent and were losing belief** — faith is the power that holds the Gates and the balance intact. Weak belief = weakened Gates = fracture.
- This ties the Hollow directly to the **FAITH resource system**: faith is not just power, it is the structural integrity of the cosmic balance. Lost faith lets the Hollow in.

## THE COLOSSI — THE ONLY WAY BACK

Since the Gates moved the deities to their own realms, the **colossus has been their only way back to the mortal realm.**

- A colossus is a stone vessel that the deity **grows and carves itself** through the power of its Mythos Gate — no civilization ever carved them (self-forged doctrine).
- The deity powers and shapes its colossal **remotely, through the Gate** — remove the Gate and it is just weathered stone.
- The colossal "comes to life" because divine essence crosses the Gate and floods the vessel.
- Through their colossi, the deities can once more act in Mythos — fight the Hollow, wage campaigns, and reclaim the realm that exiled them.

## THE GREAT ARC (campaign spine)

1. **The Age of Walking** (hidden lore): deities shaped Mythos with their own hands.
2. **The Exile**: Mythos forced the deities through the Gates to balance their power; the Forgotten was trapped in the void.
3. **The Long Silence**: deities reach Mythos only through their Gates; mortals forget; belief thins.
4. **The Fracture**: the Forgotten escapes through its broken Gate, unleashing the Hollow on Mythos.
5. **The War of the Colossi** (the game): the deities, unable to walk Mythos themselves, grow their colossi through their Gates and send them to fight the Hollow — through mortals who carry their faith.

## RULES THIS ESTABLISHES (binding on all art/design)

- The mortal realm is always called **Mythos** in design docs and copy.
- Mythos is the theater of war — every campaign/battle system is set there.
- The Hollow is the enemy, born from the Forgotten's resentment in the void.
- **The Avatar Doctrine: the deities never cross into Mythos themselves — each sends a draconian avatar, a Gate-crossing vessel forged of Aetherium in the First Forge grammar, carrying the deity's element and wielding its relic weapon. When an avatar falls in the mortal realm, that deity's voice goes silent among mortals until the vessel is re-forged. Avatars are the only divine presence mortals ever witness; every god-sighting in Mythos is one of the twenty-four.
Faith/belief is structural**: losing belief weakens Gates and enables Hollow incursion. (Ties to FAITH single-resource combat spec — kills feed the god.)
- Colossi are the deities' only presence in Mythos. They are self-grown through the Gates — never mortal-crafted.
- The deities-walked age stays hidden/forgotten in-world — but its evidence is everywhere in the exaggerated architecture.

## THE ORIGIN, DEEPENED (BudE404 canon, Sept 4 2026)

1. The six elemental powers helped FORM Mythos — the world carries their fingerprints (god-shaped architecture is the standing evidence).
2. Creation left a residue: THE HOLLOW — a VOID OF RAW UNFORMED ELEMENTAL POWER.
3. The Hollow — a void of stolen elemental powers — threatened to unmake the young world. The six TOGETHER were its FIRST JAILERS: they imprisoned it at the world's founding, and that imprisonment is what SAVED Mythos. But the act left the mortal realm saturated with divine power — to keep balance, Mythos ITSELF expelled the six into their realm domains. Salvation and exile were one act.
4. But the act concentrated too much divine power inside the world — an imbalance Mythos could not hold. To keep balance, MYTHOS ITSELF EXPELLED THE SIX to their realm domains: ASHFALL (Fire), SKYREND (Air), EVERBLOOM (Life), DUSKMOURN (Death), MARENTH (Water), STONEHEART (Earth).
5. Ages passed in exile. The six grew into their realms — and FORGOT the Hollow. Their imprisonment of it was built on combined vigilance, and vigilance faded with memory.
6. The Gates: one per realm, standing in Mythos — the only bridge. Colossi are the six's only way back: vessels grown in exile, powered remotely from the realms. Mortal faith is the structural integrity of the bridge.
7. The Hollow stirs where memory fails. Forgotten by its jailers, it tests the seams of its prison — the fracture in its Gate.

KEY REFRAME: the deities were not expelled as punishment — they were expelled to KEEP BALANCE after the act that SAVED the world. The world owes them everything and cast them out anyway. Tragedy is structural.


## AETHERIUM — THE SUBSTANCE OF MATTER (BudE404 canon, Sept 4 2026)

**Aetherium is what makes up matter itself.** Not a metal that was forged — the fundamental substrate of all physical form. Everything that HAS form in Mythos, forms because of aetherium.

**THE PREMISE (and why the armor works):** the deities' power is raw elemental force — fire, storm, tide, stone, growth, shadow — and raw force is formless. Aetherium is what gives force a shape to inhabit. This is why the ancient armor can hold their elemental forms: the Aetherium relic plate is made of the very substance of matter, and elemental power GRIPS it naturally, the way water grips a riverbed. The armor doesn't cage the element — the element holds onto it, because aetherium is the only thing it CAN hold.

- **The colossi:** elemental body given form by aetherium armor — form and force, substrate and power.
- **The Gates:** transmit belief (formless) into aetherium structure (form) — the power line.
- **The Hollow:** the residue that never took form — unformed power WITHOUT aetherium's gift. Its hunger is the hunger of the formless for form.
- **The death pile:** the element releases its grip and dissipates; the aetherium remains — because matter remains. The armor is never destroyed, only emptied.

**One-line cosmology:** aetherium is the body, the elements are the breath, belief is the pulse.

The Hollow expresses itself through spawn, not avatars: swarms of basic husks, a few elite brutes, and rare world bosses — the largest being shards of the Forgotten itself. The Hollow has no pantheon; it has an infestation.
