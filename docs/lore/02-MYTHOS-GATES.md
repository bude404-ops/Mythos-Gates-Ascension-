# 02 — Mythos Gates

## Definition

Mythos Gates are ancient dimensional gateways connecting powerful Realms.

They are the primary method of inter-Realm travel. They allow Avatars, powers, relics, memories, conflicts, and wars to cross between dimensions.

A Mythos Gate is not a portal made by technology. It is an ancient reality-structure: part threshold, part wound, part sacred mechanism, part living law.

## The Gate and the Avatar System

The Mythos Gates are the mechanism through which Deities project their Avatars into other Realms. A Deity cannot fully manifest across dimensional boundaries — the Gate filters and limits divine power. When a Deity sends an Avatar through a Gate, the Avatar arrives as a fraction of the Deity's full strength.

### How the Gate Affects Avatars

- **Home Realm Gate:** The Avatar enters at maximum power. The Gate reinforces the home Realm's laws, allowing the Deity's full divine presence to channel through the Avatar.
- **Enemy Realm Gate:** The Avatar enters weakened. The Gate into hostile territory resists foreign divine power, reducing the Avatar's aura, ability charges, and increasing cooldowns. Invading through an enemy Gate is the most dangerous action in the game — but offers the greatest Belief and Influence rewards.
- **Earth Gate:** The Avatar enters at balanced power. Earth's Gates are damaged and neutral — no Realm's law holds dominance. All Avatars manifest equally. These damaged Gates are also where the Hollow invade reality.

### The Gate as Crossing Mechanism

Every time a player enters a dungeon or mission, the Avatar is projected through a Mythos Gate. The Gate determines:
- Which Realm the Avatar enters
- The power modifier applied (home advantage, enemy disadvantage, or neutral)
- The available enemy types and threat level
- The reward modifiers (higher for invasions, standard for home, balanced for Earth)

## Function

Mythos Gates can:

- Connect one Realm to another
- Open passage into sealed ruins
- Expose forgotten dimensional roads
- Distort distance, memory, weather, gravity, identity, and time
- Awaken dormant divine power
- Create Gateborn entities
- Pull relics or battlefield terrain from impossible locations
- Serve as campaign mission sites
- Serve as dungeon entrances
- Serve as boss arenas
- Serve as Avatar projection points
- Create Hollow invasion corridors on Earth

## The Ascension Collapse

The Ascension Collapse shattered the old dimensional order.

Before the Collapse, the Realms used ancient Gate roads. Some routes were sacred. Some were military. Some were sealed by treaty. Some were older than any surviving civilization.

When the Collapse struck:

- Realm civilizations fractured
- Ancient roads vanished
- Sealed Realms cracked open
- Relics were severed from their original powers
- Living resonance implements lost their song
- Gate maps became useless
- Forgotten powers woke beneath sealed dimensions
- Some Gates fused multiple Realm laws into unstable anomaly zones
- Earth's Gates cracked open, allowing Hollow incursions into neutral territory

## Gate States

### Stable Gates

Stable Gates still connect known Realms. They are valuable, dangerous, and politically contested.

### Ruin Gates

Ruin Gates open into dead cities, broken temples, abandoned halls, and collapsed civilizations.

### Distortion Gates

Distortion Gates warp anything passing through them. These create strange mission mechanics and unstable terrain. Avatars passing through Distortion Gates may experience temporary ability mutations.

### Sealed Gates

Sealed Gates were closed for a reason. Their locks may hold campaign secrets, ancient bosses, or threats that predate the current Realms.

### Wound Gates

Wound Gates are damaged reality scars. They can generate Gateborn entities and mutate battlefield rules mid-mission. Wound Gates on Earth are the primary entry points for Hollow invasions.

## Campaign and Dungeon Use

Every major campaign arc should involve at least one Mythos Gate with a clear identity:

- What Realm law shaped it?
- What event damaged or awakened it?
- What does it want, if anything?
- What does it distort?
- What threat is emerging through it?
- Which Deity has lore resonance with it?
- What power modifier does it apply to the Avatar?
- Which rooms, branches, shrines, traps, treasure spaces, elite encounters, and boss chambers logically belong beyond it?

## Dungeon-Crawler Integration

The latest gameplay format treats Mythos Gate routes as dungeon corridors into dangerous Realm locations, sealed ruins, sacred domains, corrupted regions, monster territories, and boss arenas. Players project their Avatar through the Gate, explore the route, and fight through the dungeon. This does not replace existing Gate lore. It is a tactical framing for exploring the already-established Gate roads, ruin gates, distortion gates, sealed gates, and wound gates.
