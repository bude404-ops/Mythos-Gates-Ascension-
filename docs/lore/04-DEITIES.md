# 04 — Playable Deities and Avatars

## Definition

Deities are the supreme divine warforms of the Realms.

Deities are colossal extradimensional divine beings shaped by the laws, myths, symbols, materials, and powers of their home Realms. They are NOT humans in armor. They are NOT humans in costume. They are NOT machines. They are NOT ordinary fantasy units.

Each Deity is a divine being whose anatomy integrates the laws and materials of its Realm directly into its living form. Their physiology transcends mortal design — they are the originals that ancient humans encountered and later worshipped as gods.

Each Deity must feel powerful enough to have become the source of an ancient god myth.

## The Avatar System

Players do not control Deities directly. Players control **Avatars** — divine projections of a Deity's power sent through the Mythos Gates to fight, explore, and reclaim lost territory.

An Avatar is not the Deity. An Avatar is a fraction of the Deity's will, identity, and power, projected across dimensional boundaries through the Gate. The Deity remains in its home Realm, safe and immortal, channeling power through the Avatar from across the divide.

### Why Avatars?

- **Progression makes sense** — You level up the Avatar, not the god. Belief and Influence are the resources that strengthen the Avatar's connection to its Deity.
- **Death is meaningful but not final** — If the Avatar falls, the Deity survives. The player respawns at their Deity's domain with a penalty.
- **Earth is neutral ground** — all campaign missions take place in each faction's mythological homeland on ancient Earth. No faction advantage or penalty.
- **Scale is maintained** — Avatars are still colossal (god-scale), even though they are not the full Deity. The power difference is shown through aura intensity, ability charges, and cooldowns, not physical size.

## Divine Anatomy Rule

Avatars project the divine anatomy of their source Deity. The visual design must communicate:

- Clearly non-human, extradimensional divine presence
- Realm-integrated anatomy — the Realm's laws and materials are part of their living body, not worn as accessories
- Colossal scale that makes ancient architecture look small
- Individual mythological identity tied to their source deity
- No halos, no text, no readable symbols — pure divine form
- Aura intensity is full on Earth — the Gate's energy amplifies divine power equally for all factions

## Avatar Scale Rule (Option A)

Avatars remain **GOD-SCALE** everywhere regardless of Realm. The power difference between Realms is shown through:

- **Aura intensity** — Full radiance on Earth; the Gate amplifies all divine energy equally
- **Ability charges** — More charges available in home Realm, fewer in enemy Realm
- **Cooldowns** — Standard on Earth; no realm modifiers in campaign
- **NOT physical size** — The Avatar is always colossal

### Scale by Realm

| Realm | Avatar Size | Aura | Ability Charges | Cooldowns |
|-------|------------|------|-----------------|-----------|
| Home Realm | Full god-scale | Maximum radiance | Full charges | Shortest |
| Enemy Realm | Full god-scale | Dimmed | Reduced charges | Longer |
| Earth (Neutral) | Full god-scale | Medium | Balanced | Normal |

## Belief and Influence Progression

Avatars are powered by two resources that represent the Avatar's growing connection to its Deity:

- **Belief** — Spiritual fuel earned from followers, victories, and realm control. Spent to level Avatar core stats (HP, DE, ATK, DEF, SPD). More believers = stronger Avatar projection.
- **Influence** — Territorial power earned from completing missions, winning battles, and spreading the Deity's domain. Spent to unlock and upgrade abilities, relics, and Ascension tiers.

### Progression Flow

1. Earn Belief and Influence through gameplay (missions, combat, dungeons)
2. Spend Belief to increase Avatar level and base stats
3. Spend Influence to unlock and upgrade abilities
4. Higher Avatar level = stronger projection = more of the Deity's power channeled
5. At max level, enter Ascension tiers for endgame progression

## Avatar Death and Respawn

When an Avatar dies:
- The Deity is NOT dead — only the projection fell
- The Gate returns the divine essence to the home Realm
- Player respawns at the Deity's domain
- **Penalties:** Loss of some Belief and Influence, cooldown before re-deployment
- **Preserved:** Avatar level, unlocked abilities, relics, and progression

Death is a setback, not an ending. The Deity endures. The Avatar is rebuilt.



## Lore-to-Gameplay Rule

Lore defines extradimensional divine power identity.
Divine power identity defines battlefield role.
Battlefield role defines stat budget and counterplay.

No god or goddess should exist only because they look cool. Every playable deity needs a source mythology, civilization role, divine domain, battlefield purpose, dungeon-crawler function, and unique visual identity.

## Single-Avatar Rule

Current format:

- 4 deities per playable Realm (28 total)
- Player selects one Deity and controls its Avatar
- 5 active ability slots per Avatar (Passive + 3 Active + 1 Ultimate)
- No playable non-Avatar entries
- No normal units

The design goal is encounter-specific single-Avatar mastery. The player projects one Avatar through the Mythos Gates, enters dangerous dungeon spaces, and fights multiple mythological enemies, elites, guardians, rival divine forces, mini-bosses, and bosses.
