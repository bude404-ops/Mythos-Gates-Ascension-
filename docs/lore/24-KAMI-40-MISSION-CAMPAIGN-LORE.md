# Kami Full Faction Campaign Lore — v0.5.0

The Kami campaign begins when the Torii-Moon Gate reopens across the Shrine-Path Archipelago. The Kami do not treat the Gate as an enemy. They treat it as a boundary that has lost its manners.

The campaign begins on the torii road where the Sacred Kingdoms once maintained the threshold between spirit, nature, memory, and storm. The Gate's reopening breaks that threshold. Reflections move before their owners. Torii roads appear in the wrong order. Paper seals rewrite themselves after being read. The Kami deities enter not to conquer the Gate but to restore the boundary — because a boundary that is not respected will kill both sides it once protected.

Across five chapters, the campaign moves from threshold breach through mirror corruption, foxfire oath-breaker insurgence, and a final confrontation where the Gate creates a false reflection of the chosen deity and asks the player to prove which version is real.

Elite missions reinterpret these events as sharper precision trials. The Gate has learned from the Normal campaign. It changes path order mid-mission, rewrites seals faster, and forces the deity to win without relying on any path that worked before.

The finale leaves the Gate corrected but not closed. The Kami win the right to restore the boundary, not the right to seal it permanently.

## Campaign Spine

### Chapter 1 — Torii-Moon Threshold
The Gate reopens on the Shrine-Path Archipelago. Torii gates that once stood in sacred order now flicker between positions. The first torii is inverted — it marks a boundary that leads inward instead of outward. Sayo Shrine-Cartographer maps the impossible routes by refusing to trust any path that flatters the traveler. The chosen deity must bow before crossing — not for humility, but because the Gate records posture before intent. Hollow foxfire doubles emerge from the inverted torii, wearing the deity's own shape.

**Mission Focus:** Threshold breach. Establish that boundaries are alive. First Hollow contact through mirror-fox spirits. The discipline of precision under disorientation.

### Chapter 2 — False Reflection Road
The Gate's distortion creates reflection anomalies. The chosen deity's reflection begins moving independently — it takes actions before the deity decides to take them. The reflection is not hostile at first; it is simply ahead. But the Hollow uses the reflection gap to insert Hollow mask-wearers who look like the deity, act like the deity, and attack the shrine nodes the deity is trying to protect. The deity must fight their own reflection's shadow while protecting the shrine law that says only the original may cross.

**Mission Focus:** Reflection corruption. The deity fights Hollow copies of themselves. The question: what makes you real if your reflection acts first?

### Chapter 3 — The Seal That Rewrote Itself
Deep in the torii road, a paper seal from before the Collapse rewrites itself. It originally said KEEP OUT. It now says COME IN. Someone — or something — has inverted the seal's intent. The Kami deities know that a seal is not decoration; it is a contract between the boundary and the person who crosses it. An inverted seal is not a broken seal; it is a trap that uses consent as the weapon. The chosen deity must navigate a road where every seal may have been rewritten, and the Hollow shrine-masks use the rewritten seals as legal authority to attack.

**Mission Focus:** Seal inversion. Boundaries that invite what they should repel. The deity must read every seal before crossing — and some seals fight back against being read.

### Chapter 4 — Mirror Lake Oath
The torii road opens onto Mirror Lake — a sacred boundary where the Kami once tested whether a visitor's reflection matched their intent. The lake now shows the chosen deity as someone they have never been. It is not a lie; it is a possibility the Gate has extrapolated. The Foxfire Oath-Breakers — spirits who broke their own boundary vows and were consumed by the Hollow — use the false reflection to claim the deity has already broken an oath. The deity must fight through the legal and spiritual consequences of a crime they have not committed.

**Mission Focus:** Mirror Lake trial. False accusation through extrapolated reflection. Oath-breaker spirits as enemies. The deity must prove intent, not just action.

### Chapter 5 — The False Reflection Kami
The Gate's final room is a perfect mirror. Inside it stands the False Reflection Kami — a Hollow entity that has consumed enough Kami boundary law to build a complete copy of the chosen deity. It has the deity's abilities, their appearance, and their shrine authority. The only difference: it has no boundary. It does not respect thresholds. It crosses without asking, touches without permission, and destroys what it cannot own. The deity must defeat a perfect copy of themselves whose only weakness is that it does not understand why boundaries matter.

**Mission Focus:** Final confrontation. The deity vs their Hollow copy. Boss: The False Reflection Kami. Victory condition: the deity must win by using the boundary itself — not by overpowering the copy, but by proving that the threshold still has authority.

## Elite Campaign

Elite missions reinterpret the campaign as a sharper precision trial. The Gate has learned from the Normal run:
- Reflections now act two turns ahead instead of one
- Rewritten seals change mid-mission after being read
- Mirror Lake shows the deity's actual Elite campaign tactics reflected back
- The False Reflection Kami gains the deity's Elite build and loadout

The finale proves that precision was not accidental. The deity must win the boundary test twice — once against the Gate, once against the Gate's memory of their precision.
