# Kami: The Shrine-Path Archipelago
**Codex ID:** TG-REALM-004  
**Director:** Lore Director  
**Faction:** Kami  
**Gate:** The Spirit-Moon Gate  
**Status:** Canon Expanded
## Realm Thesis
Kami Titans do not conquer disorder; they purify it, name it, bind it, and let the world breathe again.
## Central Conflict
Their secrecy protects sacred balance but makes allies fear what they cannot see.
## Realm Atmosphere
The The Shrine-Path Archipelago is defined by ritual precision, sacred fog, moonlit shrines, storm calligraphy, and masks hiding divine intent. Its visual language is torii gates, lacquer red seals, moonwater, ink lightning, shrine armor, ghostly blossom storms.

## Realm Hazards
- Possessed paper wards.
- mist corridors that rearrange loyalty.
- spirit tides that drown memory.

## Campaign Hooks
- Chart a moving shrine path.
- seal an Oni pact without breaking canon law.
- hunt a Hollow hiding inside a sacred festival.
