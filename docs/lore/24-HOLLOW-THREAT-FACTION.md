# The Hollow — Threat Faction

The Hollow is a hostile threat faction, not an eighth playable Realm civilization. It is the major antagonist pressure across all seven playable faction campaigns.

## Canon Rule

The Hollow is not one of the seven playable Realm civilizations. It is a cross-campaign enemy faction, event pressure system, and Gate-born anti-civilization used in missions, Elite remixes, raids, ascension modifiers, and seasonal events.

The Hollow is what exists in the spaces BETWEEN the Realms — the void that the Mythos Gates were built to keep sealed. When the Ascension Collapse shattered the old Gate network, the Hollow began seeping through the cracks.

## Origin

Before the Realms were divided, there was the Gate — the original boundary between existence and absence. The Realms were carved FROM something, and that something left a wound. The Hollow is what grows in that wound. It is not evil in the way the Infernal Dominion is ambitious, or the Empyrean is ordered. The Hollow is EROSION. It is what happens when reality forgets itself.

The Hollow does not conquer. It erases. Where a deity radiates divine light, the Hollow absorbs it. Where a deity IS alive — living marble, living iron, living wood — the Hollow is the ABSENCE of life. Where a deity has identity, memory, and law, the Hollow has void, fracture, and silence.

The Hollow imitates the shapes of cultures it has consumed — not to mock them, but because it has no form of its own. It wears dead civilizations like hollow masks. Every Hollow creature is a negative-space echo of something that was real.

## Philosophy

Erasure is not death; erasure is ownership without memory.

The Hollow does not hate the Realms. It does not seek power, territory, or worship. It seeks SILENCE. It wants the Realms to forget they ever existed. A Hollowed deity does not die — it simply stops being, and the space where it stood becomes nothing.

## Visual Identity

The Hollow is the ANTI-thesis of the divine:
- Where deities RADIATE, the Hollow ABSORBS — light does not escape a Hollow creature, it falls INTO them
- Where deities are ALIVE (living marble, living iron, living wood, living light), the Hollow is ABSENCE — charcoal-black void-stuff, negative space, missing form
- Where deities have FACES, the Hollow has MASKS — incomplete, broken, or entirely absent. A Hollow face is a wound where a face should be
- Where deities have AURAS, the Hollow has ANTI-AURAS — white fracture cracks that emit cold nothing instead of warm radiance
- Where deities have LAW (Ma'at, oaths, contracts), the Hollow has ERASURE — broken Gate stone embedded in bodies, fragments of the original boundary that failed

Visual language:
- Charcoal-black void-stuff body (NOT metal, NOT stone, NOT flesh — pure absence given form)
- White fracture cracks (like broken glass or reality cracking — NOT glowing energy, NOT fire, but the inverse: cold white lines where light dies)
- Incomplete or broken masks (faces that are partially formed, half-erased, or simply missing)
- Broken Gate stone embedded in bodies (fragments of the original Mythos Gates — arch pieces, keystone shards, threshold rubble fused into the Hollow form)
- Negative-space wounds (holes in the body that show NOT emptiness but ACTIVE NOTHING — the void that consumes)
- Torn banners of erased civilizations (shreds of cloth bearing sigils that are being erased in real-time, fading to blank)
- Anti-light seams (where a deity would have golden light bleeding through armor, the Hollow has seams of absolute black that drink surrounding light)
- Ash falling UPWARD (gravity inverts around Hollow creatures — ash and debris drift toward the sky, not the ground)

The Hollow should NEVER look like a playable deity. It may imitate armor, banners, wings, crowns, or shrine forms — but every silhouette must remain INCOMPLETE, FRACTURED, MASKLESS, or visibly Gate-corrupted. A Hollow creature that looks complete is WRONG.

## Combat Philosophy

Pressure morale, isolate Titans, corrupt objectives, punish overextension, duplicate forgotten battlefield roles, and turn terrain memory into hostile rules.

The Hollow fights by making the Realms forget their own laws. It corrupts objective markers, silences abilities, steals cooldowns, and turns the terrain against the player. It does not fight with skill — it fights by making the player's own skill meaningless.

## Faction Passive

Hollowing: when a Hollow unit damages an isolated deity or corrupted objective, nearby Hollow units gain pressure, silence, or memory-drain effects until cleansed.

## Encounter Identity

- **Swarmers** (Wretch, Choirling) punish isolation and crowd the battlefield
- **Archers** (Knell Archer) apply ranged pressure from distance
- **Anchors** (Gate Anchor) corrupt objectives and change battlefield rules
- **Commanders** (Sunless Magistrate, Chain Auditor) make lesser Hollow units dangerous
- **Specialists** (Oath-Eater, Memory Thief, Mirror Saint, Root Parasite) attack specific game mechanics
- **Imitators** (Banner Maw, Shrine-Mask, Laurel Butcher) wear the shapes of consumed civilizations
- **Siege** (World-Wound Behemoth) turns map terrain into hostile history
- **Event Bosses** are Gate wounds given bodies

## Campaign Function

The Hollow gives designers a universal enemy pressure layer. It can invade Aten Ra judgment zones, Asgardian oath bridges, Olympian trial courts, Kami shrine paths, Tuatha groves, Empyrean edict halls, and Infernal contract grounds without rewriting those factions as mixed chosen deity deployments.

## Visual Rule

Hollow enemies should NEVER look like playable deities. They may imitate armor, banners, wings, crowns, or shrine forms, but every silhouette must remain incomplete, fractured, negative-space, maskless, or visibly Gate-corrupted. A Hollow creature is a WOUND in reality wearing a shape it stole from something that was real.

## Counterplay

- Stay inside one-active-deity support ranges
- Cleanse corrupted objectives
- Break anchors before commanders arrive
- Do not let Wretches mark isolated deities
- Prioritize Standard Bearers and Choir units before swarms

## Connection to the Mythos Gates

The Hollow and the Gates are the same wound viewed from different sides. The Gates were built to keep the void OUT. When the Gates broke during the Ascension Collapse, the void came IN. Every Hollow creature carries fragments of the broken Gate in its body — not as armor, but as the scar of its own creation. The World-Wound Behemoth is the largest Gate fracture given form. The Gate Anchor is a living piece of the broken threshold. The Hollow is what the Gates were holding back.

The seven playable factions are fighting over the broken machinery of reality. The Hollow IS the broken machinery. It is not a faction you fight — it is the consequence of the war itself.
