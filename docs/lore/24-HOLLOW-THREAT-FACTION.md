# The Hollow — Threat Faction

The Hollow is a hostile threat faction, not an eighth playable Realm civilization.

## Canon Rule

The Hollow is not one of the seven playable Realm civilizations. It is a cross-campaign enemy faction, event pressure system, and Gate-born anti-civilization used in missions, Elite remixes, raids, ascension modifiers, and seasonal events.

## Philosophy

Erasure is not death; erasure is ownership without memory.

## Campaign Function

The Hollow gives designers a universal enemy pressure layer. It can invade Aten Ra judgment zones, Asgardian oath bridges, Olympian trial courts, Kami shrine paths, Tuatha groves, Empyrean edict halls, and Infernal contract grounds without rewriting those factions as mixed strike forces.

## Encounter Identity

- Swarms punish isolation.
- Anchors corrupt objectives.
- Commanders make lesser Hollow units dangerous.
- Memory thieves attack cooldowns, summons, and mission rules.
- Siege creatures turn map terrain into hostile history.
- Event bosses are Gate wounds given bodies.

## Visual Rule

Hollow enemies should never look like playable Titans. They may imitate armor, banners, wings, crowns, or shrine forms, but every silhouette must remain incomplete, fractured, negative-space, maskless, or visibly Gate-corrupted.
