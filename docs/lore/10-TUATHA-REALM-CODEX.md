# Tuatha: The Root-Crowned Green Below
**Codex ID:** MG-REALM-005  
**Director:** Lore Director  
**Faction:** Tuatha  
**Gate:** The Thorn-Memory Gate  
**Status:** Canon Expanded
## Realm Thesis
Tuatha deities protect continuity. They know worlds die when stories lose roots, names, and obligations to the dead.
## Central Conflict
Their memory is power, but it can become vengeance when old wounds refuse to compost.
## Realm Atmosphere
The Root-Crowned Green Below is defined by ancient roots, living songs, moon pools, stone scripts, barrow dawns, and nature remembering every betrayal. Its visual language is emerald forests, silver moon pools, Celtic knotwork, antler crowns, moss-covered megaliths, golden dawn mist.

- **The Moon-Grove Rootlabyrinth** — a campaign-facing location for Tuatha stories.

- **The Thorn-Song Court** — a campaign-facing location for Tuatha stories.
- **The Barrow Dawn** — a campaign-facing location for Tuatha stories.
- **The Stone Script Hill** — a campaign-facing location for Tuatha stories.

## Realm Hazards
- Roots that bind liars.
- fae bargains hidden in birdsong.
- barrow lights that lure deities into old wars.

## Campaign Hooks
- Recover a stolen ancestral name.
- bargain with the dead without waking a forgotten army.
- stop root-memory from consuming a living city.
