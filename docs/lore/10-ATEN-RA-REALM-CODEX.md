# Aten Ra: The Solar Dominion of Khepra
**Codex ID:** TG-REALM-001  
**Director:** Lore Director  
**Faction:** Aten Ra  
**Gate:** The Sun-Scale Gate  
**Status:** Canon Expanded
## Realm Thesis
Aten Ra believes the Ascension Collapse happened because divine power lost its measuring rod. They seek to restore order before every Realm becomes a wound.
## Central Conflict
Their law can heal chaos, but it can also harden into tyranny when other Realms refuse the Solar Measure.
## Realm Atmosphere
The The Solar Dominion of Khepra is defined by sacred law, geometric light, desert judgment, river-memory, and impossible noon. Its visual language is white-gold solar discs, obsidian-blue shadows, colossal pylons, papyrus circuitry, radiant court armor.
## Major Landmarks
- **The Noon Court** — a campaign-facing location for Aten Ra stories.
- **The Glass Horizon Necropolis** — a campaign-facing location for Aten Ra stories.
- **The River of Verdicts** — a campaign-facing location for Aten Ra stories.
- **The Pyramid Engines** — a campaign-facing location for Aten Ra stories.
- **The Black-Sun Archive** — a campaign-facing location for Aten Ra stories.

## Realm Hazards
- Judgment rays that expose hidden lies.
- mirage courts that repeat failed choices.
- sand that remembers bloodlines.

## Campaign Hooks
- Recover the broken Measure from Hollow-thieves.
- judge a Titan accused of opening a forbidden Gate.
- seal a Black-Sun eclipse before it crowns the wrong king.
