# Infernal Mission Dialogue Set — v1.0.0

## Overview

Full chapter-specific dialogue for all 40 Infernal missions (20 Normal + 20 Elite), following the 5-chapter campaign arc. Each mission includes 6 beats: Mission Intro, Enemy Introduction, Mid-Battle, Victory, Defeat, and Codex.

## NPC Witness

**Varak Chain-Magistrate** — speaks during enemy introductions, mid-battle pressure, and codex entries. Guides, warns, and interprets without commanding the deity.

## Tone

lawful pressure, contract clarity. Deities speak with restraint and faction-specific authority. No modern slang, no comedic one-liners, no quips after danger.

## Chapter Arc

### Chapter 1 — Black-Iron Threshold
**Focus:** Contract system, every action has cost, Ash Market

### Chapter 2 — The Debt Ledger
**Focus:** Compounding debt, inherited obligations, efficiency as defense

### Chapter 3 — Ash Market
**Focus:** Trade debt onto enemies, Chain-Bound Colossus, every trade creates a chain

### Chapter 4 — Execution Sigil Crisis
**Focus:** Own law weaponized, rewrite contracts under fire

### Chapter 5 — The Volcanic Glass Magistrate (Boss)
**Focus:** Court authority consumed, founding contract eaten, debt void because creditor does not exist

## Beat Structure

Each mission includes:
- **Mission Intro** — Divine Voice sets the scene and stakes
- **Enemy Introduction** — NPC witness describes the threat
- **Mid-Battle** — NPC witness provides tactical pressure
- **Victory** — Divine Voice acknowledges the win
- **Defeat** — Divine Voice acknowledges the loss
- **Codex** — NPC witness provides lore context

## Dialogue Rules

- Deities speak rarely and with restraint
- NPC witnesses guide, warn, and pressure without commanding
- Threats speak with dread or law, never jokes
- No modern slang or comedic one-liners
- Elite dialogue acknowledges pressure without repeating Normal
- Every line reveals worldview, objective, or danger
- All dialogue is canon-aligned with the campaign lore
