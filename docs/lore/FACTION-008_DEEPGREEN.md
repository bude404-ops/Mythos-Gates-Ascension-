## Identity
- **THE DEEPGREEN** — the oldest of the eight pantheons. The mountain does not hurry, and neither do they.
- Creed: **"The mountain does not hurry."**
- Faith philosophy: THE LONG WATCH — faith grows with persistence. Enduring without retreating, holding a line without breaking, staying unbroken through a phase: patience is literal power in this realm.
- Realm: **THE DEEPGREEN** — old-growth so old the Hollow haven't finished eating it. Mist valleys, cedar switchbacks, the Return Run river, and the Sleeping Ridge volcano at the heart. The Gate opens in fog — and the oldest faction answers slowest, because they have watched faster things fail.
- [CONFIRMED] The Deepgreen is the oldest faction. [RUMOR] They were the first to see the Gates arrive. [SECRET: they remember what the Gates were BEFORE they were Gates.]

## The Four Gods (solo-first: all heals/buffs self-only; roles: Warrior M, Caster M, Archer F, Assassin F)
1. **MAWKRETH, THE SLEEPING RIDGE** (M, Warrior) — the volcano god who wakes when the Gate opens. Weapon: **THE QUARRY**, a cedar-and-basalt maul. Active 1: Ridgebreaker (ground-slam AOE that raises stone walls — the mountain reshapes the field). Active 2: Basalt Skin (self-buff: damage reduction while standing ground — the mountain does not dodge). Ultimate: **THE RIDGE WAKES** — he erupts: shockwave plus lava vents across the field in a pattern you choose.
2. **KOLWETH, THE GRAY VEIL** (M, Caster) — god of the coastal mist that carries what the coast wants forgotten. Weapon: **THE FOGCLOAK STAVE**. Active 1: Veilcast (fog wall — blocks enemy sight and projectiles). Active 2: Mistake (deploy a mirage double of yourself — enemies strike the wrong you). Ultimate: **WHITEOUT** — the field fills; enemies wander blind while you strike from anywhere.
3. **SELMARA, THE RETURNING RIVER** (F, Archer) — goddess of the run that always comes back. Weapon: **THE RUNBOW** — arrows that behave like salmon. Active 1: Upstream (a shot that pierces forward, then RETURNS along the same line — every enemy is hit twice, going and coming). Active 2: Shallows (deployable river-snare — enemies caught wade in slow-motion). Ultimate: **THE SPAWNING RUN** — a torrent of arrows floods the river-channel you draw; everything in it is struck and swept.
4. **THUVEKA, THE SILENT WING** (F, Assassin) — the owl goddess; the witness that hunts unheard. Weapon: paired silent talons **THE QUIET**. Active 1: Featherfall (glide-strike from above with no warning cue). Active 2: Night-Eye (see marked enemies through terrain — owl-sight; also REVEALS Hollow that hide as terrain). Ultimate: **THE QUIET HUNT** — 5 seconds completely undetectable; every strike from the silence is a critical.

## NPC / Gate / Route (all new)
- NPC: **TOLMA THE MIST-WEIGHER** — she measures the fog and knows what it hides. She has kept the Gate since before the Collapse, and she never answers a question the same way twice — the mist changes the questions. Gate: **THE DEEPGREEN GATE** (opens in fog). Route: **THE CEDAR-MIST DESCENT**.

## Hollow Variant: THE GRAYFALLEN
Spore-born Hollow that eat COLOR first, then SOUND, then memory, then shape — the forest grays out around them in stages. Deepened AI identity: they hollow out old-growth and WEAR IT — tree-walker husks indistinguishable from standing timber until they move. Their tell: no birdsong near them. (Thuveka's Night-Eye is the realm's designed counter — the Silent Wing reveals the silent woods.)

## Dungeons (new architecture)
- **The Cedar-Mist Switchbacks** — the descent path that rearranges behind you; patience beats speed — rushing spawns Grayfallen, waiting starves them
- **The Salmon Gates** — the Hollow have dammed the Return Run; free the river WITHOUT breaking it (constraint-combat: the river is your ally, collateral damage punishes)
- **The Sleeping Ridge Caldera** — the volcanic vents at the Gate's root; the floor is a timing puzzle of breath and eruption

## Bosses

It cannot be killed, only OUTLASTED: an endurance boss — you win by surviving its phases (the realm's faith philosophy as the final exam). [SECRET-tier: it didn't just eat a tree.
