# 00 — Canon Lock

## Current Canon Status

Mythos Gates is a seven-Realm, Titan-only mythological tactical RPG.

The current canon is built around:

- 8 storyline faction layers: 7 playable mythological Realms plus 1 non-playable campaign threat layer
- 7 playable mythological Realms
- 4 deities per playable Realm
- one-active-Avatar combat deployments (Avatar is projection of chosen Deity)
- No playable non-Titan entries in the current format
- No non-Titan battle patterns in the current format
- No sci-fi
- No futuristic technology
- No futuristic craft
- No guns
- No Mycocide or fungal civilization content

## Core Rule

The beings within Mythos Gates are extradimensional divine civilizations and entities that existed beyond Earth's normal reality. They are not aliens, not extraterrestrials, and do not come from planets, galaxies, or outer space.

These beings are divine — they are the originals that ancient humans encountered and later worshipped as gods. They are NOT humans in costume. They are NOT humans with magical effects. They are colossal, clearly non-human, extradimensional divine beings whose anatomy, physiology, and presence transcend mortal form.

Ancient humans encountered traces, manifestations, gateways, visions, and interactions with these extradimensional divine beings. Those encounters became the foundation of Earth mythology.

Earth myths are human interpretations of real extradimensional divine beings. The Titans are the source — human gods are the echo.

## Gameplay Canon Lock

Each playable Realm has 4 deities. The eighth storyline layer is the campaign threat layer and does not add a normal playable roster.

Players control one god or goddess at a time from the Deity Roster or Divine Collection.

There are:

- 0 playable non-Titan entries per faction
- 0 non-Titan battle patterns per faction
- 9 collectible Titans per faction
- 5 active deity slots in a chosen Titan

The design goal is encounter-specific single-Avatar mastery: the player projects an Avatar of their chosen Deity and battles multiple enemies through Divine Abilities, Divine Stats, Divine Gear, Divine Level, and Divine Ascension. The Deity remains safe in its home Realm.

## Removed Canon

Mycocide is permanently removed.

Do not use:

- Mycocide
- Fungal civilization
- Spore civilization
- Fungal campaign faction
- Mycelium faction

This concept should not appear in canon, codex, campaign, art, characters, units, enemies, Directors, memory, or future generation.

## Legacy Content — Reapers

Reapers are deprecated legacy content from the older format. They are removed from current canon and should not appear in new content.

## Naming

The game is titled "Mythos Gates: Ascension."

The dimensional gateways are called "Mythos Gates."

The colossal warforms are called "Titans."

The extradimensional civilization-domains are called "Realms."
