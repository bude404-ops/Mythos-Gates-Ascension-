# FACTION 08 CONCEPT — THE DEEPGREEN (PNW-inspired, fully original)
Status: PROPOSAL — AWAITING BUD E404 APPROVAL | IDs: MG-FACTION-008

## Core Identity
The youngest pantheon — gods born from the First Rainfall on the world's first forest. Their creed: **"The Rain Remembers."** Water holds memory; these gods are the world's memory made divine. Faith = being remembered. Mortals carve rainglyphs into living trees so deeds are never forgotten.

Aesthetics (PNW-inspired, no real-world cultures referenced): rain, fog, moss, giant cedars, black rivers, volcanic peaks, storm-wracked coast.

## Divine Realm: The Deepgreen Halls
Continent-scale cedars whose canopy carries permanent storm-fronts. Roots descend into a black inland sea. Rain-glyph causeways, halls grown inside living trunks, basalt river-weirs, fog-lantern bridges.

## Mythos Gate: The Fogspire
A hollow basalt spire wreathed in fog that never lifts. Opens only while rain falls — which is why no other faction, not even the gods, has studied it closely. (Mystery hook: what happens inside a Gate nobody has mapped?)

## The Four Gods (2M/2F — Warrior/Archer/Caster/Assassin)
1. **Bhorram, the Standing Storm** (M, Warrior) — god of thunderheads, landslides, stubborn endurance. Patient as bedrock, terrifying when roused. Signature weapon: **Skyquern**, a stone maul; each impact calls a downpour. Ultimate: Landslide Verdict — splits the ground.
2. **Sylweth, the Mist-Touched** (F, Archer) — goddess of fogbanks and silent hunts. Weapon: **Whisperveil**, a driftwood longbow. Ability: fog-screen (briefly untargetable), piercing rain-shot. Ultimate: Veilbreak — every arrow the fog has hidden falls at once.
3. **Undraya, the Tidewright** (F, Caster) — goddess of rivers, lakes, and the long rain. Weapon: **The Undertow Coil**, riverstone staff wound with salvaged ship-cable. Controls currents that drag enemies. Ultimate: The Long Rain — the storm becomes her domain.
4. **Vossk, the Rootveil** (M, Assassin) — god of the dark under-canopy; what moves unseen beneath roots. Weapons: paired hook-blades **Thornjaw**. Ability: root-network blink (emerges anywhere moss grows), spore poison. Ultimate: The Canopy Closes — the great trees' shadow falls and he strikes from it.

## Hollow Variant: The Blightwet
Not a recolor — a real inversion: fog held inside a thin drowned skin. They drink color, then names, then memory itself. Where they pass, mortals forget their gods' faces — corrupted zones mechanically DRAIN FAITH. The Deepgreen's war is personal: the Hollow are erasing them from memory.
