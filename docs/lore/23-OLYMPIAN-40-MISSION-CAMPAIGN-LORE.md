# Olympian Full Faction Campaign Lore — v0.5.0


## Avatar System

Players do not control the deities directly. Each player channels an **Avatar** — a divine projection of their chosen deity. The avatar carries the deity's power, faith triggers, and faction bonuses into combat, but the deity itself remains protected in its domain.

- **Progression**: Avatars grow stronger through **Belief** (faction-core faith) and **Influence** (deity-specific faith path), earned by completing missions and conquering realms.
- **Death**: If an avatar falls, it respawns at the deity's domain. The deity is unharmed. Progression is preserved with a 10-20% Belief/Influence penalty.
- **Scale**: Avatars maintain god-scale proportions in all realms — they tower over buildings, landscapes, and mortal structures.
- **Solo Combat**: Each avatar fights alone. No allies, no healing companions. Faith triggers are self-only.
- **Realm Advantage**: Avatars fight with full power in their deity's home realm. In enemy realms, they suffer penalties. Earth is neutral ground.


The Olympian campaign begins when the Laurel-Sky Gate reopens across high air, marble platforms, and old trial arenas. Olympus does not treat the Gate as a crisis to be managed. It treats the Gate as a contest that must be won.

The campaign begins on the broken trial road where the Celestial Heights once proved their worth through witnessed excellence. The Gate reopens that road — and demands proof again. The Olympian avatars enter not to save the Realm but to demonstrate that their power deserves to exist.

Across five chapters, the campaign moves from trial reactivation through oracle corruption, Forgotten banner revelations, and a final confrontation where the Gate itself becomes the judge. Excellence is sacred only when witnessed. But what happens when the Gate is the only witness — and it is broken?

Elite missions reinterpret these events as sharper tactical trials. The trial has learned from the Normal campaign. It adjusts victory conditions, corrupts oracle vapor, and turns the Avatar's own excellence into a weapon used against them. The Gate has learned the player's belief patterns.

The finale leaves the Gate contested but not claimed. Olympus wins the right to compete, not the right to declare victory.


## CROSS-REALM CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE

**Core Direction**: The first mission of each campaign takes place in the player's HOME REALM (The Celestial Heights of Olympus). All subsequent missions have the avatar venturing OUT into OTHER REALMS, establishing cross-realm traversal as the core campaign experience. The trial dungeon (MAP-000) remains on Contested Earth as the universal entry point.

### Realm Visit Order

**Chapter 1 — Laurel-Sky Threshold**: M01: Home realm (Olympus) — establishing avatar power. M02-M04: The Storm-Rooted Aesir Holds — rival pantheon

**Chapter 2 — Order vs Chaos**: M05-M08: The Infernal Dominion — civilization vs corruption

**Chapter 3 — New vs Old**: M09-M12: The Solar Dominion of Khepra — marble vs sandstone

**Chapter 4 — Civilized vs Wild**: M13-M16: Avalora (The Living Wilds) — city vs forest

**Chapter 5 — Spirit and Light**: M17-M18: The Sacred Kingdoms of Takamagahara. M19-M20: The Radiant Hierarchies — spirit balance and divine hierarchy

### Realm Advantage System

- **Home Realm**: Avatar fights with full power — no penalties
- **Enemy Realms**: Avatar suffers power penalties — the realm's native deities hold advantage
- **Earth (Neutral)**: No advantages or penalties — used only for the trial dungeon (MAP-000)

### Lore Justification

The Gate connects all realms. When a deity's avatar manifests through the Gate, the first step anchors them in their home realm's power. But the Hollow threat and the Gate's instability require avatars to venture beyond their borders — into the realms of rival deities, enemy factions, and hostile territory. Each realm visited tests the avatar's adaptability and earns Belief through surviving foreign divine power.

## Campaign Spine

### Chapter 1 — Laurel-Sky Threshold
The Gate opens across high air. Marble platforms materialize. The old trial road activates for the first time since the Collapse. Lyrion Laurel-Envoy arrives as witness — without a witness, Olympian power has no legal standing. The avatar must prove the right to enter by surviving the Gate's first test: a trial of skill against Hollow-corrupted laurel champions who wear the shapes of forgotten Olympian victors.

**Mission Focus:** Trial reactivation. Establish that excellence requires witness. First Hollow contact through corrupted trial remnants.

### Chapter 2 — Oracle Vapor Bleed
The Gate's stability leaks oracle fumes into the trial road. These vapors show possible futures — but broken futures, where every victory leads to a worse defeat. The oracle corruption does not lie; it simply shows only the paths where excellence becomes hubris. The avatar must push through visions of their own failure while the Hollow uses the oracle bleed to predict and counter every move.

**Mission Focus:** Oracle corruption. Visions of failure. Hollow adaptation to Avatar tactics. The question: is excellence still excellence if you've seen it fail?

### Chapter 3 — The Banner That Should Not Exist
Deep in the trial road, a Forgotten banner rises from pre-Collapse stone. It carries the memory of a war between Olympian and Asgardian Realms that both sides deny starting. The banner does not attack — it testifies. It shows both civilizations at their worst and asks the chosen deity to judge. The danger is that pride and excellence may be pushed into reenacting the old catastrophe.

**Mission Focus:** Forgotten remnant. Cross-faction tension. The trial becomes a legal proceeding. The deity must fight while the banner replays old war memory as terrain pressure.

### Chapter 4 — Thunder Tribunal Fracture
The Gate's oracle vapor and the Forgotten banner's testimony create a fracture point. The trial road splits into competing jurisdictions — Olympian trial law and Asgardian oath law both claim authority over the same Gate space. The avatar must navigate a battlefield where the rules themselves are contested. Hollow victory parasites feed on the confusion, imitating both legal systems to drain meaning from both.

**Mission Focus:** Rule collision. Two legal systems fighting over live Gate space. Hollow exploits the confusion. The deity must choose which law to honor — or break both.

### Chapter 5 — The Laurel-Sky Verdict
The Gate itself becomes the final trial. It has absorbed the oracle vapors, the Forgotten testimony, and the legal fracture. It now judges the chosen deity by a single question: does your excellence serve anything beyond itself? The Thunder Tribunal Usurper — a Hollow entity wearing the shape of a forgotten Olympian-Asgardian judge — manifests as the final boss. It has no opinion. It simply weighs.

**Mission Focus:** Final trial. The Gate as judge. The deity must prove that excellence has purpose beyond ego. Boss: The Thunder Tribunal Usurper.

## Elite Campaign

Elite missions reinterpret the entire campaign as a sharper trial. The Gate has learned from the Normal run:
- Oracle vapors now show the Avatar's actual past tactics and counter them
- The Forgotten banner testifies faster, leaving less recovery time
- The legal fracture zones change rules mid-mission
- The Thunder Tribunal Usurper gains additional phases based on the Avatar's Normal campaign performance

The finale proves that victory was not accidental. The deity must win the trial twice — once against the Gate, once against the Gate's memory of being beaten.


## Cross-Realm Campaign Structure

**New Direction**: The avatar ventures OUT of their home realm after the first mission. Only Mission 1 takes place in The Celestial Heights of Olympus. All subsequent missions send the avatar into enemy realms.

### Realm Journey:
- **M01 (Chapter 1, Mission 1)**: HOME — The Celestial Heights of Olympus (avatar establishes power, full strength, no realm penalty)
- **M02-M04 (Chapter 1 remainder)**: The Storm-Rooted Aesir Holds — first venture into enemy territory
- **M05-M08 (Chapter 2)**: The Solar Dominion of Khepra — deeper into foreign realms
- **M09-M12 (Chapter 3)**: Avalora — cross-realm escalation
- **M13-M16 (Chapter 4)**: The Sacred Kingdoms of Takamagahara — advanced enemy territory
- **M17-M18 (Chapter 5)**: The Radiant Hierarchies — penultimate realm
- **M19-M20 (Chapter 5)**: The Infernal Dominion — final enemy realm, campaign climax

### Realm Penalties:
- **Home Realm (M01)**: Avatar fights at full power. No penalties. Belief generation is standard.
- **Enemy Realms (M02-M20)**: Avatar suffers realm penalty — reduced power, hostile terrain, environmental hazards from the enemy faction's lore. Belief generation is increased (harder fights = more faith earned).
- **Earth (Trial MAP-000)**: Neutral ground. No faction advantage or penalty. Used only for the initial trial when selecting a deity.

### Narrative Logic:
The Gate opens paths to all realms, not just the avatar's home. The Hollow threat destabilizes all seven realms simultaneously. The chosen deity sends their avatar out to secure alignment in foreign realms — each realm's Gate fragment must be defended or conquered. The avatar grows stronger through the Belief earned in these hostile campaigns, but fights at a disadvantage in enemy territory. Only the first mission is fought on home ground — everything after is the avatar proving their power in someone else's domain.
