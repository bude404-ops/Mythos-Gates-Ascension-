# Olympian Full Faction Campaign Lore — v0.5.0

The Olympian campaign begins when the Laurel-Sky Gate reopens across high air, marble platforms, and old trial arenas. Olympus does not treat the Gate as a crisis to be managed. It treats the Gate as a contest that must be won.

The campaign begins on the broken trial road where the Celestial Heights once proved their worth through witnessed excellence. The Gate reopens that road — and demands proof again. The Olympian deities enter not to save the Realm but to demonstrate that their power deserves to exist.

Across five chapters, the campaign moves from trial reactivation through oracle corruption, Forgotten banner revelations, and a final confrontation where the Gate itself becomes the judge. Excellence is sacred only when witnessed. But what happens when the Gate is the only witness — and it is broken?

Elite missions reinterpret these events as sharper tactical trials. The trial has learned from the Normal campaign. It adjusts victory conditions, corrupts oracle vapor, and turns the player's own excellence into a weapon used against them.

The finale leaves the Gate contested but not claimed. Olympus wins the right to compete, not the right to declare victory.

## Campaign Spine

### Chapter 1 — Laurel-Sky Threshold
The Gate opens across high air. Marble platforms materialize. The old trial road activates for the first time since the Collapse. Lyrion Laurel-Envoy arrives as witness — without a witness, Olympian power has no legal standing. The chosen deity must prove the right to enter by surviving the Gate's first test: a trial of skill against Hollow-corrupted laurel champions who wear the shapes of forgotten Olympian victors.

**Mission Focus:** Trial reactivation. Establish that excellence requires witness. First Hollow contact through corrupted trial remnants.

### Chapter 2 — Oracle Vapor Bleed
The Gate's stability leaks oracle fumes into the trial road. These vapors show possible futures — but broken futures, where every victory leads to a worse defeat. The oracle corruption does not lie; it simply shows only the paths where excellence becomes hubris. The chosen deity must push through visions of their own failure while the Hollow uses the oracle bleed to predict and counter every move.

**Mission Focus:** Oracle corruption. Visions of failure. Hollow adaptation to player tactics. The question: is excellence still excellence if you've seen it fail?

### Chapter 3 — The Banner That Should Not Exist
Deep in the trial road, a Forgotten banner rises from pre-Collapse stone. It carries the memory of a war between Olympian and Asgardian Realms that both sides deny starting. The banner does not attack — it testifies. It shows both civilizations at their worst and asks the chosen deity to judge. The danger is that pride and excellence may be pushed into reenacting the old catastrophe.

**Mission Focus:** Forgotten remnant. Cross-faction tension. The trial becomes a legal proceeding. The deity must fight while the banner replays old war memory as terrain pressure.

### Chapter 4 — Thunder Tribunal Fracture
The Gate's oracle vapor and the Forgotten banner's testimony create a fracture point. The trial road splits into competing jurisdictions — Olympian trial law and Asgardian oath law both claim authority over the same Gate space. The chosen deity must navigate a battlefield where the rules themselves are contested. Hollow victory parasites feed on the confusion, imitating both legal systems to drain meaning from both.

**Mission Focus:** Rule collision. Two legal systems fighting over live Gate space. Hollow exploits the confusion. The deity must choose which law to honor — or break both.

### Chapter 5 — The Laurel-Sky Verdict
The Gate itself becomes the final trial. It has absorbed the oracle vapors, the Forgotten testimony, and the legal fracture. It now judges the chosen deity by a single question: does your excellence serve anything beyond itself? The Thunder Tribunal Usurper — a Hollow entity wearing the shape of a forgotten Olympian-Asgardian judge — manifests as the final boss. It has no opinion. It simply weighs.

**Mission Focus:** Final trial. The Gate as judge. The deity must prove that excellence has purpose beyond ego. Boss: The Thunder Tribunal Usurper.

## Elite Campaign

Elite missions reinterpret the entire campaign as a sharper trial. The Gate has learned from the Normal run:
- Oracle vapors now show the player's actual past tactics and counter them
- The Forgotten banner testifies faster, leaving less recovery time
- The legal fracture zones change rules mid-mission
- The Thunder Tribunal Usurper gains additional phases based on the player's Normal campaign performance

The finale proves that victory was not accidental. The deity must win the trial twice — once against the Gate, once against the Gate's memory of being beaten.
