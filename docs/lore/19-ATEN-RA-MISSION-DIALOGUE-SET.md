# Aten Ra Mission Dialogue Set — v1.0.0

## Overview

Full chapter-specific dialogue for all 40 Aten Ra missions (20 Normal + 20 Elite), following the 5-chapter campaign arc. Each mission includes 6 beats: Mission Intro, Enemy Introduction, Mid-Battle, Victory, Defeat, and Codex.

## NPC Witness

**Mesha Gate-Scribe** — speaks during enemy introductions, mid-battle pressure, and codex entries. Guides, warns, and interprets without commanding the deity.

## Tone

restrained mythic tactical. Deities speak with restraint and faction-specific authority. No modern slang, no comedic one-liners, no quips after danger.

## Chapter Arc

### Chapter 1 — Sun-Scale Threshold
**Focus:** Gate as judgment site, Ma'at as terrain pressure, Hollow sun-husks

### Chapter 2 — The Ma'at Scale Hall
**Focus:** Every action weighed by the Gate, scale-wraiths drain heaviest ability

### Chapter 3 — The Khepri Dawn Loop
**Focus:** No repeating tactics, scarab-mirrors counter known patterns

### Chapter 4 — The Hidden-Sun Archive
**Focus:** Archive defense, false histories, Gate predates deities revelation

### Chapter 5 — The Misweighed Dawn Judge (Boss)
**Focus:** Verdicts as physical weight, prove Judge's Ma'at is broken

## Beat Structure

Each mission includes:
- **Mission Intro** — Divine Voice sets the scene and stakes
- **Enemy Introduction** — NPC witness describes the threat
- **Mid-Battle** — NPC witness provides tactical pressure
- **Victory** — Divine Voice acknowledges the win
- **Defeat** — Divine Voice acknowledges the loss
- **Codex** — NPC witness provides lore context

## Dialogue Rules

- Deities speak rarely and with restraint
- NPC witnesses guide, warn, and pressure without commanding
- Threats speak with dread or law, never jokes
- No modern slang or comedic one-liners
- Elite dialogue acknowledges pressure without repeating Normal
- Every line reveals worldview, objective, or danger
- All dialogue is canon-aligned with the campaign lore
