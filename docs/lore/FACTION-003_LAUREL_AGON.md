## Identity
- Was: "Olympian" → Now: **THE LAUREL AGON** — the pantheon of witnessed excellence. Their realm is one long contest.
- Creed: **"Excellence unwitnessed is nothing."**
- Faith philosophy: WITNESS — faith grows when deeds are seen; fame is literal power here. Deeds without a witness generate nothing — a core loop that makes their whole realm performance-shaped.
- Realm: **THE SKY COLONNADE** — arena-terraces floating above storm-fronts, marble colonnades hung with ten thousand victory-laurels, each one a witnessed deed.

2. **THERISSA THE SILENT LAUREL** (F, Archer — was Artemis) — goddess of the hunt at the arena's unlit edge; her arrows arrive before applause. Weapon: **THE MOONFLETCH BOW**. Active 1: Laurel Snare (arrow plants a binding laurel-root). Active 2: Hunter's Pace (move speed stacks per hit). Ultimate: **THE WITNESS SHOT** — one perfect arrow; if it kills, the whole arena witnesses — faith surge to you.
3. **AETHROKLES THE SKY-VERDICT** (M, Caster — was Zeus) — god of the storm that attends great deeds; thunder is the audience. Weapon: **THE VERDICT SPEAR**, a spear-staff of storm-glass. Active 1: Ostracize (target exiled from ally buffs — strips synergies). Active 2: Sky-Writ (lightning mark, damage over time). Ultimate: **THE UNCONTESTED SKY** — the storm flattens the arena: enemy cover destroyed, enemy casting silenced.
4. **SOPHRONA THE CALCULATED** (F, Assassin — was Athena) — goddess of the perfect plan; her strikes arrive exactly where the plan says. Weapon: paired short-blades **AXIOM & ANACLEXIS**. Active 1: Premise (marks a target with a logical condition). Active 2: Conclusion (blink to any marked target and strike). Ultimate: **THE PERFECT PLAN** — 6 seconds of auto-targeting weak points; kills in it restore your faith.
