# 08 — Faction Belief Configuration

## Overview

Each faction's belief system has four configuration layers:

1. **Passive Belief Bonus** — +10% Belief from all victories (shared by all factions)
2. **Faction Bonus Trigger** — +5% Belief from a faction-specific condition (unique per faction)
3. **Tiered Faction Buffs** — Permanent stat buffs unlocked at Avatar levels 10, 30, 50
4. **Pantheon Resonance** — Server-wide buff activated when all 4 deities in a faction reach max belief

---

## 1. Aten Ra — "Ma'at Devotion"

### Passive
+10% Belief from all victories

### Faction Bonus Trigger: "Balance Maintained"
+5% Belief when winning an encounter without dropping below 50% HP.
**Lore:** Ma'at requires balance. Victories that maintain divine composure earn extra devotion — the people see a god who enforces order without being shaken.

### Tiered Faction Buffs

| Tier | Unlock Level | Buff Name | Effect | Lore |
|------|-------------|-----------|--------|------|
| 1 | Avatar Level 10 | Solar Initiation | +5% DEF when above 50% HP | The Avatar's skin hardens with solar plating — the faithful see the sun's armor manifest. |
| 2 | Avatar Level 30 | Solar Barque | +5% ATK when above 50% HP | The Avatar channels the solar barque's power — every strike carries the weight of the sun. |
| 3 | Avatar Level 50 | Ma'at Incarnate | +5% to ALL stats when above 50% HP | The Avatar becomes the living embodiment of cosmic balance. Above the threshold, they are unbreakable. |

**At full stack (all 3 tiers):** +5% DEF + 5% ATK + 5% ALL = +15% DEF, +10% ATK, +5% HP/DE/SPD when above 50% HP

### Pantheon Resonance: "Khepra Eternal"
**Activation:** All 4 Aten Ra deities reach Avatar Level 50
**Effect:** All Aten Ra Avatars gain +10% Belief earned (stacks with passive = +20% total faction bonus)
**Server Effect:** A global solar event — all players see the Aten Ra faction banner glow gold for 24 hours

### Balance Notes

- Every faction has the same total potential Belief earn rate (145 max per win with Pantheon Resonance)
- The stat buffs differ by faction but total to the same +15% across all stats at max tier
- Infernal Dominion stacks ATK harder (+15%) but has no defensive tier buffs — trade-off for offensive power
- Empyrean stacks DEF harder (+10%) — trade-off for defensive power
- Kami stacks DE regeneration — trade-off for ability sustain
- Tuatha stacks HP regeneration — trade-off for survival sustain
- All Pantheon Resonances give the same +10% Belief — no faction has an advantage in belief farming
