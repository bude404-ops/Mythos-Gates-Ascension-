Weapon: **THE AEGIS LENS**, a focusing shield — every block throws a blinding shockwave-ring. Active 1: Flareguard (block + blinding ring). Active 2: Ignited Resolve (self-buff: attack speed). Ultimate: **THE FINAL BEACON** — plants a light-standard; for 8 seconds he cannot die and all enemies are drawn to it.
2. **THRANIEL, THE FAR LUMEN** (M, Archer — was Gabriel) — light travels ahead of everything; his photons arrive before the arrow. Weapon: **THE PRISM BOW** — arrows of focused light that refract around cover. Active 1: Far Glint (long-range mark that GLOWS through walls). Active 2: Prismatic Volley (one arrow splits into a spectrum of element-typed shots). Ultimate: **SUNRISE VERDICT** — he names the strongest enemy on the field; light converges on it from every angle. You cannot outrun dawn.
3. **SOTHIEL, THE WHITE LEDGER** (F, Caster — was Sophia) — goddess of the archive of light: everything light has ever touched is recorded, and she reads the record. Weapon: **THE LEDGER OF LIGHTS**, a floating tome of captured luminance. Active 1: Refraction (bends an enemy's last ability back at it — wisdom as returned light). Active 2: Annotated Ray (precision beam; bonus vs marked targets). Ultimate: **THE REVERSAL** — the light reverses direction: every enemy buff inverts into a debuff.
4. **TASHARETH, THE ALREADY-THERE** (F, Assassin — was Shekinah) — light is in the room before you enter it; so is she. Weapon: paired vault-glass hook-blades **THE WAITING**. Active 1: Threshold Glow (place a light-node anywhere — swap to it, photon-quick). Active 2: Irradiate (infuse an enemy for 3 seconds; your next strike from within it is a guaranteed crit). Ultimate: **EVERYWHERE AT ONCE** — for 5 seconds every light-node acts as her: simultaneous strikes from all thresholds.
