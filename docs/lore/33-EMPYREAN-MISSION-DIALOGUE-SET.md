# Empyrean Mission Dialogue Set — v1.0.0

## Overview

Full chapter-specific dialogue for all 40 Empyrean missions (20 Normal + 20 Elite), following the 5-chapter campaign arc. Each mission includes 6 beats: Mission Intro, Enemy Introduction, Mid-Battle, Victory, Defeat, and Codex.

## NPC Witness

**Celiane White-Vault Witness** — speaks during enemy introductions, mid-battle pressure, and codex entries. Guides, warns, and interprets without commanding the deity.

## Tone

formal luminance, quiet danger. Deities speak with restraint and faction-specific authority. No modern slang, no comedic one-liners, no quips after danger.

## Chapter Arc

### Chapter 1 — Choir-Vault Threshold
**Focus:** Frequency site, choir husks silence, coherence not compliance

### Chapter 2 — Memory-Burn Radiance
**Focus:** Erases combat memory, instinct vs emptiness

### Chapter 3 — The Dissonant Choir
**Focus:** Split choir, protect survivors, stop Broken Heaven Engine

### Chapter 4 — Glass Stair War
**Focus:** Vertical ascent, frequency-matching, final step requires own frequency

### Chapter 5 — The Silent Seraph of Broken Praise (Boss)
**Focus:** Silences harmony, dissonance is the weapon, imperfection makes song alive

## Beat Structure

Each mission includes:
- **Mission Intro** — Divine Voice sets the scene and stakes
- **Enemy Introduction** — NPC witness describes the threat
- **Mid-Battle** — NPC witness provides tactical pressure
- **Victory** — Divine Voice acknowledges the win
- **Defeat** — Divine Voice acknowledges the loss
- **Codex** — NPC witness provides lore context

## Dialogue Rules

- Deities speak rarely and with restraint
- NPC witnesses guide, warn, and pressure without commanding
- Threats speak with dread or law, never jokes
- No modern slang or comedic one-liners
- Elite dialogue acknowledges pressure without repeating Normal
- Every line reveals worldview, objective, or danger
- All dialogue is canon-aligned with the campaign lore
