# Deity Unlock System — Mythos Gates: Ascension

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Locked:** Aug 31, 2026  
**Design:** Solo progression — start with 1, unlock 27 more through campaign, mastery, and currency  

---

## Starting Deity Selection

New players choose **1 starter deity** from 7 options — one per faction. All starters are **Warriors** (simplest role, most forgiving mechanics) to ease new players into the game.

| Faction | Starter Deity | Why They're the Starter |
|---------|---------------|------------------------|
| Aten Ra | Aten Ra | Straightforward melee + shield. Solar Edict rewards aggression. Easy to learn. |
| Asgardian | Thor | Hammer throws are intuitive. Rune Oath rewards taking hits — forgiving for beginners. |
| Olympian | Athena | Spear + shield is classic. Aegis Favor gives free cooldowns — noob-friendly. |
| Kami | Amaterasu | Mirror shield + blade. Spirit Seal rewards dodging — teaches core mechanics. |
| Tuatha | Dagda | Big club, simple rotations. Geas Bloom rewards kills — snowball feels great early. |
| Empyrean | Michael | Holy sword, straightforward leap + stun. Choir Edict builds from ability use — natural flow. |
| Infernal | Asmodeus | Big glaive, burn stacks. Blood Contract rewards taking damage — aggressive but sustainable. |

**Starter Bonuses:**
- First deity is free
- Starter gets +20% Belief gain for first 10 levels (faster onboarding)
- Starter gets a unique "First Ascension" cosmetic skin at Level 10

## Three Unlock Methods

After choosing your starter, the remaining 27 deities unlock through 3 methods:

### Method 1: Campaign Progression (12 Deities)

Complete campaign chapters to unlock deities. Each Act introduces new factions and their deities.

| Act | Chapters | Factions Introduced | Deities Unlocked |
|-----|----------|---------------------|------------------|
| Act I | Ch 1-2 | (Starter already chosen) | — |
| Act I | Ch 3 | Aten Ra faction | Sutekh, Iset, Amunet |
| Act I | Ch 4 | Asgardian faction | Odin, Skadi, Freyja |
| Act I | Ch 5 | Olympian faction | Zeus, Artemis, Ares |
| Act I | Ch 6 | Act I Boss | — |
| Act II | Ch 7-8 | Tuatha faction | Brigid, Morrígan, Lugh |
| Act II | Ch 9-10 | Kami faction | Tsukuyomi, Susanoo, Izanami |
| Act II | Ch 11-12 | Empyrean faction | Gabriel, Raphael, Jophiel |
| Act II | Ch 13 | Act II Boss | — |
| Act III | Ch 14-15 | Infernal Dominion | Lucifer, Lilith, Naamah |
| Act III | Ch 16-19 | Final Arc + World Bosses | — |

**How it works:** When you complete a chapter that introduces a faction, all 3 remaining deities from that faction unlock automatically. This gives you 12 deities just from playing the campaign.

**Example:** You start with Thor (Asgardian). Complete Ch 3 → Aten Ra deities unlock (Sutekh, Iset, Amunet). Complete Ch 5 → Olympian deities unlock (Zeus, Artemis, Ares). Etc.

### Method 2: Faction Mastery (9 Deities)

The 3 remaining deities in your starter's faction unlock through mastery — no campaign required.

| Requirement | Unlock |
|-------------|--------|
| Starter reaches Level 10 | 2nd deity in faction unlocks |
| Starter reaches Level 20 | 3rd deity in faction unlocks |
| Starter reaches Level 30 | 4th deity in faction unlocks |

**How it works:** As you level your starter deity, you earn "Faction Reputation" with that faction. At milestones (10, 20, 30), the next deity in that faction unlocks automatically.

**Example:** You picked Thor (Asgardian). At Level 10, Odin unlocks. At Level 20, Skadi unlocks. At Level 30, Freyja unlocks. Now you have all 4 Asgardian deities.

### Method 3: Gate Shard Purchase (6 Deities)

The remaining 6 deities can be unlocked early by spending Gate Shards — the game's premium currency earned from routes, bosses, and daily challenges.

| Deity | Gate Shard Cost | Unlock Condition |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| Any deity locked behind Act II | 500 Gate Shards | Can purchase early — unlocks before reaching Act II |
| Any deity locked behind Act III | 1,000 Gate Shards | Can purchase early — unlocks before reaching Act III |
| Any deity from your starter faction | 250 Gate Shards | Can purchase early instead of waiting for mastery levels |

**Gate Shard Earning Rates:**

| Source | Gate Shards |
|--------|-------------|
| Route completion | 20-50 (by difficulty) |
| Boss defeat | 50-100 |
| Daily challenge | 75 |
| Achievement | 100-500 |
| Weekly challenge | 200 |

**How it works:** Want to play Susanoo before reaching Act II Ch 9? Pay 500 Gate Shards. Want Lilith before Act III? Pay 1,000. This lets dedicated players unlock any deity early through grinding, without paying real money.

## Why Unlock More Deities?

### 1. Different Playstyles
Each deity has a unique role, weapon, abilities, and faction buff. Playing Athena (tactical shield tank) feels completely different from playing Ares (berserker assassin). With 28 deities × 4 weapon skill tree paths = **112 unique builds**, every deity offers a fresh experience.

### 2. Faction Advantage System
Certain dungeons and bosses are easier with specific factions:

| Faction | Strong Against | Weak Against |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Aten Ra | Hollow enemies, shadow types | Infernal Dominion bosses |
| Asgardian | Ice/frost enemies, swarms | Kami spirit types |
| Olympian | Armored enemies, boss fights | Tuatha wild types |
| Kami | Spirit/demon types, CC-heavy | Empyrean holy types |
| Tuatha | Beast enemies, nature bosses | Asgardian storm types |
| Empyrean | Infernal Dominion, shadow bosses | Hollow void types |
| Infernal | Empyrean enemies, holy types | Aten Ra solar types |

Having a diverse roster means you always have the right deity for the job.

### 3. Route & Dungeon Variety
- Each faction has unique dungeon routes with faction-specific terrain, hazards, and lore
- Earth dungeons are neutral territory where any deity works
- Realm Raids are limited-time events that favor specific factions
- Playing all 7 factions gives access to all dungeon content

### 4. Lore & Codex Completion
- Each deity has a unique backstory, chapters, and lore ties
- The Codex unlocks entries as you play each deity
- Completing all 28 deity lore arcs unlocks a hidden ending
- Lore Shards from each deity's routes unlock realm codex entries

### 5. Achievements & Mastery
- "Pantheon Master" — unlock all 28 deities
- "Divine Council" — max level all 4 deities in one faction
- "Ascendant" — reach Level 30 with any deity
- "All-Father" — reach Level 30 with all 28 deities
- "Weapon Master" — complete all 4 weapon skill tree paths for one deity

### 6. Faction Buff Synergy
- Each faction's buff synergizes differently with the weapon skill tree
- Path A (Aggressive) + Aten Ra's Solar Edict = maximum burst
- Path B (Defensive) + Asgardian's Rune Oath = unkillable tank
- Unlocking multiple deities lets you test all 7 faction buffs with different weapon builds

## Pantheon Resonance (Endgame)

When you max-level all 4 deities in a faction, you activate **Pantheon Resonance** — a permanent server-wide buff for that faction:

| Faction | Pantheon Resonance Name | Effect |
|---------|------------------------|--------|
| Aten Ra | Khepra Eternal | +5% ALL stats for all Aten Ra deities |
| Asgardian | World-Tree Ascendant | +5% ALL stats for all Asgardian deities |
| Olympian | Olympian Council | +5% ALL stats for all Olympian deities |
| Kami | Sacred Kingdom Eternal | +5% ALL stats for all Kami deities |
| Tuatha | Avalora Sovereign | +5% ALL stats for all Tuatha deities |
| Empyrean | Choir Eternal | +5% ALL stats for all Empyrean deities |
| Infernal Dominion | Black Iron Throne | +5% ALL stats for all Infernal deities |

This incentivizes players to unlock and max-level all 4 deities in each faction — giving a clear long-term goal after the campaign.
