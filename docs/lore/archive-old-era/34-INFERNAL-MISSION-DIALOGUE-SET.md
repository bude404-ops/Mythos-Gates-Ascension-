# Infernal Dominion Mission Dialogue Set — v1.0.0


> **Avatar System**: Players channel Avatars of their chosen deity — divine projections that carry the deity's power into combat. The deity remains protected in its domain. Progression via Belief and Influence. Avatar death = respawn at deity domain with 10-20% penalty. Solo combat only. God-scale in all realms.


The dialogue set supports all 40 Infernal Dominion missions with concise mobile-friendly beats.

Each mission includes: Mission Intro, Enemy Introduction, Mid-Battle, Victory, Defeat, Codex.

## Tone Rules

- Deities speak with formal, transactional authority. Every line carries contract, cost, and the weight of explicit terms.
- Varak Chain-Magistrate is the NPC witness. He is formal, transactional, dangerous because he is often truthful. He does not command.
- Hollow enemies speak in fragments of consumed contracts — broken terms, voided clauses, not jokes.
- No modern slang. No comedic one-liners. No chosen-one flattery.
- Elite dialogue acknowledges the Normal campaign without repeating it.
- Everything has a price. Dialogue reflects this — every action is a transaction, every word is a term.
