# FACTION-002 CULTURAL DESIGN — STORMMOOT (Norse)

Lore is original; the aesthetic is authentic Norse. No historical god names — the titans are ours, the visual language is the North Atlantic.

## Cultural Source
- **Era anchor:** Migration / Viking Age North Atlantic — fjord country, longhalls, runestones, carved stave pillars.
- **Materials:** storm-iron (bog-iron aesthetic: dark, rough-hammered, rune-stamped), whalebone, walrus ivory, grey wool, seal/wolf fur, silver arm rings and torcs, oak.
- **Motifs:** Urnes-style knotwork (carved, never printed), elder-futhark-adjacent ORIGINAL rune marks (oath-marks — invented glyph set, not real futhark), ravens, falcons, storm-cloud whorls, knotted oath-braids.
- **Color world:** storm grey, cold steel blue, sea-green, dusk purple, silver — against pale cold-light godflesh seams. (Deliberately COLD palette vs F001's warm desert gold.)
