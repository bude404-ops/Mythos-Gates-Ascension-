# F001 Meridian Court — Cultural Design Doc

## The Realm: the Sun-Verdict Desert
A bronze-age desert theocracy built around solar judgment. Culture centers on **the weighing of souls at high noon** — scales, gnomon obelisks, sun-disc iconography, and verdict-glass (amber glass forged under concentrated noon light). Architecture: sandstone obelisk courts, causeways of balanced stone, horizon-wide temples.

**Cultural motifs (all F001 gear):** sun-disc scales, gnomon/obelisk lines, verdict-glass amber inlays, sun-faded lapis and bone accents, wrapped linen under bronze.

## Per-Deity Cultural Armor & Weapon Language

### Khaveth, the Measured Light (Caster M)
- **Armor:** judge's regalia — layered sun-verdict linen with bronze scale-plate over the chest forming a balance beam; obelisk-styled pauldrons; amber verdict-glass circlet
- **Weapon:** *The Amber Verdict* — a bronze beam-scale staff, pans holding twin captive suns
- **Unique silhouette:** tall vertical obelisk line, symmetrical scale head — reads as a living monument

### Djekhur, the Ashen Gale (Warrior M)
- **Armor:** storm-scarred bronze cuirass scored by horizontal sand-wind grooves; a pauldron shaped like a dune crest on his shield arm; face wrapped to the eyes in ashen linen
- **Weapon:** *Dunemaw* — a heavy crescent maul like a biting dune, haft wrapped in storm-bleached hide
- **Unique silhouette:** wide grinding wedge — a moving sandstorm front

### Shemris, the Glasswind (Archer F)
- **Armor:** seductive-divine heat-shimmer regalia — sheer sun-bleached linens over verdict-glass scale-mail that shifts like water; bare-midriff silhouettes with bronze hip-scales; veil of shimmer-spun glass
- **Weapon:** *Semblance* — a bow of tensioned glass and bronze that bends light around its limbs
- **Unique silhouette:** vertical shimmer arc, trailing mirage doubles

### Amekhet, the Sunless Eye (Assassin F)
- **Armor:** the noon-shadow look — sleek obsidian-and-bronze bodysuit styled like living shadow at high noon; sharp hip-scales and a kohl sun-mask over the eyes; wrappings ending in glass-edged greaves
- **Weapon:** *Crescent Veil* — paired sickle-daggers shaped like crescent shadows
- **Unique silhouette:** hard-edged single shadow with one absolute black accent

## Scale Rule (all F001 concepts)
Every deity concept must read 30-feet-tall: tiny mortal petitioners and obelisk courts at their feet, low-angle composition, temple doors they dwarf.
