## The Four Gods (solo-first: all heals/buffs self-only; roles: Warrior M, Archer M, Caster F, Assassin F)
1. **TOLVETH THE ROOTWARD** (M, Warrior — was Dagda) — god of the deep strength of old growth; carries the grove's weight. Weapon: **THE GROWTHCLUB**, a massive oaken club — one end shatters, the other regrows terrain (destruction/regrowth duality; walls of root rise behind the swing). Active 1: Rootslam (AOE strike + raises a root-ridge barrier). Active 2: Second Growth (self-heal from grove-sap). Ultimate: **THE FOREST RISES** — the battlefield sprouts; terrain reshapes itself around you.
2. **CAELVARIN THE EVERY-HANDED** (M, Archer — was Lugh) — god of mastery of every craft; each arrow a different discipline. Weapon: **THE LINKED DART**, a spear that splits into chained segments. Active 1: Tradeshot (each hit grants a Craft stack — swaps your next arrow type: fire/frost/shock). Active 2: Footwork (dash; next shot crits). Ultimate: **MASTER OF THE MOMENT** — for 6 seconds every skill he owns fires in sequence, all at once.
3. **VENNAITH THE UNBANKED FLAME** (F, Caster — was Brigid) — goddess of the forge-flame kept burning since before the Collapse; it has never been let die. Weapon: **THE FIRST BELLOWS**, a forge-staff. Active 1: Cinderbind (flame-roots enemies). Active 2: Rekindle (self-heal from the First Flame). Ultimate: **THE SMELTING** — a wave of forge-fire that melts armor and strips enemy buffs.
4. **CORVETH THE BATTLE-CROW** (F, Assassin — was Morrigan) — goddess of the omen; she does not predict a battle's end, she arrives at it early. Weapon: paired sickle-blades **WINTER'S REAPING**. Active 1: Crowfall (leap-strike from above; briefly untargetable in crow-form). Active 2: War-Omen (mark a target — takes more damage the lower its health). Ultimate: **THE END OF THE BATTLE** — she lands as a flock; all marked enemies take their fate-damage immediately.
