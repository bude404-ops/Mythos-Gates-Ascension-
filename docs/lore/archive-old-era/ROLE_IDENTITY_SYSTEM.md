# Role Identity System — Mythos Gates: Ascension

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Locked:** Aug 31, 2026  
**Design:** 4 roles, each with a distinct fantasy, gameplay loop, and reason to play  

---

## Design Philosophy

Every role in Mythos Gates feels fundamentally different to play. Not just "melee vs ranged" — each role has its own combat rhythm, decision-making, and "hell yeah" moments. If you enjoy one role, the others offer something the first one can't give you.

---

## 1. WARRIOR — The Unbreakable

### Fantasy
You are the wall the enemy breaks against. You don't dodge — you **absorb**. You don't run — you **advance**. Every hit you take makes you stronger. You are the deity who walks into the storm and doesn't flinch.

### Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Walk into the fight** — no kiting, no positioning games, just walk forward
2. **Block and absorb** — time your shield/block to absorb incoming hits, build faction stacks
3. **Trade blows** — take a hit, deliver a harder one, lifesteal back
4. **Control space** — your presence pushes enemies back, your zones deny territory
5. **Outlast** — when everyone else would be dead, you're still standing

### Why It's Fun
- **You never feel fragile.** Even on low HP, your armor and lifesteal keep you in the fight. The tension isn't "will I die?" — it's "can I kill them before they out-heal me?"
- **Every blocked hit is satisfying.** The screen shakes, the enemy staggers, and you counter with a heavy strike. Blocking is an active mechanic, not passive.
- **You dictate the pace.** Enemies have to deal with YOU. You don't chase — you make them come to you or walk through your zone.
- **Big hits feel BIG.** Warriors have the heaviest, slowest, most impactful basic attacks. When your 3-hit combo lands, it FEELS like a god hitting something.

### Unique Mechanics
| Mechanic | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Active Block | Tap block button to absorb incoming damage — timed blocks reflect 20% |
| Heavy Combo | 3rd basic attack is a slow, heavy strike that staggers enemies |
| Zone Control | Warrior abilities create persistent zones that deny enemy movement |
| Armor Stacking | Warriors build armor during combat — the longer the fight, the tankier they get |
| Lifesteal Recovery | Warrior self-heal scales with max HP — bigger HP pool = bigger heals |

### Weapon Skill Tree Feel
- **Path A (Aggressive):** You become a juggernaut — more damage per hit, wider cleave, 4th combo hit explosion. You're still tanky but now you HIT like a truck.
- **Path B (Defensive):** You become unkillable — shield regenerates, reflect damage increases, lifesteal on block. You can stand in a boss AoE and survive through sheer mitigation.

### Signature Moments
- **The Last Stand:** Boss is at 30%, you're at 10% HP. You pop your ultimate, gain 30% armor, and out-heal the boss's damage while finishing them off.
- **The Wall:** 6 enemies swarm you. You block, counter, zone, and slowly grind them all down without taking meaningful damage.
- **The Counter:** Boss charges a massive attack. You time your block perfectly — reflect 50% back, stunning the boss, then deliver your heaviest combo.

### Why Unlock and Play Warriors
- **Simplest to learn, deepest to master.** No positioning puzzles, no timing windows — just block, hit, repeat. But the mastery is in WHEN to block vs attack vs use abilities.
- **Best for boss fights.** Warriors excel in prolonged single-target fights where their armor stacking and lifesteal shine.
- **Best for new players.** Forgiving mechanics, high HP, easy to understand.
- **7 Warriors available:** Aten Ra, Thor, Athena, Amaterasu, Dagda, Michael, Asmodeus

## 2. CASTER — The Stormweaver

### Fantasy
You are the artillery. You don't trade blows — you **reshape the battlefield**. Every ability you cast changes the terrain, controls the enemy, or unleashes devastation from range. You are the deity who wins the fight before the enemy reaches you.

### Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Assess the field** — position at max range, identify enemy clusters
2. **Cast zone abilities** — drop AoE zones that damage, silence, or trap enemies
3. **Chain abilities** — combo abilities together for compound effects (zone → pull → detonate)
4. **Reposition** — when enemies approach, reposition to a new angle — never let them close
5. **Unleash the ultimate** — when the field is set, drop the screen-clearing nuke

### Why It's Fun
- **You're playing a different game than melee.** While Warriors are trading blows, you're playing chess — setting up zones, planning combos, controlling space 5 seconds ahead.
- **Ability combos are addictive.** Drop a silence zone → pull enemies into it → chain lightning through all of them → ultimate for the kill. The setup-and-payoff loop is deeply satisfying.
- **Screen-wide impact.** When a Caster ultimates, the whole screen changes. Lightning everywhere, the ground erupts, enemies scatter. It's the most visually spectacular role.
- **High risk, high reward.** You're squishy — if enemies get close, you're in trouble. The thrill is in staying alive through positioning and CC while dealing massive damage.

### Unique Mechanics
| Mechanic | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Ability Chaining | Using abilities in sequence creates compound effects (zone + pull + detonate) |
| Terrain Manipulation | Caster zones permanently alter the battlefield — lava, ice, silence fields |
| Longest Range | Casters have the longest ability range in the game — hit enemies before they reach you |
| CC Specialist | Casters have the most crowd control options — stun, silence, freeze, push, pull |
| Cooldown Management | Caster damage is gated by cooldowns — timing is everything |

### Weapon Skill Tree Feel
- **Path A (Aggressive):** Your abilities hit harder, zones are larger, and ability chains refund cooldown. You become a walking artillery platform — constant AoE pressure.
- **Path B (Defensive):** Your zones protect you — lava damages enemies who approach, shields absorb hits, lifesteal on ability hits. You become a self-sustaining turret.

### Signature Moments
- **The Setup:** You drop a silence zone, pull 5 enemies into it with a vortex, then chain lightning through all of them. They can't use abilities, can't escape, and take 2x damage.
- **The Nuke:** Boss is at 50% HP. You've set up your zones, your stacks are at 5, and your ultimate is ready. You cast it — the screen fills with divine storm, and the boss melts.
- **The Escape:** 3 enemies flank you. You freeze one with an ice zone, push another back with a lunar wave, and teleport to safety — all while your DoT zones finish off the first enemy.

### Why Unlock and Play Casters
- **Most strategic depth.** If you enjoy planning, setup, and combo execution, Casters offer the most complex decision-making.
- **Best for crowd control.** Casters excel against groups — their AoE and CC make them the best at clearing rooms and controlling swarms.
- **Most visually spectacular.** Caster ultimates are the biggest, flashiest abilities in the game.
- **7 Casters available:** Sutekh, Odin, Zeus, Tsukuyomi, Brigid, Gabriel, Lucifer

## 3. ARCHER — The Divine Marksman

### Fantasy
You are the hunt itself. You don't fight — you **hunt**. Every arrow marks your prey. Every step repositions you for the perfect shot. You are the deity who kills from across the battlefield and never lets the enemy close enough to touch you.

### Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Mark your target** — basic attacks apply marks/stacks that build toward bonus effects
2. **Kite and shoot** — move between every shot, never standing still, maintaining max range
3. **Stack and detonate** — build marks to 3-5 stacks, then trigger the bonus (freeze, pierce, 2x damage)
4. **Control the distance** — if an enemy gets close, use mobility/knockback to create space
5. **Rain death** — ultimate fires multiple arrows hitting all enemies on screen

### Why It's Fun
- **The rhythm is addictive.** Shoot-move-shoot-move-shoot-detonate. The Archer gameplay loop has a musical quality — you find your rhythm and the enemy can't break it.
- **Stack management is engaging.** Every arrow builds toward something. Do you detonate at 3 stacks for the safe play, or risk building to 5 for the big payoff?
- **Kiting feels like an art form.** A good Archer never gets hit. You're constantly moving, positioning, and shooting — the enemy chases but never catches you.
- **Marks create hunting scenarios.** When you mark an enemy, you WANT to chase them. It creates a predator-prey dynamic unique to Archers.

### Unique Mechanics
| Mechanic | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Mark System | Basic attacks apply stacks — at threshold, trigger bonus effect (freeze, pierce, 2x) |
| Kite-and-Shoot | Archer moves at full speed between shots — no movement penalty for attacking |
| Longest Sustained DPS | Archers have the highest damage-per-second over extended fights |
| Stealth Ambush | Several Archers have stealth abilities — set up, ambush, kill from shadow |
| Pierce Mechanics | Archer arrows can pierce through multiple enemies in a line |

### Weapon Skill Tree Feel
- **Path A (Aggressive):** Your arrows hit harder, pierce more enemies, and your marks detonate for bigger effects. You become a piercing sniper — every shot goes through the whole enemy line.
- **Path B (Defensive):** Your marks heal you, your kiting tools improve, and you gain stealth. You become an untouchable ghost — enemies can't pin you down, and every hit heals you.

### Signature Moments
- **The Hunt:** You mark a fleeing boss, chase them across the battlefield, build to 5 stacks, and detonate — a rain of arrows from above that finishes them from across the screen.
- **The Pierce Line:** 5 enemies are in a line. You draw your bow, fire one arrow — it pierces all 5, marking each one. Your next shot detonates all 5 marks simultaneously.
- **The Ghost:** Boss charges you. You stealth, reposition behind them, and fire a 2x ambush shot point-blank. They turn around — you're already gone, shooting from max range again.

### Why Unlock and Play Archers
- **Highest sustained damage.** Archers are the DPS kings — over a 20-second fight, nobody out-damages them.
- **Best for kiting enthusiasts.** If you enjoy movement-based combat where positioning is everything, Archers are the most rewarding role.
- **Best against single tough targets.** Mark stacking makes Archers devastating against bosses with high HP pools.
- **7 Archers available:** Iset, Skadi, Artemis, Susanoo, Morrígan, Raphael, Lilith

## 4. ASSASSIN — The Shadow Strike

### Fantasy
You are the kill — not the fight, the **kill**. You don't trade blows or wear enemies down. You appear, strike, and they're dead. You are the deity who ends fights in 3 seconds and spends the rest of the time looking for the next victim.

### Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Find your opening** — wait, circle, stealth, or dash to the target's weak point
2. **Burst** — unleash everything in a 2-3 second window: backstab, crit, mark detonate, execute
3. **Kill or reposition** — if the target dies, move to the next. If they survive, reset and wait for the next opening.
4. **Execute** — when enemies drop below the execute threshold (20-50% HP), finish them instantly
5. **Snowball** — each kill resets cooldowns, heals, or builds momentum — chain kills across the battlefield

### Why It's Fun
- **The burst is intoxicating.** Nothing in the game compares to the Assassin's 3-second kill window. You stealth, dash behind the enemy, backstab for 3x, crit for 2x, and execute. They're dead before they can react.
- **Every fight is a puzzle.** Assassins are squishy — you can't facetank anything. Every engagement is a puzzle: "How do I get in, kill them, and get out without taking a hit?"
- **Execute threshold creates hunting.** Once an enemy is below 30% HP, they're YOURS. The execute mechanic makes you actively hunt weakened enemies — it's predatory and thrilling.
- **Mobility feels like freedom.** Teleport, dash, fly — Assassins move like no other role. The battlefield is your playground. You go where you want, when you want.

### Unique Mechanics
| Mechanic | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Stealth | Several Assassins can go invisible — reposition, set up backstabs, escape |
| Backstab | Attacks from behind deal 2-4x damage — positioning matters enormously |
| Execute | When enemies are below a HP threshold (20-50%), abilities execute them instantly |
| Burst Window | Assassins deal 3-5x damage in a 2-3 second window, then drop to low DPS while resetting |
| Kill Reset | Kills reset cooldowns or provide heals — chain kills to snowball |

### Weapon Skill Tree Feel
- **Path A (Aggressive):** Your burst window gets bigger — 4x backstabs, lower execute thresholds, faster resets. You become a one-shot machine — if you find the opening, they're dead.
- **Path B (Defensive):** Your lifesteal and mobility increase — stealth lasts longer, teleports reset faster, every kill heals more. You become an immortal shadow — kill, heal, reset, repeat.

### Signature Moments
- **The One-Shot:** Boss is at 40% HP. You stealth, teleport behind them, backstab for 4x, detonate all marks for 3x each. The boss drops below 30% — execute triggers. Dead. 2.5 seconds.
- **The Chain Kill:** 4 enemies in a room. You kill the first — cooldowns reset. Teleport to the second — backstab, dead. Dash to the third — execute, dead. The fourth tries to run — you chase, mark, and finish. 6 seconds, 4 kills.
- **The Escape:** You're at 10% HP, surrounded. You stealth, teleport across the room, heal 15% from lifesteal, and re-enter the fight from behind. The enemy never saw you leave.

### Why Unlock and Play Assassins
- **Highest burst damage.** Nobody kills faster than an Assassin. If you want the thrill of instant kills, this is your role.
- **Most mobility.** Teleport, dash, fly, stealth — Assassins have the most movement options, making them the most dynamic role to play.
- **Best for skilled players.** Assassins reward positioning, timing, and game knowledge. A skilled Assassin is untouchable — an unskilled one dies fast. The skill ceiling is the highest of any role.
- **7 Assassins available:** Amunet, Freyja, Ares, Izanami, Lugh, Jophiel, Naamah

## Role Comparison

| Aspect | Warrior | Caster | Archer | Assassin |
|--------|---------|--------|--------|----------|
| **HP/Armor** | Highest | Medium | Low-Med | Lowest |
| **Damage Type** | Sustained melee | AoE ranged | Sustained ranged | Burst melee |
| **Range** | Short (melee) | Long (ranged) | Longest (ranged) | Short (melee) |
| **Skill Floor** | Low (easy to learn) | Medium | Medium | High (hard to learn) |
| **Skill Ceiling** | Medium | High | High | Highest |
| **Best Against** | Bosses, 1v1 | Groups, swarms | Single target, bosses | Weakened enemies, squishies |
| **Worst Against** | Ranged kiting, groups | Melee pressure, gap-close | Melee gap-close, flanks | Tanky enemies, sustained fights |
| **Combat Time** | Long (outlast) | Medium (setup + nuke) | Long (sustained DPS) | Short (burst or reset) |
| **Fantasy** | Unbreakable wall | Stormweaver | Divine marksman | Shadow strike |
| **Fun Factor** | Tanking big hits | Combo mastermind | Kiting & stacking | One-shot thrills |

## Role-Specific Content

### Warrior-Exclusive Content
- **Gauntlet Dungeons:** Wave-based survival modes where you face endless enemies. Warriors excel here because their armor stacking and lifesteal scale with fight length.
- **Boss Tank Challenges:** Special boss fights designed for sustained melee — bosses with high HP that require blocking and countering.
- **Zone Control Objectives:** Maps where you must hold territory — Warriors are the only role that can stand in a contested zone and not die.

### Caster-Exclusive Content
- **Puzzle Rooms:** Dungeon rooms where you must use terrain manipulation to solve environmental puzzles (freeze water, melt ice, create bridges).
- **Swarm Events:** Rooms with 15+ enemies — Casters are the only role with enough AoE to clear them efficiently.
- **Elemental Trials:** Bosses that require specific elemental damage types — Casters have the most diverse damage types.

### Archer-Exclusive Content
- **Moving Target Challenges:** Bosses that constantly reposition — Archers are the only role that can reliably hit and chase at the same time.
- **Sniper Routes:** Dungeon paths with long sightlines where range advantage is maximized.
- **Mark Mastery Trials:** Challenges that require stacking marks on multiple targets simultaneously — only Archers can do this.

### Assassin-Exclusive Content
- **Speed Kill Challenges:** Time-limited kills where you must defeat enemies before a timer expires — Assassins are the only role that can consistently one-shot.
- **Stealth Routes:** Dungeon paths with patrol enemies where stealth avoids detection — Assassins are the only role with reliable invisibility.
- **Execute Trials:** Challenges where enemies start at low HP and you must execute them all before they heal — only Assassins have execute mechanics.

## Why Play All 4 Roles?

### The Full Experience
Each role sees the game differently:
- **Warrior** sees the game as a test of endurance — "Can I outlast this?"
- **Caster** sees the game as a puzzle — "How do I set up the perfect combo?"
- **Archer** sees the game as a hunt — "How do I mark and kill without getting caught?"
- **Assassin** sees the game as a series of openings — "Where's my window?"

Playing all 4 roles gives you 4 completely different game experiences with the same content. The same boss fight feels like 4 different games depending on your role.

### Faction × Role = 28 Unique Experiences
Every faction has all 4 roles, and each faction-role combination plays differently:
- **Asgardian Warrior (Thor)** feels like an unstoppable Viking tank
- **Asgardian Assassin (Freyja)** feels like a Valkyrie harvesting souls
- **Infernal Caster (Lucifer)** feels like a fallen god raining hellfire
- **Infernal Archer (Lilith)** feels like a shadow hunter seducing prey

The faction buff changes HOW the role plays:
- A Kami Archer (dodge-based stacks) plays completely differently from an Asgardian Archer (damage-taken stacks)
- An Olympian Assassin (hit-based stacks) plays completely differently from an Infernal Assassin (damage-taken stacks)

### 112 Unique Builds
28 deities × 4 weapon skill tree paths = 112 distinct builds. No two builds play the same way. This means:
- You can play Aten Ra 4 different ways (Dawnbreaker, Judgment Lord, Fortress of Ma'at, Radiant Bastion)
- You can play the same role in different factions and get a different experience
- You can switch weapon paths at Level 5 and Level 20 to fine-tune your build

**The depth is the point.** Every unlock isn't just a new character — it's a new way to experience the game.
