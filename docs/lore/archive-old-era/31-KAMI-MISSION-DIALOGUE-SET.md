# Kami Mission Dialogue Set — v1.0.0


> **Avatar System**: Players channel Avatars of their chosen deity — divine projections that carry the deity's power into combat. The deity remains protected in its domain. Progression via Belief and Influence. Avatar death = respawn at deity domain with 10-20% penalty. Solo combat only. God-scale in all realms.


## Overview

Full chapter-specific dialogue for all 40 Kami missions (20 Normal + 20 Elite), following the 5-chapter campaign arc. Each mission includes 6 beats: Mission Intro, Enemy Introduction, Mid-Battle, Victory, Defeat, and Codex.

## NPC Witness

**Sayo Shrine-Cartographer** — speaks during enemy introductions, mid-battle pressure, and codex entries. Guides, warns, and interprets without commanding the deity.

## Tone

spare precision, ritual consequence. Deities speak with restraint and faction-specific authority. No modern slang, no comedic one-liners, no quips after danger.

## Chapter Arc

### Chapter 1 — Torii-Moon Threshold
**Focus:** Boundaries are alive, mirror-fox spirits copy and predict

### Chapter 2 — False Reflection Road

### Chapter 3 — The Seal That Rewrote Itself
**Focus:** Seal inversion, boundaries invite instead of repel

### Chapter 4 — Mirror Lake Oath
**Focus:** Intent trial, extrapolated false evidence from behavioral patterns

### Chapter 5 — The False Reflection Kami (Boss)
**Focus:** Perfect copy, identity is boundary, threshold decides what is real

## Beat Structure

Each mission includes:
- **Mission Intro** — Divine Voice sets the scene and stakes
- **Enemy Introduction** — NPC witness describes the threat
- **Mid-Battle** — NPC witness provides tactical pressure
- **Victory** — Divine Voice acknowledges the win
- **Defeat** — Divine Voice acknowledges the loss
- **Codex** — NPC witness provides lore context

## Dialogue Rules

- Deities speak rarely and with restraint
- NPC witnesses guide, warn, and pressure without commanding
- Threats speak with dread or law, never jokes
- No modern slang or comedic one-liners
- Elite dialogue acknowledges pressure without repeating Normal
- Every line reveals worldview, objective, or danger
- All dialogue is canon-aligned with the campaign lore
