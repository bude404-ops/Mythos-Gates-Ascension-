## THE ORIGINALITY FILTER (applies to all 7 rebuilds)
REPLACE anything traceable to real-world mythology: god names, faction names, pantheon identities, mythologies, histories, sacred symbols, NPC names borrowed from real figures.
KEEP everything already original: the 4-deity roster shape, Gate-Scribe NPC pattern, dialogue chapter structure, visual/aesthetic identity (art survives untouched), role assignments (Warrior/Archer/Caster/Assassin).

## Demo: TG-FACTION-001 "Aten" (Egyptian) → MG-FACTION-001 **"The Meridian Court"**
Aesthetic preserved: sun, desert, scales, verdict-light, eternal afternoon.

- Realm: **The Measured Noon** — a realm of unending afternoon where every soul is weighed in light.
- Gate: **The Meridian Gate** — a gate of standing light-plates; travelers pass only at the day's exact center. (Renamed from Sun-Scale Gate to fully de-couple from Aten; Mesha the Gate-Scribe renamed too — "Mesha" is a real biblical king.)
- Gate-Scribe NPC: **Mereseth the Weigh-Warden** — waits at the Gate with a scale of sunlight; "the scale waits for weight."

## Naming Convention (regional phonetic flavor — per BudE404, Sept 2)
Egyptian-flavored originals: hard kh/dj/sh/th consonants, endings -eth/-et/-is. Invented theophoric roots only — never real deity fragments (no Ra/Amen/Hor/Seth/Os/Is/Thot/Anub elements).

## The Four Gods (roles preserved, identities rebuilt)
1. Caster M (was Aten Ra): **Khaveth, the Measured Light** — god of the verdict-noon; weighs deeds in light and burns the unworthy sentence into the air. Weapon: **The Amber Verdict**, a staff whose head is a beam-scale. Ultimate: Meridian Judgement — a pillar of noonlight marks and condemns.
2. Warrior M (was Sutekh): **Djekhur, the Ashen Gale** — god of the desert's anger; the scouring storm that erases pride. Weapon: **Dunemaw**, a heavy crescent maul. Ability: sand-gale dash that strips enemy armor. Ultimate: The Erasing Wind.
3. Archer F (was Iset): **Shemris, the Glasswind** — goddess of heat-shimmer; her arrows bend light and arrive from where you are not looking. Weapon: **Semblance**, a shimmer-spun bow. Ability: mirage double (decoy). Ultimate: Mirage Volley — a dozen false suns, one true volley.
4. Assassin F (was Amunet): **Amekhet, the Sunless Eye** — goddess of the shade cast at perfect noon — the one shadow the light cannot banish. Weapons: paired sickle-daggers **Crescent Veil**. Ability: shadow-step between sun-spots. Ultimate: High Noon Eclipsed — she becomes the day's only shadow.

## Hollow Variant: The Glaresworn
Light-swimmers — Hollow that travel inside sunbeams. In Meridian territory, LIGHT is the danger: their corruption makes noonlight burn, inverting the faction's own power against players. Mechanically: light-patch hazards, safe shadow routing (assassin synergy).

## Why This Template Works
- Zero real-world references survive; every name is original and ownable.
- The aesthetic DNA (sun/scales/verdict) is 100% preserved — existing approved art remaps cleanly.
- Each god keeps a distinct combat identity tied to faction theme (light, sand, shimmer, shadow).
- Faith hook: The Meridian Court's power grows when mortals witness justice — verdicts passed openly build Faith.
