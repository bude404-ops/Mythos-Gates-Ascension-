# Aten Ra Full Faction Campaign Lore — v0.5.0

Aten Ra does not treat the reopened Gate as a monster to be shouted down. It treats the wound as a legal crisis in creation itself.

The campaign begins beneath dead stone where light should still remember its source. Three Titans enter the first threshold and learn that Sun Realm power is not raw brightness. It is order under pressure: battle pattern, movement, measured fire, and the refusal to panic when shadow gains language.

Across five chapters, the campaign moves from first battle pattern lessons to Gate confrontations that behave less like doors and more like witnesses. The Sun Realm discovers that the Gate does not merely open; it observes, records, and pressures law until weaker powers begin to imitate obedience.

Elite missions reinterpret these same events as sharper tactical trials. The wound has learned from the Normal campaign. It changes lanes, targets objectives, denies recovery, and forces the three-Titan team to prove that victory was not accidental.

The finale leaves the Gate damaged but not solved. Aten Ra wins the right to continue the war, not the right to declare it understood.
