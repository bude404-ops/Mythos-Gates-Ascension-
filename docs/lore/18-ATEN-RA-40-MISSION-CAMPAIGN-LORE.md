# Aten Ra Full Faction Campaign Lore — v0.6.9


## Avatar System

Players do not control the deities directly. Each player channels an **Avatar** — a divine projection of their chosen deity. The avatar carries the deity's power, faith triggers, and faction bonuses into combat, but the deity itself remains protected in its domain.

- **Progression**: Avatars grow stronger through **Belief** (faction-core faith) and **Influence** (deity-specific faith path), earned by completing missions and conquering realms.
- **Death**: If an avatar falls, it respawns at the deity's domain. The deity is unharmed. Progression is preserved with a 10-20% Belief/Influence penalty.
- **Scale**: Avatars maintain god-scale proportions in all realms — they tower over buildings, landscapes, and mortal structures.
- **Solo Combat**: Each avatar fights alone. No allies, no healing companions. Faith triggers are self-only.
- **Realm Advantage**: Avatars fight with full power in their deity's home realm. In enemy realms, they suffer penalties. Earth is neutral ground.


Aten Ra does not treat the reopened Gate as a monster to be shouted down. It treats the wound as a legal crisis in creation itself.

The campaign begins beneath dead stone where light should still remember its source. The player's Avatar — a divine projection of their chosen Deity — enters the first threshold and learns that Solar Dominion power is not raw brightness. It is order under pressure: divine judgment, measured radiance, sacred law, and the refusal to panic when shadow gains language.

Across five chapters, the campaign moves from first divine judgment lessons to Gate confrontations that behave less like doors and more like witnesses. The Solar Dominion of Khepra discovers that the Mythos Gate does not merely open; it observes, records, and pressures law until weaker powers begin to imitate obedience.

Aten-Ra anchors the campaign as the noon authority. The Avatar channels Aten-Ra's solar judgment through belief-fueled combat — the living solar judgment that forces chaos to declare its weight. Sutekh feeds the Gate's light through Khepri renewal, converting wounds into dawn. Iset controls the threshold where shadow meets dawn, deciding what may cross. Amunet's prism verdict splits hidden corruption open. Sutekh storms the lanes where order cannot reach. Iset restores what the Hollow empties. Karnu sees from the horizon and strikes before enemies arrive. Maahes guards the gate with lion fury. Amunet moves through shadow, erasing what should not exist.

Elite missions reinterpret these same events as sharper tactical trials. The wound has learned from the Normal campaign. It changes lanes, targets objectives, denies recovery, and forces the player's avatar to prove that victory was not accidental.

The finale leaves the Gate damaged but not solved. Aten Ra wins the right to continue the war, not the right to declare it understood.
