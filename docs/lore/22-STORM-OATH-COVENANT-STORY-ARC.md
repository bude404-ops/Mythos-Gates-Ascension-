# Storm-Oath Covenant Story Arc

The Asgardian campaign begins when the Storm-Oath Bridge answers the reopened Gates with thunder instead of prayer. Valhalla does not treat courage as mood or spectacle. Courage is a vow, and a vow has weight.

The Gate tries to harvest that weight. Every oath spoken near the bridge becomes measurable pressure: iron warms, lightning follows old names, and forgotten banners rise where broken promises once fell.

## Core Rule

Oath is responsibility, not fuel. When the Gate attempts to turn courage into a resource, the Asgardian answer is covenant: shared risk, chosen endurance, and counterattack only when the strike matters.

## Campaign Shape

1. Oath Under Thunder — introduce oath-stones, counterpressure, and the bridge as a living war record.
2. The Banner That Forgot Its King — confront erased command memory without restoring forbidden civilization names.
3. Marble Law, Iron Oath — let Olympian trial law test Asgardian duty without enabling free faction mixing.
4. The Bridge Remembers Blood — defend the living while the bridge replays old dead vows as terrain pressure.
5. Covenant at the Thunder Gate — deny the Gate ownership of courage and convert oath into protection.

## Future Hooks

- Elite banners can return as optional pressure.
- Olympian diplomacy can branch through trial terms.
- Ascension can reuse Covenant Stand as a high-risk modifier.
- Raids can use oath-shield objectives against ancient bosses.
