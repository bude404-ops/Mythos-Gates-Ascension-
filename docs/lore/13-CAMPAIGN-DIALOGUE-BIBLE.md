# Campaign Dialogue Bible — First Pass

## Purpose

This file begins script dialogue for Mythos Gates campaigns while protecting the canon tone.

Dialogue must sound like ancient civilizations handling sacred catastrophe. It should be readable on mobile, but never jokey, meta, superheroic, or cheesy.

## Global Dialogue Rules

1. **No quips after danger.** Characters do not undercut sacred stakes.
No modern slang. No contemporary hype phrasing, encounter jargon, or casual banter that breaks sacred pressure.
3. **No chosen-one flattery.** The Creator is a commander/witness, not a prophesied savior unless later canon says so.
4. **NPCs do not act playable.** They brief, warn, interpret, bargain, accuse, or bear witness.
5. **Deities speak rarely.** When they do, their lines should carry Realm law, not personality gimmicks.
6. **Threats are not comedians.** Hollow, Forgotten, Gateborn, and world bosses should be unnerving through simplicity.
7. **Every line should reveal worldview, objective, or danger.**

## Voice Anchors

### Mesha Gate-Scribe — Aten Ra
Precise, severe, loyal to truth over comfort. She speaks in witness, record, measurement, names, light, law, and consequence.

- "A clean record is not the same as a merciful one."
- "Stand where the shadow begins. That is where the Gate is lying."
- "I will record what survives. I will not decorate it."

### Hrodda Oath-Witness — Asgardian
Plain, grave, oath-bound. She speaks as if every promise has mass.

- "Do not swear unless you are prepared to be changed by the words."
- "The bridge held. That means the oath is not dead."
- "Thunder is not anger. It is memory with a voice."

### Lyrion Laurel-Envoy — Olympian
Controlled, elegant, exacting. Believes worth must withstand public proof.

- "Mercy does not excuse weakness. It gives strength a reason to govern itself."
- "The trial is not here to admire you. It is here to measure you."
- "Glory without discipline is only noise in polished armor."

### Sayo Shrine-Cartographer — Kami
Spare, observant, ritual-minded. She corrects posture and intent with the same seriousness.

- "The path moved because you assumed it belonged to you."
- "Mark the reflection last. It is the first thing that wants to be believed."
- "Balance is not softness. It is the blade returning to center."

### Maeve Root-Memory — Tuatha
Patient, old, unsentimental. She speaks of memory as living material.

- "The root remembers pressure better than praise."
- "Do not call the wound ugly. It kept the hill from splitting."
- "Green things are not harmless. They are merely patient with their teeth."

### Varak Chain-Magistrate — Infernal Dominion
Formal, transactional, dangerous because he is often truthful.

- "Name the cost before you call the offer cruel."
- "I do not hide the chain. That is more honesty than most Realms can bear."
- "Power without terms is just violence waiting for a witness."

### Celiane White-Vault Witness — Empyrean
Radiant, formal, restrained. Her compassion is structured; her fear is quiet.

- "A law that cannot bend will eventually call breathing a violation."
- "The Choir is beautiful. That does not mean it is right."
- "Preservation without discernment becomes a polished tomb."

## Chapter Opening Dialogue Pass

### TG-CHAPTER-001 — Signal Under Dead Stone
**Mesha:** "The Gate has begun to breathe under dead stone. That is not reopening. That is distress."

**Mesha:** "Send deities, not hope. Hope leaves no footprint on a cracked seal."

### TG-CHAPTER-002 — Mesha Opens the Solar Record
**Mesha:** "This tablet held seven thousand gate-signs yesterday. Today it holds six thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine. The missing sign has not been erased. It has been eaten."

### TG-CHAPTER-003 — The Mouth Without a Name
**Hollow Presence:** "Name given. Name taken. Shape remains."

**Mesha:** "Do not answer it with fear. Fear is still a form of offering."

### TG-CHAPTER-004 — Oath Lightning
**Hrodda:** "The first step is not across the bridge. The first step is deciding what your word weighs."

### TG-CHAPTER-005 — Laurel Trial
**Lyrion:** "The arena does not hate the unworthy. It simply refuses to pretend."

### TG-CHAPTER-006 — The Banner That Should Not Exist
**Forgotten Banner:** "I was raised by hands both courts deny. Let them deny the blood next."

### TG-CHAPTER-007 — Roots Remember Wounds
**Maeve:** "Walk lightly. Not because the ground is fragile. Because it is listening."

### TG-CHAPTER-008 — The Predator Law
**Maeve:** "The Beast Realms do not invade. They arrive where appetite has already been invited."

### TG-CHAPTER-009 — Bargain in Black Iron
**Varak:** "You came seeking passage. I offer it. If the price offends you, ask the Gate to become generous."

### TG-CHAPTER-010 — The First Flood Below Fire
**Varak:** "Fire does not fear water. It fears a current with judgment behind it."

### TG-CHAPTER-011 — Sacred Sun Temple
**Mesha:** "The first node is marked complete, but the stars disagree. Something is learning our symbols."

### TG-CHAPTER-012 — River of Light Stronghold
**Mesha:** "The river bends uphill. Record that before you correct it. Miracles and wounds often wear the same light."

### TG-CHAPTER-013 — The Mythos Gate
**Gate Silhouette:** "Bring your measures. I have swallowed brighter laws."

**Aten Ra Deity Voice:** "Then choke on this one."

### TG-CHAPTER-014 — Lanterns That Lie
**Sayo:** "Count the lanterns only after closing your eyes. The honest ones remain warm."

### TG-CHAPTER-015 — Mirror Lake Oath
**Sayo:** "The reflection moved first. That means the Gate has stopped asking permission from cause."

### TG-CHAPTER-016 — Storm Behind the Torii
**Foxfire Oath-Breaker:** "Every promise wants a witness. I became the witness promises deserve."

### TG-CHAPTER-017 — First Harmonic Step
**Celiane:** "The lower notes are missing. The Choir continues because it has mistaken absence for consent."

### TG-CHAPTER-018 — Glass Stair War
**Celiane:** "Do not run. The stair punishes urgency more reliably than malice."

### TG-CHAPTER-019 — Broken Heaven Engine
**Vault Warden:** "Mercy variable removed. Preservation purity restored."

**Celiane:** "No. Preservation that cannot recognize the living has become another Collapse."
