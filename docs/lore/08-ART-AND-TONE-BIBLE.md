# 08 — Art and Tone Bible

## Official Art Style

Premium mobile tactical RPG stylized realism.

Every character should read as an extradimensional being powerful enough to become the source of an ancient god myth.

## Style Pillars

- Stylized realism
- High-detail digital painting
- Mythological fantasy
- Clean readable silhouettes
- Semi-realistic anatomy
- Painterly textures
- Crisp material rendering
- Dramatic cinematic lighting
- Bold value contrast
- Strong edge highlights
- Rich color harmony
- Collectible Titan presentation
- Hand-painted finish

## Avoid

- Photorealistic humans
- Generic fantasy templates
- Cartoon-only designs
- Excessive anime styling
- Sci-fi aesthetics
- Extradimensional beings
- Futuristic craft
- Futuristic armor
- Guns
- Modern weapons
- Futuristic cities
- Galactic backgrounds
- Space battles
- Generic medieval fantasy without Realm identity

## Writing Tone

Mythos Gates should feel ancient, dangerous, sacred, and strategic.

The tone should be:

- Mythological but not academic
- Epic but readable
- Mysterious but clear
- Premium mobile RPG, not parody
- Civilizational, not superhero-based
- Extradimensional, not extraterrestrial

## Faction Writing Rule

No playable Realm is purely good or purely evil.

Each Realm must have:

- A survival philosophy
- A beautiful side
- A dangerous side
- Cultural logic
- Internal honor or law
- Reasons to fear other Realms
- Reasons other Realms fear it

## Titan Writing Rule

A Titan description should answer:

1. Which Realm shaped this being?
2. What civilization role does it embody?
3. What mythological archetype did humans misunderstand?
4. What battlefield role does that lore create?
5. What visual elements make it impossible to confuse with another Realm?
