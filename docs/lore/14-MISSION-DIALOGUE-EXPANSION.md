# Mission Dialogue Expansion — v0.3.8

## Purpose

This pass expands the dialogue spine from simple chapter openings into complete mission-ready beats for every current chapter.

Each chapter now has:

- Mission intro lines
- Mid-battle tactical pressure lines
- Low-health warning lines
- Victory lines
- Defeat / fallback lines
- Post-mission consequence lines

## Tone Standard

Dialogue remains restrained, mythic, tactical, and lore-bound. NPCs interpret and pressure the story; Titans remain the chosen deity. Threats speak with dread or law, not jokes.
