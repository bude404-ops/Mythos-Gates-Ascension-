# 03 — Realms

## Realm Rule

A Realm is an extradimensional civilization-domain shaped by mythic law, history, materials, symbols, and power. Each playable Realm must feel like its own ancient civilization, not a costume pack.

## 1. Aten Ra

**Realm:** The Solar Dominion of Khepra  
**Ascension Route:** Sun-Scale Verdict Descent  
**Entry Gate:** The Sun-Scale Gate  
**Gate State:** Stable Gate under Ma’at strain  
**Identity:** An extradimensional solar civilization whose crowns, pylons, collars, sun-discs, scarabs, throne-magic, river rites, lion guardians, and desert-storm wardens later appeared in human Egyptian myth as imperfect memories of the source Realm.  
**Philosophy:** Ma’at is not morality to Aten Ra; it is physics. Solar authority, measured truth, and sacred kingship keep Isfet from unmaking reality.  
**Route Function:** One selected Aten Ra god or goddess enters Sun-Scale Verdict Descent to test the Realm's law through source-culture hazards, Hollow pressure, elite locks, lore detours, and the boss chamber.

Visual identity: Solar-disc crowns with hand-like rays, pharaonic collars and pectorals, linen-and-gold regalia, faience blue-green inlays, lapis/obsidian shadows, scarab renewal marks, falcon/jackal/lion/cobra masks, lotus/papyrus river motifs, pylon harnesses, was-sceptre weapon geometry, Ma’at balance lines, Nile-black and desert-red duality.

## 2. Asgardian

**Realm:** The Storm-Rooted Aesir Holds  
**Ascension Route:** Thunder-Oath Root Gauntlet  
**Entry Gate:** The Thunder-Oath Gate  
**Gate State:** Ruin Gate with active oath memory  
**Identity:** A realm-born oath civilization whose halls, runes, ravens, world-root bridges, funerary feasts, shield walls, Valkyrie weatherfields, and storm kings later entered human Norse myth as distorted saga memory.  
**Philosophy:** A vow is not language to Asgardians; it is weather, weapon, bloodline, and law. Oath held becomes power. Oath broken becomes storm judgment.  
**Route Function:** One selected Asgardian god or goddess enters Thunder-Oath Root Gauntlet to test the Realm's law through source-culture hazards, Hollow pressure, elite locks, lore detours, and the boss chamber.

Visual identity: storm-iron mail, oath-ring collars, raven and wolf omen shapes, world-root veins, Valkyrie wing-weather, thunder scars, frost-giant mass, shield-wall geometry, ash wood, funeral flame, runes burned as physical wounds not UI glow.

## 3. Olympian

**Ascension Route:** Laurel-Sky Hubris Trial

**Philosophy:** Excellence is sacred only when witnessed. Olympian power is trial, beauty, rivalry, law, and victory turned into divine physics.  
**Route Function:** One selected Olympian god or goddess enters Laurel-Sky Hubris Trial to test the Realm's law through source-culture hazards, Hollow pressure, elite locks, lore detours, and the boss chamber.

Visual identity: living marble-light bodies, bronze cuirasses, laurel circuits, aegis scales, thunder tribunal auras, oracle smoke, amphitheater armor curves, hoplite shield geometry, winged victory accents, gold-blue skyfire, carved but non-readable relief bands.

## 4. Kami

**Realm:** The Sacred Kingdoms  
**Ascension Route:** Torii-Moon Mirror Road

**Philosophy:** Every boundary is alive. Kami power is respect for the threshold between human, spirit, nature, memory, and storm.  
**Route Function:** One selected Kami god or goddess enters Torii-Moon Mirror Road to test the Realm's law through source-culture hazards, Hollow pressure, elite locks, lore detours, and the boss chamber.

Visual identity: torii silhouettes, shrine lacquer red, white paper shide, braided shimenawa cords, mirror discs, foxfire blue, ink-wash smoke, cedar bark, moonlit silk, oni iron, tengu feather wind, dragon-rain scales, mask geometry, seasonal petals and ash.

## 5. Tuatha

**Philosophy:** Memory grows roots. A promise, a song, a bloodline, or a forbidden act can change the shape of the forest forever.  
**Route Function:** One selected Tuatha god or goddess enters Silver-Root Geas Labyrinth to test the Realm's law through source-culture hazards, Hollow pressure, elite locks, lore detours, and the boss chamber.

Visual identity: silver branch crowns, moonlit green-gold, living bark armor, woven thorn mantles, mist veils, standing stone geometry, battle-crow omens, cauldron glow, druid knots as organic growth not decoration, geas scars, fae glass, root-lace anatomy.

## 7. Infernal Dominion

**Realm:** The Infernal Dominion

**Philosophy:** Power is debt with teeth. Every bargain creates a throne, a chain, or a wound.  
**Route Function:** One selected Infernal Dominion god or goddess enters Black-Iron Debt Descent to test the Realm's law through source-culture hazards, Hollow pressure, elite locks, lore detours, and the boss chamber.

Visual identity: black iron crowns, volcanic glass armor, contract parchment skin, chain mantles, ember fissures, horn hierarchies, bat/dragon wing shadows, clawed court gloves, blood wax seals, ash velvet, obsidian thrones, coin/debt motifs, execution architecture.

## Campaign Threat Layer

The Hollow is not an eighth playable Realm. It is the cross-route anti-civilization pressure layer that attacks memory, identity, terrain law, and isolated deity decisions across every Ascension route.

## Empire Conflict Rule

Empyrean versus Infernal Dominion is not good versus evil. Empyrean represents ORDER. Infernal Dominion represents POWER. Both can produce honorable individuals. Both can commit terrible acts.
