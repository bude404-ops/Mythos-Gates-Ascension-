# 03 — Realms

## Realm Rule

A Realm is not a planet. A Realm is an extradimensional civilization-domain shaped by mythic law, history, materials, symbols, and power.

Each playable Realm must feel like its own ancient civilization, not a costume pack.

## 1. Aten Ra

**Realm:** The Golden Dominion of Aten  
**Inspiration:** Ancient Egyptian mythology  
**Identity:** A divine solar civilization built around sacred order, divine kingship, solar power, ancient temples, celestial knowledge, and solar law.  
**Philosophy:** Sacred order channels solar power into civilization.

Aten Ra is a civilization of judgment, radiance, command, divine law, and ancient solar mathematics. Its Titans should feel like sacred monuments brought to life by the logic of the sun.

Visual identity: gold, bronze, sacred geometry, ancient temples, divine realm influence, solar light, obsidian enamel, white-fire glyphs, amber Radiance, pyramid-pylon armor shapes.

## 2. Asgardian

**Realm:** Valhalla Beyond the Veil  
**Inspiration:** Norse mythology  
**Identity:** A warrior civilization built around honor, courage, strength, storms, ancient oaths, legendary combat, and the warrior afterlife.  
**Philosophy:** Oaths give war meaning; courage gives power form.

The Asgardian Realms measure power by vows endured and battles survived. Storms are not weather here; they are memory, judgment, and inheritance.

Visual identity: steel, iron, rune markings, storm energy, ancient warrior architecture, mountain-forged mass, giant-blooded bodies, frost scars, thunder wounds, oath-rune armor.

## 3. Olympian

**Realm:** The Celestial Heights of Olympus  
**Inspiration:** Greek mythology  
**Identity:** A divine civilization built around glory, perfection, achievement, strength, beauty, and divine supremacy.  
**Philosophy:** Excellence proves divinity.

Olympian civilization believes the divine is proven through excellence. Its Titans should feel sculptural, terrifyingly ideal, and radiant with competitive supremacy.

Visual identity: bronze, gold, marble, celestial light, ancient temples, divine weapons, living statue anatomy, ichor-lit cracks, sculptural bodies, perfect but non-human divine forms.

## 4. Kami

**Realm:** The Sacred Kingdoms  
**Inspiration:** Japanese mythology  
**Identity:** A spiritual civilization built around sacred duty, ancestral power, spirits, nature, discipline, and divine balance.  
**Philosophy:** Balance is defended by precision.

The Kami Realms treat imbalance as both wound and invasion. Their Titans should feel graceful, disciplined, spectral, shrine-bound, and exact.

Visual identity: lacquered armor, steel, sacred markings, spirit energy, shrines, masks, spirit ribbons, floating long-line anatomy, ink, mist, shrine-light, disciplined divine silhouettes.

## 5. Tuatha

**Realm:** Avalora  
**Inspiration:** Celtic mythology  
**Identity:** An ancient civilization intertwined with nature, sacred forests, fae beings, ancient stones, druidic magic, and the living world.  
**Philosophy:** The living world adapts and endures.

Tuatha civilization does not command nature from outside it. It survives by becoming part of root, stone, moonlight, oath, grove, and thorn.

Visual identity: emerald, silver, living wood, nature magic, ancient stones, sacred forests, fae danger, rootlike anatomy, bark-bronze skin, antler crowns, moonlit grove power.

## 6. Empyrean

**Realm:** The Radiant Hierarchies  
**Inspiration:** Human legends of Heaven, celestial kingdoms, angels, divine messengers, radiant beings, and heavenly guardians  
**Identity:** A civilization built around divine order, hierarchy, purpose, law, preservation, and control of chaos.  
**Philosophy:** Existence survives through order.

Empyreans are not literal angels. They are extradimensional beings that inspired human celestial interpretations.

Visual identity: radiant gold-white metals, celestial light, ordered winglike silhouettes, sacred geometry, divine halls, law sigils, light-matter skin, halo-spine geometry, throne-like bodies.

## 7. Infernal Dominion

**Realm:** The Infernal Dominion  
**Inspiration:** Human legends of Hell, demons, devils, underworld rulers, infernal beasts, fire, and darkness  
**Identity:** A powerful civilization built around strength, conquest, ambition, hierarchy, survival, and freedom through power.  
**Philosophy:** Power belongs to those capable of wielding it.

Infernal beings are not literal demons. They are extradimensional beings that inspired human underworld interpretations. They have culture, laws, honor, politics, warriors, sorcerers, artisans, and leaders.

Visual identity: obsidian, hellfire, dark iron, chains, burning sigils, infernal fortress forms, horn-lineage anatomy, ash-fire skin, ember eyes, volcanic cracks, predatory royal posture.

## Empire Conflict Rule

Empyrean versus Infernal Dominion is not good versus evil.

Empyrean represents ORDER.

Infernal Dominion represents POWER.

Empyreans believe uncontrolled power creates chaos. Infernal beings believe absolute order becomes tyranny.

Both can produce honorable individuals. Both can commit terrible acts.
