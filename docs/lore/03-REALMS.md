# 03 — Realms

## Realm Rule

A Realm is an extradimensional civilization-domain shaped by mythic law, history, materials, symbols, and power. Each playable Realm must feel like its own ancient civilization, not a costume pack.

## Realm Advantage System

When Avatars cross through the Mythos Gates, their power is affected by which Realm they fight in:

| Territory | Avatar Power | Aura | Ability Charges | Cooldowns | Risk | Reward |
|-----------|-------------|------|-----------------|-----------|------|--------|
| Home Realm | Maximum | Full radiance | Full charges | Shortest | Low | Standard |
| Enemy Realm | Reduced | Dimmed | Reduced | Longer | High | Greater Belief/Influence |
| Earth (Neutral) | Balanced | Medium | Balanced | Normal | Medium | Balanced |

- **Home Realm:** The Avatar channels maximum divine power. Home faction enemies are allies. This is where you farm, level, and prepare.
- **Enemy Realm:** Crossing through the Gate into hostile territory weakens the Avatar. The home faction has the advantage. Invading is risky but offers greater Belief and Influence rewards.
- **Earth:** No faction has home advantage. All Avatars are balanced. Hollow invasions create dungeons. This is the proving ground for cross-faction PvP and PvE.

## Earth — Neutral Territory

Earth is the neutral ground of the Mythos Gates universe. It is the original crossroads where all Realms once intersected, and where ancient humans first witnessed the divine beings that became their myths.

### Role in Gameplay
- **Dungeons:** Corrupted zones where the Hollow are invading reality through damaged Gates
- **Cross-faction combat:** PvP and PvE on balanced ground where no faction has home advantage
- **The proving ground:** Where Avatars test their strength against all threats equally
- **World Boss arena:** Ancient World Bosses emerge through damaged Earth Gates

### Lore
Earth is not controlled by any single Realm. After the Ascension Collapse, damaged Gates across Earth became entry points for Hollow corruption, rival faction incursions, and primordial threats. The seven Realms now compete to control these Gate sites, using Earth as the contested battleground for their divine war.

Earth is where the mythology began. It is also where it might end.

## 1. Aten Ra

**Realm:** The Solar Dominion of Khepra
**Inspiration:** Ancient Egyptian mythology
**Identity:** A divine solar civilization built around sacred order, divine kingship, solar power, ancient temples, celestial knowledge, and solar law.
**Philosophy:** Sacred order channels solar power into civilization.
**Avatar Advantage:** Aten Ra Avatars fight at full power within the Solar Dominion. The desert sun fuels their radiance, and the sacred law strengthens their judgment abilities.

Visual identity: Solar-disc crowns with hand-like rays, pharaonic collars and pectorals, linen-and-gold regalia, faience blue-green inlays, lapis/obsidian shadows, scarab renewal marks, falcon/jackal/lion/cobra masks, lotus/papyrus river motifs, pylon harnesses, was-sceptre weapon geometry, Ma'at balance lines, Nile-black and desert-red duality.

## 2. Asgardian

**Realm:** The Storm-Rooted Aesir Holds  
**Ascension Route:** Thunder-Oath Root Gauntlet  
**Entry Gate:** The Thunder-Oath Gate  
**Gate State:** Ruin Gate with active oath memory  
**Identity:** A realm-born oath civilization whose halls, runes, ravens, world-root bridges, funerary feasts, shield walls, Valkyrie weatherfields, and storm kings later entered human Norse myth as distorted saga memory.  
**Philosophy:** A vow is not language to Asgardians; it is weather, weapon, bloodline, and law. Oath held becomes power. Oath broken becomes storm judgment.  
**Avatar Advantage:** Asgardian Avatars fight at full power within the Aesir Holds. Storm-iron skin glows brightest, oath-bound abilities charge fastest, and the world-root channels maximum energy.
**Route Function:** One selected Asgardian Avatar enters Thunder-Oath Root Gauntlet to test the Realm's law through source-culture hazards, Hollow pressure, elite locks, lore detours, and the boss chamber.

Visual identity: storm-iron mail, oath-ring collars, raven and wolf omen shapes, world-root veins, Valkyrie wing-weather, thunder scars, frost-giant mass, shield-wall geometry, ash wood, funeral flame, runes burned as physical wounds not UI glow.

## Campaign Threat Layer

## 8. Campaign Threat Layer

**Faction Role:** Non-playable campaign antagonist layer
**Domains:** Forgotten ruins, Hollow voids, Beast Realms, Gateborn anomaly zones, and Ancient World Boss sites
**Identity:** The eighth storyline pressure layer is not a normal playable Realm. It is the combined force of everything the Ascension Collapse exposed, mutated, starved, or failed to keep sealed.
**Philosophy:** The Collapse did not create one enemy; it revealed every unfinished wound beneath the Mythos Gates.

This layer exists so the full game can speak in eight faction-facing forces without inventing an eighth ordinary playable civilization. The seven playable Realms remain the collection and campaign backbone. The Campaign Threat Layer supplies enemies, bosses, events, anomaly missions, Hollow corruption, Forgotten memory-loss, Beast Realm hunts, Gateborn mutations, and Ancient World Boss guardians.

Visual identity: broken gods, identity-drained cities, primordial monsters, distorted Gate geometry, memory voids, unstable anomaly terrain, and legendary singular threats.

## Empire Conflict Rule

Empyrean versus Infernal Dominion is not good versus evil. Empyrean represents ORDER. Infernal Dominion represents POWER. Both can produce honorable individuals. Both can commit terrible acts.

## Realm Access

**Earth** is the primary campaign zone. All 20 missions per faction take place on Earth (MAP-001). Earth is neutral ground — no faction advantage or penalty.

**Faction Realms** (Khepra, Asgard, Olympus, Takamagahara, Avalora, Radiant Hierarchies, Infernal Dominion) are reserved for special events:
- Realm Raids: Limited-time incursions for exclusive rewards
- Faction Wars: Cross-realm PvP events
- Seasonal Content: Holiday-themed events
- World Boss Arenas: Boss encounters in realm-specific locations

Faction realm maps (MG-MAP-002 through MG-MAP-012) are used ONLY for these events, not for the main campaign.
