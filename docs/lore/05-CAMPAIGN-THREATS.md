# 05 — Campaign Threats

## The Forgotten

The Forgotten are ancient beings and remnants of civilizations that lost identity after disconnection from their original Realm.

Examples:

- Broken gods
- Lost warriors
- Ruined civilizations
- Ancient relics
- Memory fragments
- Forgotten guardians

Role:

- Campaign enemies
- Lore mysteries
- Ruined Realm encounters
- Bosses
- Exploration events

Tone:

Mysterious rather than simply evil.

## The Hollow

The Hollow are entities that consume life, magical energy, memories, and identity, leaving civilizations physically intact but spiritually empty.

Role:

- Campaign attrition enemies
- Debuff elites
- Healing denial bosses

Mechanics:

- Draining
- Debuffs
- Memory effects
- Healing denial
- Ability suppression
- Attrition

Tone:

Unnatural and frightening.

## The Beast Realms

The Beast Realms are primordial mythological creature territories from extradimensional wilderness, not one organized civilization.

Examples:

- Dragons
- Giants
- Griffins
- Hydras
- Ancient beasts
- Fae monsters
- Sea monsters
- Divine remnants

Role:

- Enemy packs
- Elite encounters
- Bosses
- Wilderness events
- Rare hunts

Tone:

Primordial wilderness.

## The Gateborn

The Gateborn are entities created, transformed, or mutated by unstable Titan Gates. Their forms depend on the Gate that created them.

Role:

- Unusual missions
- Surprise encounters
- Dimensional events

Mechanics:

- Unpredictable abilities
- Terrain manipulation
- Dimensional distortion
- Mutations
- Portal mechanics
- Strange battlefield effects

Tone:

Unstable anomaly.

## Ancient World Bosses

Ancient World Bosses are singular legendary beings that do not belong to conventional factions and should not automatically become full factions.

Examples:

- Ancient dragons
- Forgotten giants
- World serpents
- Primordial beasts
- Ancient divine remnants
- Gate guardians
- Lost Titans

Role:

- Unique legendary encounters
- Bosses
- Titan Gate guardians

Tone:

Legendary singular threat.
