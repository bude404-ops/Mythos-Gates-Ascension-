# 05 — Campaign Threats

## The Hollow

The Hollow is the primary cross-route pressure layer in Mythos Gates: Ascension. It is not one of the seven playable Realm civilizations and it is not a hidden eighth normal faction. It is an anti-civilization that consumes memory, identity, magical energy, terrain law, and isolated deity decisions.

In standard missions, the Hollow pressures one selected playable god or goddess at a time. It punishes overextension, corrupts objectives, drains route memory, imitates source-culture forms it has consumed, and turns each Realm's sacred law against itself.

- **Sun-Scale Verdict Descent:** Hollow scale-thieves, Isfet-branded jackal guardians, revived verdict shades, sun-scorched oathbreakers; boss contrast — The Misweighed Dawn Judge.
- **Thunder-Oath Root Gauntlet:** oathless draugr, rune-bound wolves, storm revenants, Hollow oath-eaters; boss contrast — The Forgotten Standard Bearer.
- **Laurel-Sky Hubris Trial:** laurel-bound champions, oracle-maddened shades, bronze shield automata shaped by myth not machinery, Hollow victory parasites; boss contrast — The Thunder Tribunal Usurper.
- **Torii-Moon Mirror Road:** foxfire doubles, impure shrine shades, oni iron trespassers, Hollow mask-wearers; boss contrast — The False Reflection Kami.
- **Silver-Root Geas Labyrinth:** fae time-thieves, thornbound oathbreakers, battle-crow doom markers, Hollow changelings; boss contrast — The Crow-Crowned Geas Breaker.
- **Choir-Vault Discord Ascent:** discordant choir husks, memory-burned cherub guardians, eye-wheel fragments, Hollow silence swarms; boss contrast — The Silent Seraph of Broken Praise.
- **Black-Iron Debt Descent:** contract devils, debt-marked prisoners, ash-velvet tempters, Hollow clause-eaters; boss contrast — The Volcanic Glass Magistrate.

## The Forgotten

The Forgotten are ancient beings, remnants, and broken civilizations that lost identity after disconnection from their original Realm. They appear as lore mysteries, ruined Realm encounters, guardians, bosses, and memory-fragment events. They are tragic before they are evil.

## The Beast Realms

The Beast Realms are primordial mythological creature territories from extradimensional wilderness, not one organized civilization. Dragons, giants, griffins, hydras, fae monsters, sea monsters, divine remnants, and ancient beasts can appear as packs, elites, bosses, rare hunts, and wilderness route pressure.

## The Gateborn

The Gateborn are entities created, transformed, or mutated by unstable Mythos Gates. Their forms depend on the Gate that created them. They belong in anomaly missions, surprise encounters, terrain-distortion mechanics, and strange battlefield effects.

## Ancient World Bosses

Ancient World Bosses are singular legendary beings that do not belong to conventional factions and should not automatically become full factions. They are unique mythic threats: ancient dragons, forgotten giants, world serpents, primordial beasts, divine remnants, Gate guardians, or sealed route catastrophes.
