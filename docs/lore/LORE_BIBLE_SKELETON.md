## DESIGN RULE — SOLO-FIRST COMBAT (locked per BudE404, Sept 2 2026)
The main game is SOLO play. All ability kits are designed and worded for solo gameplay:
- ALL healing is SELF-ONLY. No ally heals.
- ALL buffs are SELF-ONLY. No ally/team buffs.
- Kit wording addresses "you"/the player directly.
- Crowd control, marks, debuffs, and terrain effects on enemies are unaffected by this rule.
- Multiplayer may come later — if it does, self-buffs stay self-buffs; no kit rebalance will introduce ally-targeted heals/buffs.
Applies retroactively to all approved faction docs (F001-F008) and to every future faction.
