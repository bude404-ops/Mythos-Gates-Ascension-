# Cinders of Dominion — Campaign Lore

## Canon Function

**Cinders of Dominion** establishes the Infernal Dominion as more than evil imagery. Its civilization believes survival belongs to those willing to bear consequence openly. Chains, contracts, fire, and authority are cultural law, not decoration.

The campaign asks whether a bargain made under catastrophe can ever be clean.

## Narrative Premise

A black iron Gate opens beneath a court of execution sigils. The Infernal Dominion does not hide its desire for control. It argues that control is the only mercy left after the Ascension Collapse proved trust insufficient.

Varak Chain-Magistrate offers information, passage, and power — never freely, never falsely. The danger is not that he lies. The danger is that he tells the truth in a shape that benefits Dominion law.

The Creator must navigate a campaign where every tactical victory creates a record, every record creates leverage, and every leverage point can become a chain.

## Campaign Threat Fit

- **Primary threat:** Infernal bargain politics around unstable Gates.
- **Secondary threat:** Gateborn flame distortions and execution sigil hazards.
- **Moral pressure:** Useful information with a cost attached.

## Lore-Friendly Dialogue Seed

**Varak Chain-Magistrate:** "A free gift is only a debt whose owner has not stepped forward. I prefer honest chains."

**Varak Chain-Magistrate:** "You may refuse the bargain. The Gate will not refuse its hunger."

**Execution Sigil:** "Witness. Weight. Payment."

## Tone Guardrails

Do not write Infernal characters as cartoon devils. Their menace should come from lawful pressure, not melodrama.
