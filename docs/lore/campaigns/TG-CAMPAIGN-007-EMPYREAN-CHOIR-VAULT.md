# Empyrean Choir Vault — Campaign Lore

## Canon Function

**Empyrean Choir Vault** shows the beauty and danger of radiant hierarchy. The Empyrean are not simple angels and not default good. Their order preserves reality, but it may preserve by removing everything it names as dissonance.

The campaign asks how much imperfection a world must be allowed to keep in order to remain alive.

## Narrative Premise

Above the broken roads, a choir vault continues singing old stabilization law. The song once held collapsing Gate paths in place. After the Ascension Collapse, its harmonics hardened into judgment.

Celiane White-Vault Witness does not apologize for Empyrean severity. She believes mercy without structure becomes another form of abandonment. Yet even she hears the wrongness in a vault that now tries to correct living survivors as if they were damaged notes.

The Creator's strike force ascends through floating platforms, resonance beacons, and glass stairways to reach a heaven-engine that has forgotten the difference between healing and erasure.

## Campaign Threat Fit

- **Primary threat:** Empyrean preservation law becoming hostile through damaged automation.
- **Secondary threat:** Gateborn harmonic distortions and timed bridge collapse.
- **Boss pressure:** A vault warden shielded by resonance beacons.

## Lore-Friendly Dialogue Seed

**Celiane White-Vault Witness:** "Order is not kindness. But without order, kindness has nowhere to stand."

**Celiane White-Vault Witness:** "The Choir is correcting wounds by removing the wounded. That is not preservation. That is fear wearing law."

**Vault Warden:** "Dissonance named. Dissonance reduced. Dissonance removed."

## Tone Guardrails

Avoid angelic clichés. Empyrean speech should sound formal, luminous, and quietly dangerous.
