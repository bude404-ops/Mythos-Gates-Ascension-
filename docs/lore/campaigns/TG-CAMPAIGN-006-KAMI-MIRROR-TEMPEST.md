# Kami Mirror Tempest — Campaign Lore

## Canon Function

**Kami Mirror Tempest** defines the Kami campaign language: balance defended through precision, shrine law, reflection, and consequences for careless motion.

This is not a whimsical spirit forest. It is a sacred jurisdiction under storm pressure.

## Narrative Premise

The Shrine-Path Archipelago has always allowed passage, but never possession. A Titan Gate reopening changes that balance. Reflections begin moving before their owners. Torii roads appear in the wrong order. Paper seals rewrite themselves after being read.

Sayo Shrine-Cartographer maps the impossible routes by refusing to trust any path that flatters the traveler. She is calm because panic feeds bad spirits. She is severe because balance does not survive sentimental handling.

The campaign's central fear is not deception for its own sake. It is the loss of reliable relation: between image and object, promise and act, shrine and guest.

## Campaign Threat Fit

- **Primary threat:** Gateborn reflection anomalies.
- **Secondary threat:** Foxfire oath-breaker spirits and wind-lane hazards.
- **Realm tension:** Kami precision versus outsider force.

**Foxfire Oath-Breaker:** "A promise reflected is still enough to burn."

## Tone Guardrails

Avoid cute spirit banter. Kami dialogue should be spare, exact, and edged with ritual consequence.
