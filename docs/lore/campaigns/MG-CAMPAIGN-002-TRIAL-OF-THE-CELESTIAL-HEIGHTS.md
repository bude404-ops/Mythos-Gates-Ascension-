# Trial of the Celestial Heights — Campaign Lore


> **Avatar System**: Players channel Avatars of their chosen deity — divine projections that carry the deity's power into combat. The deity remains protected in its domain. Progression via Belief and Influence. Avatar death = respawn at deity domain with 10-20% penalty. Solo combat only. God-scale in all realms.


## Canon Function

**Trial of the Celestial Heights** is the first campaign to show Realm conflict without reducing either side to villainy. Olympian excellence and Asgardian oath-law both have beauty. Both can become dangerous when the Gates reopen.

The campaign tests the difference between glory and duty.

## Narrative Premise

A Gate path forms across high air, marble platforms, storm-root bridges, and old trial arenas. The Olympian side sees the reopening as proof that worth must be demonstrated before power is claimed. The Asgardian side sees the same Gate as an oath-site where reckless ambition once broke dimensional trust.

Neither side is lying. Both remember a different wound.

The Creator's chosen deity enters a trial already poisoned by a Forgotten banner — a remnant from a pre-Collapse war that both Realms deny starting. The danger is not merely combat. The danger is that pride and oath may be pushed into reenacting an old catastrophe.

## Campaign Threat Fit

- **Primary threat:** Forgotten war-remnant bound to a banner that should not exist.
- **Secondary threat:** Beast Realm sky predators drawn by trial blood.
- **Realm tension:** Olympian proof versus Asgardian restraint.

## Lore-Friendly Dialogue Seed

**Hrodda Oath-Witness:** "The bridge remembers every foot that crossed it in anger. Step with purpose, or it will count you among the oath-breakers."

**Lyrion Laurel-Envoy:** "A trial is not cruelty. It is mercy with its eyes open. Power untested becomes a public disaster."

**Forgotten Banner:** "You both swore. You both broke. I remain."

## Tone Guardrails

Do not make the factions petty rivals. Their conflict should feel like two ancient legal systems colliding over a live weapon.
