## Identity
- Was: "Kami" → Now: **THE THOUSAND TORII** — the pantheon of boundaries. Gods of every threshold: matter/spirit, reflection/original, storm/shrine, life/death.
- Creed: **"The boundary is sacred because it can be crossed."**
- Faith philosophy: THE KEPT LINE — faith grows where boundaries are respected (torii maintained, thresholds honored). Their shrine-path tests attention, not power (preserved from canon).
- Realm: **THE MIRROR SHRINE-PATH** — a shrine-road under an enormous low moon; torii march to the horizon; mirror-lakes between them reflect what the Gate wants you to believe.

## The Four Gods (roles: Warrior M, Archer M, Caster F, Assassin F)
1. **ARASHIDO THE THRESHOLD STORM** (M, Warrior — was Susanoo) — god of the storm that guards boundaries; the loudest gate-keeper. Weapon: **KAZENAGI**, a storm-bladed naginata. Active 1: Gale Step (dash through torii at speed). Active 2: Tempest Sweep (AOE knockback). Ultimate: **THE STORM CROSSES** — a line of storm-torii marches across the field; enemies caught between are struck.
2. **YORUKA THE HOLLOW MOON** (M, Archer — was Tsukuyomi) — god of the moon that watches without blinking. Weapon: **THE PHASE BOW** — moonlight arrows that pass through walls (spirit-boundary synergy). Active 1: Crescent Volley (3 piercing shots). Active 2: Moonmark (see marked enemies through walls; bonus at range). Ultimate: **TOTAL ECLIPSE** — the field darkens; only Yoruka's moonmarks glow, and his arrows always find them.
3. **HIKARUNE THE WEAVING SUN** (F, Caster — was Amaterasu) — goddess of sunlight as a woven cloth. Weapon: **THE SUNWOVEN MIRROR**, a bronze mirror-staff (Shinto mirror motif preserved from the aesthetic). Active 1: Sunthread (binding light-beam roots enemies). Active 2: Radiant Weave (self shield woven from light). Ultimate: **DAWN REWOUND** — restores true light to the field: heals YOU AND purges false reflections (the hard-counter to this realm's illusions — her whole kit is anti-Hollow here).
4. **MUKAGE THE UNFINISHED** (F, Assassin — was Izanami) — goddess of the first boundary: she died drawing the line between life and death and remained standing on it. Weapon: **THE BOUNDARY BLADES**. Active 1: Spirit-Step (blink across the boundary — through terrain). Active 2: Unmaking Cut (bonus damage vs illusions and false reflections). Ultimate: **THE THRESHOLD CLOSES** — marked enemies are pulled halfway out of their bodies, defenseless — the INVERSE of the boundary-thieves' move; your strikes land true on half-spirited enemies.
