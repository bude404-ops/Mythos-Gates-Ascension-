# Mythos Gates: Ascension — Progression & Monetization Specification
**Locked:** August 19, 2026
**Approved by:** BudE404 (Creative Director), BIGagent404
**Power Model:** Option C — Hybrid (Level 20% / Gear 50% / Abilities 30%)

---

## Design Philosophy

Mythos Gates is **free-to-play**. The grind must feel REWARDING, not punishing. Players pay to skip TIME, not to skip FUN. Zero pay-to-win — a free player who grinds can compete with a paying player at every level.

---

## Power Distribution (Option C — Hybrid)

| Source | % of Total Power | How to Get | Can Pay to Skip? |
|--------|-----------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Avatar Level** | 20% | Belief from combat | Yes (minor — QoL time-skip) |
| **Gear** | 50% | Drops from missions, bosses, raids, crafting | NO |
| **Ability Upgrades** | 30% | Belief investment in ability trees | NO |

**Core Rule:** A Level 100 avatar with Common gear loses to a Level 50 avatar with Epic gear. Real power comes from PLAYING the game, not paying.

---

## Leveling System

### Avatar Level (1-100)
- Levels up by spending **Belief** earned from combat
- Each level grants small base stat increases (dodge, parry, attack, HP, ability power)
- Total contribution to power: **20%** — levels unlock content, not dominance
- Level gates access to harder missions where better gear drops

### Divine Ascension (Every 10 Levels)
- Level 10, 20, 30... 100 = **Divine Ascension** milestone
- Major stat jump + **visual upgrade** to avatar appearance
- Avatar gets more ornate armor, bigger aura, new particle effects
- Level 100 = fully ascended deity avatar (cosmetic prestige + content unlock)

### Belief (Soft Currency — Grindable)
- Earned from every fight — wins give more, losses give less
- Used for: avatar leveling, ability upgrades, minor stat boosts
- **No daily cap**, but diminishing returns after 10 fights/day
- F2P players live on this currency — everything gameplay-related costs Belief
- Bonus Belief from: first win of the day, streak bonuses, chapter completion

### Influence (Premium Currency — Purchasable)
- Earned slowly through: weekly challenges, achievements, league rankings
- Can be **purchased** with real money
- Used for: **cosmetics, time-skip (QoL), battle pass, energy refill**
- **NEVER buys power** — no gear, no stats, no ability levels

## Gear System

### Gear Slots (6 per Avatar)

| Slot | Determines |
|------|----------|
| **Weapon** | Attack range + damage type |
| **Armor** | Defense + parry rating |
| **Helm** | Belief gain rate |
| **Boots** | Dodge rating + movement speed |
| **Relic** | Ultimate charge speed |
| **Sigil** | Ability cooldown reduction |

### Gear Rarity Tiers

| Rarity | Color | Stat Bonuses | Drop Source |
|--------|-------|-------------|------------|
| Common | Gray | Base stats | Normal missions |
| Uncommon | Green | +1 stat bonus | Chapter bosses |
| Rare | Blue | +2 stat bonuses | Elite remix missions |
| Epic | Purple | +3 stat bonuses + set bonus | Raid bosses |
| Legendary | Gold | Unique effects | World bosses (weekly) |
| Mythic | Red | Faction-exclusive effects | Crafting (rare materials) |

### Set Bonus System

| Pieces | Bonus |
|--------|-------|
| 2-piece | +15% ability damage |
| 4-piece | +30% ultimate charge speed |
| 6-piece | Unique faction ability unlocked |

- Sets are **faction-themed** (Aten Ra solar set, Asgardian storm set, etc.)
- Set hunting makes gear progression addictive without being required
- Players can beat the game with Rare gear; Mythic sets make you FEEL like a god

### Gear Sources (The Grind)

| Source | Rarity Range | Frequency |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| Normal missions | Common / Uncommon | Every fight |
| Chapter bosses | Uncommon guaranteed + Rare chance | Once per chapter |
| Elite remix missions | Rare drops | Repeatable daily |
| Raid bosses (multiplayer) | Epic drops | Weekly |
| World bosses | Legendary drops | Weekly |
| Crafting | Mythic gear | Rare materials from multiple campaigns |
| Daily challenges | Gear chest (random) | Daily |
| Weekly league | Top performers get guaranteed Epic | Weekly |

## Ability Upgrade System

### Ability Trees (30% of Total Power)
- Each deity has a unique ability tree with 3 branches
- Branches focus on: offense, defense, utility
- Upgrades cost Belief (soft currency)
- Player chooses which abilities to upgrade — build customization
- Respec costs Influence (premium) — encourages commitment to builds

### Ability Tiers
| Tier | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|-------|
| 1 (base) | Unlocked at avatar unlock | Base ability |
| 2 | 500 Belief | +20% effect |
| 3 | 1,500 Belief | +40% effect |
| 4 | 5,000 Belief | +60% effect + secondary effect |
| 5 (max) | 15,000 Belief | +100% effect + unique modifier |

## Monetization (F2P Done Right)

### What Players Pay For (No Power)
| Item | Cost | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| **Time-skip** | Influence | Instantly claim earned level (QoL, skips animation) |
| **Cosmetic skins** | Influence | Alternate deity looks, aura colors, ability VFX |
| **Battle pass** | $9.99/month | Seasonal reward track: extra Belief + cosmetics |
| **Energy refill** | Influence | More fights per day (energy regenerates free over time) |
| **Ability respec** | Influence | Reset ability tree to rechoose upgrades |

### What Players NEVER Pay For
- **Power** — no gear is cash-only
- **Stats** — can't buy dodge/parry/attack directly
- **Wins** — no pay-to-win combat advantages
- **Content** — all missions, factions, bosses are free

### Battle Pass Structure (Seasonal)
- Season length: 90 days
- Free track: Belief bonuses, 1 guaranteed Rare gear chest, cosmetic banner
- Premium track ($9.99): All free rewards + exclusive cosmetic sets, extra Belief, name plate, aura effects
- Reward tiers unlock by playing — no skip tickets (must PLAY to earn)
- Premium track accelerates Belief gain by 2x (faster grind, not more power)

## The Fun Loop

1. **Fight enemies** → earn Belief → level up → unlock harder missions
2. **Fight bosses** → get gear drops → equip better gear → feel stronger
3. **Complete gear sets** → unlock faction abilities → feel powerful
4. **Hit difficulty wall** → grind elite missions for upgrades → break through
5. **Weekly cycle:** raid bosses for Epic gear, league for rankings, world boss for Legendary
6. **Seasonal cycle:** new battle pass, new cosmetics, new challenges, new content

Every fight gives SOMETHING. The grind feels good because progression is constant and visible.

---

## Energy System

- Each fight costs energy (10 energy per fight, 100 max)
- Energy regenerates at 1 per 12 minutes (full refill in ~20 hours)
- F2P players get ~8 fights per day on natural regen
- Energy can be refilled with Influence (premium) — more chances at gear, but you still have to WIN
- First fight of the day is free (0 energy) — ensures daily engagement

---

## Anti-Pay-to-Win Guarantees

1. **Gear only comes from playing** — no gear chests in the shop
2. **Stats only come from gear + abilities + level** — can't buy raw stats
3. **Time-skip only affects 20% of power** — the other 80% requires gameplay
4. **Battle pass accelerates grind, doesn't bypass it** — must play to earn rewards
5. **A Level 50 with Epic gear beats a Level 100 with Common gear** — skill and grind > wallet
