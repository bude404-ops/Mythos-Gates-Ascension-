# Mythology Deep Dive — All 7 Factions

> **Avatar System**: Players channel Avatars — divine projections of their chosen Deity. The Deity remains protected in its domain. Progression via Belief and Influence. Avatar death = respawn. Solo combat. God-scale in all realms.


## Game Concept
The deities are the TRUE divine beings. Human cultures (Egyptians, Norse, Greeks, Japanese, Celts, etc.) copied their gods, architecture, clothing, and customs from DIMENSIONS of these real deities leaking through the Mythos Gates. The gods did not copy humans — humans copied gods.

## 1. ATEN RA (Egyptian Mythology)
### Sources: Pyramid Texts, Book of the Dead, Amduat, Hymn to Aten
**Aura Color:** Amber-gold solar radiance

### Aten Ra (Ra + Aten + Ma'at)
- Ra: Falcon-headed sun god, solar disc crown, solar barque. Noon sun = judgment without shadow.
- Aten: Sun-disc with radiating hand-rays ending in HANDS. Non-anthropomorphic origin.
- Ma'at: Ostrich feather, scales of judgment, truth, balance.
- Combined: Solar kingship, divine judgment, skull IS the solar disc (pharaohs copied the crown)

### Sutekh (Set/Seth)
- "Set animal" head — undefined beast with curved snout, square ears, forked tail. Red-haired, red-eyed.
- Storm god, desert, chaos, violence — BUT necessary force. Defends Ra from Apophis nightly.
- Murdered and dismembered Osiris. Strongest defender of the sun god. Aggressive, muscular build.

### Iset (Isis)
- Woman with throne-shaped crown. Later cow horns + sun disc.
- Throne IS her power. Most powerful magician — knew Ra's secret true name.
- Reassembled Osiris, used magic to resurrect him. Protective mother. Graceful, regal, maternal.

### Amunet (Amaunet)
- Woman with Red Crown or ostrich feather. Sometimes snake-headed.
- "The Hidden One." Hidden breath, unseen sovereignty, invisible power. Veiled, secret.

## 2. ASGARDIAN (Norse Mythology)
### Sources: Poetic Edda, Prose Edda (Snorri Sturluson), Sagas
**Aura Color:** Blue-white lightning + warm gunmetal forge-heat

### Odin (Odin)
- Eddas: "Tall, one-eyed, grey-bearded." Eye is "pale blue." Blue/parti-colored cloak. Broad hat. Gungnir spear.
- Companions: Ravens Huginn (Thought) & Muninn (Memory). Wolves Geri & Freki.
- Sacrifices: Eye to Mimir's Well (wisdom). Hung from Yggdrasil 9 nights (runes).
- Physical: Tall, lean (NOT bulky). Ancient. Wanderer-sage-king.

### Freyja (Vanir Goddess)
- Eddas: "Most beautiful of all goddesses." Golden hair, blue eyes. "Beautiful and mighty."
- Brisingamen necklace (glowing gold). Falcon-feather cloak. Chariot pulled by two cats.
- Weeps tears of GOLD. Seidr magic. Chooses who dies in battle. Takes half the dead.
- Physical: Tall, beautiful, powerful warrior-queen.

### Skadi (Allmother)
- Blue or white robes. Falcon-cloak. Keys at waist. Spinning wheel, distaff.
- Knows ALL fates but tells NO ONE. Made everything swear not to harm Baldur (except mistletoe).
- Hall: Fensalir (Marsh Hall). Weaves the clouds. Silent seer. Regal, composed, woven aesthetic.

### Thor (Thunder God)
- Eddas: "Burly, thick waist." Red hair, red beard. "Fierce eyes." Large, strong.
- Mjolnir (hammer). Járngreipr (iron gloves). Megingjord (power belt). Goat chariot.
- Protector of mankind. Most popular with common people. Fights with brute force. Massive, bulky.

## 3. OLYMPIAN (Greek Mythology)
### Sources: Iliad, Odyssey (Homer), Theogony (Hesiod), Homeric Hymns
**Aura Color:** Bronze-gold lightning + marble-white radiance

### Zeus (Sky King)
- Homer: "Cloud-gathering Zeus." "Father of gods and men." Storm-grey eyes. Thunderbolt. Eagle.
- Mature, regal, kingly. Braided beard. Commands armies. Bronze-gold lightning aura.

### Artemis (Huntress)
- Virgin huntress. Bow and arrows. Hunting dogs, stags. "Lady of wild things." Moon-associated.
- Athletic, lithe, fast. NOT heavily armored — hunter, not soldier. Moonlight silver aura.

### Ares (War Fury)
- Bronze armor. Spear. Blood and fury. "Hated by the gods" — violent, bloodthirsty.
- Pure violence, courage, bloodlust. Blood-stained. Massive, muscular, aggressive. Blood-red + bronze aura.

### Athena (Strategy)
- "Gray-eyed" (glaukopis). Helmet. Aegis shield with Medusa's head. Born fully armed from Zeus's forehead.
- Strategy, wisdom, disciplined warfare. Owl companion. General, not berserker. Gray-white + bronze aura.

## 4. KAMI (Japanese/Shinto Mythology)
### Sources: Kojiki (712 CE), Nihon Shoki (720 CE)
**Aura Color:** Red-gold imperial flame + moonlight silver

### Amaterasu (Sun Goddess)
- Born from Izanagi's LEFT EYE. "Radiant light that illuminated the world." Beautiful.
- Yata no Kagami (sacred mirror). Weaves in heaven. Queen of Takamagahara.
- Hid in cave (world went dark). Lured out with mirror and dancing. The sun IS her presence.

### Tsukuyomi (Moon God)
- Born from Izanagi's RIGHT EYE. "Beautiful and serene." Young, androgynous. White bronze mirror.
- Order, ritual, night law. Killed food goddess — Amaterasu split from him (day/night separated).
- Cold precision. Moonlight silver aura.

### Susanoo (Storm God)
- Born from Izanagi's NOSE. Wild, impetuous. "Beard eight hands long." Wild hair. Sword-wielding.
- Storm/sea god. Slew eight-headed serpent Yamata-no-Orochi. Found Kusanagi sword in its tail.
- Wild, powerful, chaotic. Storm-blue + sea-green aura with lightning.

### Izanami (Creation/Death)
- Created Japan with Izanagi. Died giving birth to fire god. Ruler of Yomi (underworld).
- "Young woman with graying hair, fair skin, bloodshot eyes." Decay, maggots — also creation.
- Dual nature: beautiful creator + decaying death. Dark purple + bone-white aura.

## 5. TUATHA (Celtic/Irish Mythology)
### Sources: Mythological Cycle, Lebor na hUidre, Metrical Dindshenchas
**Aura Color:** Emerald-green earth-glow + flame-orange fire

### Dagda (Good God)
- "Tall warrior of immense strength, uncut hair and beard, untidy appearance." Red hair. Large body.
- Cauldron (never empty). Club (one end kills, other resurrects). Harp (controls seasons).
- Father-king. Earth, abundance, generosity. Huge, powerful, untidy. Earth-green + warm gold aura.

### Morrigan (Phantom Queen)
- Shapeshifter: maiden, hag, crow, raven, cow, eel, wolf. Most famous as battlefield crow.
- War, fate, death, sovereignty. Foretells doom. "Phantom Queen." Chooses who dies.
- Dark, shifting. Blood-crow black + crimson edges aura.

### Brigid (Triple Flame)
- "Born with flames shooting from her head reaching to heaven." Beautiful. Rays of fire from head.
- Triple goddess: Poetry/inspiration, Healing/smithing, Craft/hearth. Daughter of Dagda.
- Sacred flame and holy wells. NOT destructive — INSPIRING fire. Orange-gold flame aura.

### Lugh (Many-Skilled)
- "Sun-faced" (good-looking). Young, handsome. Spear that never misses (becomes lightning).
- Samildanach — skilled in ALL arts. Slew Balor by throwing spear into his evil eye.
- Young, radiant, perfect. Golden-amber + lightning-silver aura.

## 6. EMPYREAN (Abrahamic Angelic)
### Sources: Book of Enoch, Revelation, Kabbalistic texts, Gnostic texts
**Aura Color:** White-gold holy radiance + burning-wheel gold

### Michael (Warrior Archangel)
- Sword, armor, scales. Tramples dragon/Satan. Commander of heavenly hosts.
- Warrior, protector, judge. Weighs souls. The divine general. White-gold + sword-flame aura.

### Gabriel (Messenger)
- Daniel: "Body like topaz, face like lightning, eyes like flaming torches, arms and legs like burnished bronze."
- Herald. Trumpet, scroll, lily. Announces births, revelations, judgments. Lightning-white + bronze aura.

### Raphael (Divine Wisdom)
- Gnostic: Feminine divine principle. Wisdom personified. "The thought of God."
- Radiant, serene, knowing. Soft white-gold + sapphire-blue aura.

### Jophiel (Divine Presence)
- Kabbalistic: Indwelling presence of God. Radiant glory. Cloud of glory, pillar of fire.
- Blinding radiance. Pure light. Blinding white-gold + fire aura.

## 7. INFERNAL DOMINION (Demonic)
### Sources: Book of Enoch, Ars Goetia, Book of Tobit, Isaiah, Ezekiel
**Aura Color:** Red-black hellfire + shadow-purple

### Lucifer (Morningstar)
- Isaiah: "How you have fallen from heaven, morning star, son of the dawn." Ezekiel: "Model of perfection, perfect in beauty... anointed as guardian cherub."
- Most beautiful angel ever. Light-bringer. Pride, rebellion. PERFECT beauty — corrupted. Fallen gold + dark amber aura.

### Lilith (Night Queen)
- Mesopotamian night demon. Adam's first wife who refused to submit. Beautiful, dark, independent.
- Snake and owl motifs. Night sovereignty. Shadow-purple + moonless-black aura.

### Asmodeus (King of Demons)
- Book of Tobit: Demon of lust, destroys marriages. "Prince of Desire." Fire-breathing in later lore.
- Burning desire, temptation, destructive passion. Hellfire-orange + blood-red aura.

### Naamah (Velvet Darkness)
- Jewish mythology: Demon of seduction, wife of Samael. "Velvet ash."
- Infernal charm, enchantment, restorative darkness. Velvet-dark purple + ash-grey aura.

## FACTION AURA SYSTEM

Every deity must have a visible divine AURA radiating from their:
1. BODY — glow around the skin, pulsing through veins
2. WEAPONS — energy along blade edges, glowing weapon surfaces
3. ARMOR — light pulsing through armor joints, cracks, and surface details
4. GROUND — faction-specific ground effects (already defined per deity)

The aura matches the faction color scheme and makes the deity look like a GOD, not a human in costume.
