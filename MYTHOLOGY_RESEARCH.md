# Mythos Gates: Ascension — Mythological Source Research
## "Humans copied their cultures from these divine beings, not the reverse."

## 1. ATEN RA FACTION (Egyptian Mythology)
### Aten Ra → Ra + Aten + Ma'at
- Ra: Falcon-headed, sun disc + Uraeus cobra, was-scepter, solar barque
- Aten: Sun disc with radiating hand-rays ending in ankhs
- Ma'at: Feather of truth, judgment scales, ostrich feather
### Sutekh → Set
- Storm god, chaos, desert, "Set animal" head (squared ears, forked tail)
- Red-haired, god of storms/violence/desert
### Iset → Isis
- Throne goddess (name = "throne"), magic (hekau), healing, protection
- Ankh, tyet knot, scorpion protector, mother of Horus
### Amunet → Amunet
- "The Hidden One," snake-headed or red crown, primordial darkness/wind
- One of the Ogdoad, invisibility, hidden power

## 2. ASGARDIAN FACTION (Norse Mythology)
### Odin → Allfather
- Tall, one-eyed, grey beard, pale blue eye, blue cloak, broad hat
- Gungnir spear, ravens Huginn/Muninn, wolves Geri/Freki
- Hung from Yggdrasil 9 nights, sacrificed eye to Mimis Well
### Freyja → Vanadis
- Most beautiful, golden hair, blue eyes, Brisingamen necklace
- Falcon-feather cloak, chariot pulled by cats, seiðr magic
- Tears of gold, takes half the battle-dead to Sessrumnir
### Frigg → Queen of the Aesir
- White/blue robes, spinning wheel, distaff, loom, Fensalir (Marsh Hall)
- Falcon-cloak, keys at waist, knows all fates but silent
### Thor → Thunder God
- Burly, red hair, red beard, fierce eyes
- Mjolnir hammer, iron gloves Jarngreipr, power belt Megingjord
- Chariot pulled by goats, protector of mankind

## 3. OLYMPIAN FACTION (Greek Mythology)
### Zeus → King of the Gods
- Regal mature man, dark beard, lightning bolt, royal scepter, eagle
- Aegis shield (with gorgoneion), "cloud-gatherer"
### Athena → Grey-Eyed Strategist
- Grey eyes (glaukopis), helmet, spear, aegis with gorgoneion
- Owl, olive tree, serpent, stately woman armed
### Artemis → Huntress of the Wild
- Young maiden, lean/athletic, hunting bow, quiver, stag
- Short tunic, "mistress of animals," virgin huntress, moon
### Ares → God of War
- Mature bearded warrior or nude youth, shield, spear, helmet
- Dog, vulture, boar, bloodthirsty, violent, NEGATIVE aspects of war

## 4. KAMI FACTION (Japanese/Shinto Mythology)
### Amaterasu → Sun Goddess
- Radiant, beautiful woman, sun incarnate, Yata mirror, magatama beads
- Born from Izanagi's left eye, ruler of Takamagahara
### Tsukuyomi → Moon God
- Male, born from Izanagi's right eye, moon bow (Tsukuyumi)
- Quiet, cold, counterpart to Amaterasu
### Susanoo → Storm God
- Turbulent, wild, Totsuka sword, slayer of Yamata no Orochi
- Found Ame-no-Murakumo/Kusanagi sword in serpent's tail
### Izanami → Creation/Death Goddess
- Co-creator of Japan, died giving birth to fire god
- Decayed form in Yomi, maggots/thunder gods, guardian of the dead

## 5. TUATHA FACTION (Celtic/Irish Mythology)
### Dagda → The Good God
- Father-figure, king, druid, large/stout, club that kills/revives
- Uaithne living harp, Undry cauldron, Brugh na Boyne
### Morrigan → Phantom Queen
- War goddess, shapeshifter (crow/raven/eel/wolf), three-fold
- Washes clothes in river before battle, chooses who dies
### Brigid → Bright One
- Fire, poetry, healing, smithcraft, sacred eternal flame
- Three-fold, sacred wells, "fiery arrow," daughter of Dagda
### Lugh → The Many-Skilled
- Samildanach (all-skilled), Llamhfhada (long arm), spear that never misses
- Sling-stone killed Balor, sun/light connection, harvest festival

## 6. EMPYREAN FACTION (Abrahamic/Angelic Mythology)
### Michael → Archangel Warrior
- "Who is like God?", armor, sword, shield, dragon-slayer, scales of judgment
- Leader of heavenly armies (Revelation 12)
### Gabriel → Messenger of God
- Wings, trumpet, white lily, scroll, scepter, annunciation
- Radiant, "standing in the presence of God"
### Sophia → Divine Wisdom
- Gnostic aeon, radiant feminine, intense blue light, Pleroma
- "More beautiful than the sun," highest aeon
### Shekinah → Divine Presence
- Pillar of Fire/Cloud, feminine aspect of God, radiant light
- Indwelling presence, protective, nurturing

## 7. INFERNAL DOMINION FACTION (Demonology)
### Lucifer → Light-Bearer
- Morning Star, most beautiful angel before Fall, covered in precious stones
- Pride led to Fall, "Better to reign in Hell than serve in Heaven"
### Lilith → Night Demon
- First wife of Adam, long hair, wings, serpent association
- Seductress, child-killer, demon queen, consort of Samael
### Asmodeus → Demon of Lust/Wrath
- Killed seven bridegrooms, driven away by fish liver smoke
- Lust, gambling, jealousy, destruction
### Naamah → Mother of Demons
- Seductress from the sea, beautiful, chameleon-like
- Song/music/dance, consort of Samael, mother of divination

## LORE CONCEPT: Humans copied cultures from divine beings
- Egyptian pharaohs copied Aten Ra's Gate-born crown and collar
- Norse skalds remembered Odin's blue cloak and ravens from dreams
- Greek temples copied the Olympian Marble Sky Arena
- Japanese shrines mimicked Amaterasu's Takamagahara
- Celtic burial mounds echoed Dagda's Brugh na Boyne
- Abrahamic churches copied the Empyrean Hierarchies
- Infernal contracts echoed the Infernal Dominion's legal code
